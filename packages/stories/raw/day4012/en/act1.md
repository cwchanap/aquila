# Act 1: Routine Maintenance

**Narrator**: Cold.

**Narrator**: Not winter cold. The kind that seeps outward from the bone, as if someone had sunk her in well water and left her there so long they forgot to fish her out.

**Narrator**: The pod lid slid open with barely a sound. A crust of frost broke from the rim of the glass and settled on her collarbone.

**Yi-ning**: (thinking) ...Still alive.

**Vigil**: Good morning, Engineer Cheng Yi-ning.

**Narrator**: The voice came down from somewhere overhead, steady and mild, like a cup of water left to cool until it was just right to drink.

**Vigil**: Today is day four thousand and twelve. You've been woken for routine maintenance, estimated at three days. Take your time. Don't try to stand up yet.

**Yi-ning**: (thinking) Routine.

**Narrator**: She tried to stand up anyway. Her knees declined. She tipped sideways and slapped a palm against the pod wall, and the cold of it knocked the breath out of her.

**Yi-ning**: (thinking) Great. I still remember how to fall over.

**Narrator**: The pods ran out row after row and disappeared into the blue dark. Two thousand and forty people lay inside them, a small green light glowing on every lid, like fireflies along the edge of a rice paddy at night. Quiet. Bothering no one.

**Yi-ning**: How much longer?

**Vigil**: Sixty-one years to arrival.

**Yi-ning**: ...So can I go back to sleep for a bit?

**Vigil**: In three days, yes.

**Narrator**: She laughed a little, her throat dry as sandpaper. A dispenser in the wall pushed out a small cup of something hot. She took it and caught a trace of ginger.

**Yi-ning**: (thinking) Fake ginger. But hot.

**Narrator**: The heat burned all the way down into her stomach. She thought of typhoon nights at her grandmother's house in Chiayi. The power out, the two of them sitting in the living room, a flashlight pointed at the ceiling. Grandma making ginger tea and saying, drink this and go to bed, the wind will leave on its own.

**Narrator**: It was the last thing she'd thought of before boarding. Apparently it was also the first thing she'd think of on waking.

**Yi-ning**: Vigil, send me the maintenance list.

**Vigil**: It's already on your wristband. No rush. Finish your drink.

**Narrator**: The wristband lit up. The list was short: replace filter three, recalibrate the corridor sensors, review temperature logs for cryo section two.

**Narrator**: She read it twice.

**Yi-ning**: (thinking) The repair arms could knock this out in an afternoon.

**Narrator**: Something else floated up from the frozen back of her mind, slowly, like an old note going soft in water.

**Narrator**: Page one of the manual: Routine maintenance is performed by two engineers.

**Narrator**: She turned to look at the long rows of pods behind her.

**Narrator**: Hers was the only one open.
