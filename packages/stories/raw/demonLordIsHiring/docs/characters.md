# 《魔王徵才中》角色設定文件

> 短篇單幕故事。視角鎖定魔王（第三人稱貼近）。喜劇基調為冷面辦公室荒謬：所有人都非常認真，沒有人在搞笑。

---

## 1. 旁白（Narrator）

- **ID**: `narrator`
- **Aliases**: Narrator, ナレーション

貼著魔王的感知敘事。可以有一點華麗的比喻，但每一段都要用一句平板的短句收掉。不替其他角色說出內心。

---

## 2. 魔王（Demon Lord）

- **ID**: `demon_lord`
- **Aliases**: Demon Lord

在位三百年。把「反派」當成一份正經職業在經營：每年重寫開場白、每季保養陷阱、勇者來訪前一晚把披風送洗。勇者每月第一個週六來闖城，那個紅圈撐起了他整個月。

勇者寄來一封很有禮貌的辭職信，回鄉種蕪菁。魔王因此第一次面對一張空白的月曆。

### 說話風格

- 句子短，語氣疲倦但嚴肅，從不開玩笑——他講的每一句荒謬話都是真心的
- 內心獨白比開口多，常以單詞或半句收尾（`蕪菁。` / `九歲半。`）
- 對瑟琳說話時偶爾露出依賴，但絕不承認

### Portrait Prompts

- **base**: anime visual-novel portrait, tall ageless demon lord with pale grey skin, long straight black hair, two curved obsidian horns, tired crimson eyes with faint shadows beneath, high-collared black cape with a small mended patch on the left shoulder, composed formal posture, dim warm candlelight from the side, muted black and deep red palette, upper-body shot, clean background
- **weary**: anime visual-novel portrait, demon lord with long black hair and curved obsidian horns, slumped slightly against a stone throne back, eyes half-lidded and unfocused, one hand pressed to his temple, black high-collared cape, low amber dusk light, desaturated deep red palette, upper-body shot
- **touched**: anime visual-novel portrait, demon lord with long black hair and curved obsidian horns, gaze lowered toward something in his hands, the faintest softening at the corners of his mouth, crimson eyes glassy but composed, black cape, warm soft afternoon light, upper-body shot

## 3. 瑟琳（Serin）

- **ID**: `serin`
- **Aliases**: Serin, 祕書, Secretary, セリン

魔王的祕書，任職一百一十年，從來沒有遲到、從來沒有慌過——包括東翼失火、地牢史萊姆罷工、以及勇者第一次闖城時魔王忘詞。永遠比魔王早一步：信還沒念完，徵才公告已經貼好了。

她就是「神祕應徵者」。她從不承認，魔王也從不點破。

### 說話風格

- 平穩、簡短、公文式。回答通常只有「是的」「我知道」「交給我」
- 用最正經的語氣說最荒謬的事（「可被擊敗」寫清楚比較有誠意）
- 關心藏在行程表裡

### Portrait Prompts

- **base**: anime visual-novel portrait, composed woman who looks about thirty, sleek black hair in a tight low bun, small dark horns barely visible above her ears, calm violet eyes, neat dark grey high-necked secretary uniform with silver buttons, holding a leather folder against her chest, unreadable neutral expression, cool soft window light, muted charcoal and violet palette, upper-body shot, clean background
- **smile**: anime visual-novel portrait, composed secretary with sleek black hair in a low bun and small dark horns, the barest closed-mouth smile, violet eyes calm and knowing, dark grey high-necked uniform, leather folder tucked under one arm, soft warm light, upper-body shot

## 4. 奧德里克（Aldric）

- **ID**: `aldric`
- **Aliases**: Aldric, 聖騎士, Paladin, オルドリック

王國騎士團的聖騎士，屠龍十一條（兩條是誤會，後來和解），收復北方四座城。在騎士團已經升到頂，每天都在開會。他來應徵是為了「比較有挑戰性的事」。極度有禮、極度能幹，問遣散費和勞保時跟問戰術一樣認真。

### Portrait Prompts

- **base**: anime visual-novel portrait, broad-shouldered man in his late thirties, short neatly combed sandy hair, square jaw, steady pale blue eyes, polished silver-white full plate armor with a small gold sun emblem, a tiny notebook in one gauntleted hand, perfectly upright posture, bright clean daylight, cool silver and white palette, upper-body shot, clean background

## 5. 托比（Toby）

- **ID**: `toby`
- **Aliases**: Toby, トビー

山下村子的小學生，九歲半。戴著一頂太大的頭盔，揹著一把用蠟筆寫著「聖劍」的木劍。帶著媽媽簽的家長同意書和學校的聯絡簿來面試。老師出了作文題「我的志願」，他想寫一個沒人寫過的。他說話直接，因為他還不知道有些話不用說出來。

### Portrait Prompts

- **base**: anime visual-novel portrait, nine-year-old boy, round face, messy chestnut hair poking out from under an oversized dented steel helmet tilted over one eyebrow, wide earnest brown eyes, plain linen tunic, wooden toy sword strapped to his back with crayon writing on the blade, small canvas backpack, serious determined expression, warm afternoon light, soft earthy palette, upper-body shot, clean background

---

## 6. 神祕應徵者（Mysterious Applicant）

- **ID**: `mysterious_applicant`
- **Aliases**: Mysterious Applicant, 謎の応募者

自稱「琳瑟」（英文版：Siren——Serin 的字母重組）。蓬鬆的金色捲髮、圓框眼鏡、一件魔王沒見過的洋裝。金髮邊緣露出一小截黑髮，履歷的字跡和瑟琳一模一樣。對魔王城的內部資訊瞭若指掌。

獨立成一個角色 ID 以保持說話者不混淆；「她就是瑟琳」這件事只在文本中由魔王察覺，不由旁白宣布。

### Portrait Prompts

- **base**: anime visual-novel portrait, woman who looks about thirty wearing an obviously voluminous curly blonde wig with a strand of sleek black hair escaping at the temple, small dark horns poking through the curls, large round wire-rimmed glasses, calm violet eyes, old-fashioned lavender day dress with a lace collar, hands folded neatly, perfectly composed polite expression, soft window light, muted lavender and gold palette, upper-body shot, clean background
