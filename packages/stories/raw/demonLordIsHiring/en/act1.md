# Act 1: The Resignation Letter

**Narrator**: The letter arrived on a Tuesday morning.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord's castle has no postman. In three hundred years, only three kinds of things had ever been delivered to it: declarations of war, curses, and the hero in person. So when his secretary set a pale cream envelope on the arm of the throne, the Demon Lord looked at it for a long time.

**Narrator**: The handwriting on the front was very neat. It had been sealed not with blood, but with a grain of rice.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) I have a bad feeling about this.

**Serin**: It's from the hero. Shall I read it?

**Demon Lord**: Read it.

**Serin**: "To His Majesty the Demon Lord. As of the end of this month, I hereby resign from my position as hero."

**Serin**: "Thank you for twelve years of hospitality. Every time I stormed the castle, I could feel the care you put into it. The traps were always well maintained, and you never repeated a line."

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) I rewrite the lines every year.

**Serin**: "But I'm thirty-four now, and my knees aren't what they were. The last time I rolled through the spinning blades on the seventh floor, I was in bed for a month. I think there may be other things to do with a life."

**Serin**: "I've bought a plot of land at the foot of the eastern hills. I'm going to grow turnips. The soil is good and the neighbors are kind. Wishing you continued success in all your dark endeavors. Respectfully, the Hero."

**Serin**: There's one more line on the back. "P.S. I'll send a crate of turnips once they're harvested."

**Narrator**: The throne room went quiet. The sunset slanted in through the high windows and stretched the shadows of the pillars across the floor, one after another, like a row of benches no one wanted to sit on.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Turnips.

**Narrator**: He took the letter and read it again. The handwriting didn't shake. Nothing was crossed out. You could tell there had been a draft.

**Narrator**: It was a very polite letter. That was the worst part.

**Narrator**: A cursed hero comes back. A defeated hero comes back. A hero with a grudge always comes back.

**Narrator**: A polite hero does not.

**Demon Lord**: Serin, what's the date today?

**Serin**: The fourteenth.

**Demon Lord**: After the end of the month, no one will come to fight me.

**Serin**: That does seem to be what the letter means.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord leaned back into the throne. It was very hard. He had chosen it himself three hundred years ago, on the grounds that it made him look imposing. It had never occurred to him that one day he would sit in it with nothing to do.

**Narrator**: On his calendar, the first Saturday of every month was circled in red. The night before, he would inspect the traps, run his opening speech once to the empty hall, and send his cape out to be cleaned. That red circle held up the whole month.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) From now on, just a blank page.

**Demon Lord**: A demon lord without a hero is just a man who lives in a very large house.

**Serin**: It is a very large house.

**Demon Lord**: That isn't the point.

**Serin**: I know.

**Narrator**: Serin had worked for him for a hundred and ten years and had never once panicked. Not when the east wing caught fire, not when the dungeon slimes went on strike, and not the first time the hero broke in and the Demon Lord forgot his lines.

**Narrator**: She opened the folder in her hands and drew out a sheet of paper.

**Serin**: Then we fill it.

**Demon Lord**: Fill what?

**Serin**: The hero. When a position opens up, you hire.

**Demon Lord**: A nemesis is not a position.

**Serin**: It has been since nine o'clock this morning.

**Narrator**: She turned the paper around. It was a notice, already stamped with the castle seal.

**Serin**: "The Demon Lord's Castle seeks one nemesis. Requirements: a sense of justice, ability to visit regularly, high tolerance for pressure. No experience necessary. Salary negotiable. May be defeated."

**Demon Lord**: "May be defeated" refers to me.

**Serin**: Yes. I felt it was more honest to be clear.

**Demon Lord**: When did you post this?

**Serin**: While you were reading the letter for the third time. The board in the village down the hill, and the notice wall at the market gate.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) She wrote it before she read the letter.

**Narrator**: He didn't ask. After a hundred and ten years, some things are better left unasked.

**Demon Lord**: Will anyone come?

**Serin**: Three have already applied. The interviews are scheduled for tomorrow morning.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord looked at the notice. The paper was new, the ink was black, and the words "may be defeated" had been written with particular care.

**Demon Lord**: ...How should we arrange the throne room?

**Serin**: Leave it to me.
