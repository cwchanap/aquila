# Act 2: Three Interviews

**Narrator**: The next morning, the throne room looked different.

**Narrator**: The throne had been pushed against the back wall and covered with a dust sheet. In the middle of the hall stood a long table: two glasses of water, a stack of blank scoring sheets, a pen, and a folded paper placard that read INTERVIEWER.

**Narrator**: Across from it, past a wide stretch of flagstone, sat a single chair.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) I'm more nervous than on raid days.

**Serin**: First candidate. Sir Aldric, paladin.

**Narrator**: The man who entered wore a full suit of silver-white armor, polished so bright the Demon Lord had to squint. He stopped in front of the chair, bowed at a precise angle, and only then sat down.

**Aldric**: Good morning. Here is my résumé, along with my letters of recommendation.

**Narrator**: There were seven letters. Three bore the king's seal.

**Demon Lord**: You've slain dragons.

**Aldric**: Eleven. Two were misunderstandings. We've since reconciled.

**Demon Lord**: Why do you want this position?

**Aldric**: Honestly, I've gone as far as I can in the knighthood. It's meetings every day. I'd like to do something more challenging.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) When he said "challenging," he looked at me the way a man looks at a mountain he hasn't climbed yet.

**Aldric**: Just to confirm: the role involves defeating the Demon Lord on a regular basis, correct?

**Serin**: That's correct.

**Aldric**: And if I succeed on the first attempt? Does that count as passing probation, or is there severance?

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) He's serious. He is actually asking about severance.

**Serin**: We'll look into that.

**Aldric**: Thank you. Also, is there labor insurance?

**Serin**: There is.

**Demon Lord**: There is?

**Serin**: I set it up yesterday.

**Narrator**: Aldric nodded and made a note in a small booklet. He held the pen the way he held a sword. His wrist did not move.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) He can win. Not might. Can.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) And what I need is not someone who can win.

**Demon Lord**: We'll be in touch.

**Aldric**: Thank you. I look forward to crossing swords with you.

**Serin**: Second candidate.

**Narrator**: The door opened a crack, and then a little wider.

**Narrator**: A child came in. He wore a helmet that was plainly too big for him; it slid down a little with every step, and with every step he pushed it back up. On his back was a wooden sword with HOLY SWORD written down the blade in crayon.

**Toby**: I'm Toby the Hero.

**Demon Lord**: ...Please, have a seat.

**Narrator**: Toby climbed onto the chair. His feet didn't reach the floor. He took a sheet of paper from his backpack and held it out with both hands.

**Toby**: This is my permission slip. My mom signed it.

**Narrator**: "I consent to my son Toby attending the nemesis interview at the Demon Lord's Castle. Please send him home by five-thirty for dinner." Under the signature, in smaller writing: "He's very serious about this. Please don't scare him."

**Demon Lord**: How old are you?

**Toby**: Nine and a half.

**Demon Lord**: Why do you want to be my nemesis?

**Toby**: Our teacher told us to write an essay called "What I Want to Be." Everyone else wrote baker or knight. I wanted to write something nobody wrote before. Also, you look lonely.

**Narrator**: The hand holding the permission slip stopped.

**Demon Lord**: ...Who says?

**Toby**: The notice said "able to visit regularly." People who aren't lonely don't write it that carefully.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Nine and a half.

**Serin**: Toby, do you have any special skills?

**Toby**: I'm really good at hide-and-seek. And I can come every day. I live right down the hill.

**Narrator**: He said "every day" with total certainty, as if it were something that did not need proving.

**Toby**: Can you sign my contact book? Our teacher says a parent or a boss has to sign it.

**Narrator**: On the open page, one box had already been filled in, very neatly: "Today's activity: interviewed the Demon Lord."

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord signed. His signature had three hundred years of history behind it, a thicket of flourishes, and it squeezed into the little box only with difficulty.

**Toby**: Thanks. I have to go now. I have math homework.

**Narrator**: He pushed his helmet up and left.

**Serin**: Third candidate... I'll just get some tea leaves from the storeroom first. We're nearly out.

**Demon Lord**: Now?

**Serin**: I'll be right back.

**Narrator**: She went out the side door. About a minute later, the main door opened.

**Narrator**: A woman came in. A great cloud of curly blonde hair, a pair of round glasses, a lavender dress the Demon Lord had never seen before. She sat down with her back straight and her hands folded in her lap.

**Mysterious Applicant**: Good morning. My name is Siren.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord looked down at her résumé. The handwriting was neat, every stroke ending in a slight upward hook to the right. He had been reading this handwriting for a hundred and ten years, on every schedule, every expense form, and every labor insurance document filed yesterday.

**Narrator**: At the edge of the blonde curls, a little strand of black hair showed through.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Serin.

**Demon Lord**: ...Tell me about your experience.

**Mysterious Applicant**: I'm very familiar with the castle. I know the blades on the seventh floor have a two-second delay before they start. I know there's a mended hole in the left shoulder of your cape. And I know that every time you finish your opening speech, you sneak a sip of water.

**Demon Lord**: That's internal information.

**Mysterious Applicant**: I did a lot of research.

**Demon Lord**: Why do you want to be my nemesis?

**Narrator**: She paused. Behind the glasses, her eyes rested on him, perfectly calm, exactly the way they did every morning when she handed him his schedule.

**Mysterious Applicant**: Because heroes get old. Their knees go. They leave to grow turnips. And you need someone who won't leave.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord said nothing. A crow passed the high window, and its shadow flickered across the long table.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) A hundred and ten years. She has never once been late.

**Demon Lord**: ...We'll be in touch.

**Narrator**: She stood, bowed, and left by the main door.

**Narrator**: A minute later, the side door opened. Serin came in carrying a tin of tea leaves, slightly out of breath, a single blonde hair caught on her collar.

**Serin**: Did the interview go well?

**Demon Lord**: ...It went well.

**Serin**: Then, Your Majesty, which candidate will you hire?

**Narrator**: Three résumés lay on the table. One stamped with the king's seal, one with a permission slip attached, and one in handwriting he could have recognized with his eyes closed.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) I have to pick one. The calendar is still empty.
