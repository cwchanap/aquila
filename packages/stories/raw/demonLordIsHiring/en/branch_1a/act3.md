# Act 3: The Revised Plan

**Narrator**: Aldric arrived punctually on the first Saturday.

**Narrator**: He did not break down the door. He knocked. Once inside, he handed over a document titled "Operational Plan for the Defeat of the Demon Lord," in duplicate, and asked the Demon Lord to sign for receipt.

**Narrator**: Then he cleared all seven floors of traps in four minutes.

**Narrator**: The spinning blades on the seventh floor had taken the hero twelve years to learn to roll through. Aldric walked through them.

**Narrator**: He stopped before the throne, sword point resting on the floor, breathing evenly, not a scratch on his armor.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord had only gotten one arm into his cape.

**Aldric**: Your Majesty, per section three of the plan, I am now in a position to close the case.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Here it comes.

**Aldric**: However, I've read the castle's contract. Clause twelve states that the nemesis's duty is to "visit regularly." If I close the case today, there will be no one to visit next month. That would be a breach of contract.

**Demon Lord**: ...And so?

**Aldric**: And so I propose we stop here each time. I go home and write my report. You go and improve your traps. I come back next month.

**Narrator**: He paused, his gaze drifting toward the seventh floor.

**Aldric**: Honestly, in fifteen years with the knighthood, I have never found a place I wanted to come back to.

**Serin**: We agree to those terms.

**Demon Lord**: I haven't said anything.

**Serin**: Your face did.

**Narrator**: That night, the Demon Lord tore down the blades on the seventh floor and rebuilt them. He added two more sets, changed the delay to three seconds, thought about it, then took the delay out entirely.

**Narrator**: He worked straight through until dawn. His wrists ached, his eyes were dry, and there was machine oil under his nails.

**Narrator**: For the first time in three hundred years, he felt he didn't have enough traps.

**Narrator**: In the morning, Serin set a hot cup of tea beside the workbench and turned the calendar to the next page. The first Saturday of next month was already circled in red.

**Narrator**: Beside the circle, in small writing: "Aldric. Operational plan, second edition."

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) ...Second edition.

**Narrator**: He sent his cape out to be cleaned.
