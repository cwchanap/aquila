# Act 3: What I Want to Be

**Narrator**: Toby came every afternoon at four.

**Narrator**: He didn't use the main door. He used the kitchen door, because it was closer to the mountain path. He would say hello to the gargoyle by the entrance, straighten his helmet, and then shout, "Demon Lord, prepare to die!"

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord would rise from the throne, spread his cape, and deliver the opening speech he rewrote every year. Every word of it.

**Narrator**: Then they would fight for twenty minutes. The wooden sword thumped against the cape. It didn't hurt. When the Demon Lord fell, he fell very slowly, so Toby had time to finish shouting the name of his special move.

**Narrator**: At five-twenty, Serin would cough once in the doorway.

**Toby**: That's it for today. I'll come fight you again tomorrow.

**Demon Lord**: I'll be waiting.

**Narrator**: He meant it when he said it. He meant it the way he meant the opening speech.

**Narrator**: Two months later, Toby brought a sheet of essay paper. The title was "What I Want to Be," and in the top right corner, in red pen, was a large A.

**Toby**: My teacher says I have to show it to my boss.

**Narrator**: The handwriting wobbled. A few words were spelled out by sound.

**Narrator**: "What I want to be is the one who beats the Demon Lord. The Demon Lord lives on the mountain and his house is very big. He is very strong, but he lets me win every time and then pretends he didn't. I think he is a very good Demon Lord."

**Narrator**: "When I grow up I'm going to beat him for real. So he has to live a long time."

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) ...

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord handed the essay back and signed the contact book. His signature still didn't fit in the box.

**Demon Lord**: You misspelled one word. "Demon" doesn't have an A in it.

**Toby**: Oh.

**Serin**: Your Majesty.

**Demon Lord**: I know.

**Narrator**: That night, the essay was framed and hung behind the throne, where a three-hundred-year-old battle painting used to be.

**Narrator**: At the end of the month, a wooden crate arrived from the foot of the eastern hills. Inside was a full load of turnips, the soil not quite brushed off.

**Narrator**: The next afternoon at four, the Demon Lord picked out the biggest one and had Toby take it home to his mother.

**Toby**: Is this loot?

**Demon Lord**: It is.

**Narrator**: Toby hugged the turnip to his chest, and his helmet slid down a little further. He had no free hand to push it back, so he walked down the mountain like that, helmet crooked.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Tomorrow at four.
