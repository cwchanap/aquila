# Act 3: Letter of Offer

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord wrote the offer letter himself.

**Narrator**: He went through three drafts. The first was too formal, the second too long, and halfway through the third he noticed the corners of his mouth had turned up. The one he finally sent was a single line: "Congratulations, Ms. Siren, on your appointment as this castle's nemesis."

**Narrator**: He thought about the address for a long time. In the end he wrote: West Wing, second floor, third door.

**Narrator**: Serin's room.

**Narrator**: The next morning, Serin arrived on time with her folder, as she had every day for a hundred and ten years.

**Serin**: Your Majesty, today's schedule. Nine o'clock, routine correspondence. Ten o'clock, dungeon inspection. Three in the afternoon, a visit from your nemesis.

**Demon Lord**: Mm.

**Serin**: Also, she asked me to pass on that she'd like weekends off, with battles fixed for Tuesday afternoons, and that she needs to be able to leave on time afterward.

**Demon Lord**: ...That's acceptable.

**Serin**: She also said: please don't go easy on her.

**Narrator**: At three o'clock, the main door opened. Curly blonde hair, round glasses, and in her hand, a rapier.

**Mysterious Applicant**: Demon Lord, prepare to die.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord rose and spread his cape. The opening speech went smoothly. He didn't even need the sip of water.

**Narrator**: She made only one move. She circled to his left, the side with the mended hole in the cape, and in the instant he raised his arm, she touched the tip of her blade lightly to his shoulder.

**Narrator**: In three hundred years, no one had ever noticed that he couldn't raise his left arm all the way.

**Mysterious Applicant**: You lose.

**Demon Lord**: ...You cheated.

**Mysterious Applicant**: I did a lot of research.

**Narrator**: She sheathed the rapier and straightened her wig, which had started to slip.

**Mysterious Applicant**: Same time next Tuesday. And please remember to eat lunch. You skipped it again today.

**Narrator**: She left by the main door.

**Narrator**: Five minutes later, the side door opened. Serin came in with a tray, a cup of tea and a lunch box on it, and set it on the arm of the throne.

**Serin**: The hero's turnips arrived today. I stewed them with some pork.

**Narrator**: The Demon Lord opened the lunch box. The steam rose to meet him, a little too hot.

**Demon Lord**: Serin. ...Thank you.

**Serin**: Whatever for? I'm only the secretary.

**Narrator**: On her collar, there was another blonde hair.

**Demon Lord**: (thinking) Next Tuesday.

**Narrator**: He set down his chopsticks, picked up a pen, and drew a red circle around Tuesday on the calendar.
