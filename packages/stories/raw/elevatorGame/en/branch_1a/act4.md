# Act 4: Ground Floor

**Narrator**: She pressed one.

**Narrator**: The button lit. A faint orange ring, like a bulb about to die, but it lit.

**Narrator**: The doors slid shut, the left one still half a beat behind. The red light of the corridor was squeezed into a single line, then gone.

**Narrator**: The tube flickered twice and went back to its usual sickly white.

**Narrator**: Nine. Eight. Seven.

**Narrator**: She didn't look back. She stared at the seam of the doors, at the row of red digits, and listened to the water behind her, one drop at a time.

**Narrator**: Five.

**Narrator**: The car stopped. The doors opened.

**Narrator**: Outside was the fifth floor, an ordinary fifth floor, motion-sensor light on, someone's umbrella left to dry by their door. A cold passed behind her, brushing her arm. At the edge of her vision, the black flats walked out, one step, two, without a sound.

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) Don't look. Don't look where she goes.

**Narrator**: The doors closed. The dripping stopped.

**Narrator**: Four. Three. Two.

**Narrator**: Finally, she dared to look in the mirror.

**Narrator**: Only her. Messy hair, red eyes, left lace undone. The floor in the corner was dry.

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) Dry.

**Narrator**: Ding.

**Narrator**: The lobby was just as she'd left it. The tube humming, the radio still playing old songs, the guard's jacket still hanging off the chair.

**Narrator**: She stepped out on legs that felt borrowed. She steadied herself against the mailbox wall, cold metal against her palm—an ordinary cold, a human-world cold.

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) I'm back. Final papers. I love final papers.

**Narrator**: The digital clock on the security desk glowed green. 1:58 a.m.

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) Broken. Everything in this building is broken.

**Narrator**: Her phone buzzed. A message from Fang Rui.

**Narrator**: "I'm in the lobby. Where are you?"

**Narrator**: Xu Nian looked up.

**Narrator**: There was no one in the lobby. No one behind the desk, no one by the mailboxes, no one in the arcade beyond the glass doors. Only her, and her shadow, stretched long by the fluorescent light.

**Narrator**: She called. It connected on the first ring.

**Xu Nian**: Rui, I'm in the lobby.

**Fang Rui**: I'm in the lobby too.

**Narrator**: Fang Rui's voice was close, very clear, still thick from crying. Behind it, the hum of a fluorescent tube, and a radio playing the same old song.

**Fang Rui**: Didn't you just walk out of the elevator? I saw you. You're standing right there by the mailboxes, with your back to me.

**Narrator**: Xu Nian was standing by the mailboxes.

**Narrator**: Her hand was still pressed to the metal.

**Fang Rui**: Nian? Turn around. Why won't you turn around?

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) If you step out, don't look back.

**Xu Nian**: (thinking) But I didn't step out. I pressed one.

**Narrator**: Behind her, the elevator doors were still open.

**Narrator**: A very small sound.

**Narrator**: Drip.
