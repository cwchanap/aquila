# 天亮前的一壺茶 / One Pot Before Dawn — plan

Genre: wuxia (classical jianghu). POV: 小滿 (Xiao Man), the 17-year-old tea apprentice. Length: one sitting.

## Premise

On the rainy night before a dawn duel at 一線關 (Threadline Pass), two swordsmen raised by the same old tea master come back separately to his mountain teahouse. The elder, 沈硯, believes that ten years ago his younger brother 陸遲 went back and killed a man Shen Yan had beaten and spared, then fled with Shen Yan's sword and carved 「人是我殺的」 by the door. The younger tells the apprentice what only he knows: the man died of Shen Yan's own thrust, and Chi took the sword — and the White Egret Sect's vengeance — so his brother could stay clean; at dawn he means to be slow. The apprentice who pours the tea is the only one who hears both sides, and the motif that frames her choice is the tea master's rule: the first infusion is poured away, "someone has to taste the bitterness first, for the ones who come after."

## Endings

- **b1a — Spoken**: no duel; the brothers swap their swords back without a word she can hear; Lu Chi leaves before sunrise, still carrying the blame; something unsaid remains between them and out of Xiao Man's reach.
- **b1b — Threadline Pass**: the duel happens; Lu Chi lets himself lose and dies; Shen Yan never learns why; Xiao Man becomes the one who keeps the bitterness.
