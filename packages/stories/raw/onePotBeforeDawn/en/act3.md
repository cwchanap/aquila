# Act 3: Before Dawn

**Narrator**: The fourth watch. The rain had stopped.

**Narrator**: Beyond the blinds the sky was fading from ink to a blue-grey that looked washed too many times.

**Narrator**: The stairs creaked. Shen Yan came down, the oilcloth off. A black sheath, chipped at the tip.

**Narrator**: He saw the man by the window and his step faltered by half.

**Narrator**: Only by half.

**Lu Chi**: Brother.

**Shen Yan**: Mm.

**Xiao Man**: (thinking) Ten years. One word for one word.

**Lu Chi**: I'll go check the sky. See how thick the fog is at the pass.

**Narrator**: He went out and left the door ajar. Cold air squeezed through, and the stove fire flinched.

**Narrator**: Shen Yan sat at the same table and laid the sword across his knees.

**Shen Yan**: Make the tea. The last pot before dawn.

**Narrator**: Xiao Man brought the water to the sound of wind in the pines, warmed the pot, added the leaves. Her hands weren't steady. A few leaves scattered across the tray.

**Shen Yan**: Why are your hands shaking?

**Xiao Man**: ...It's cold.

**Narrator**: Water in. Lid on. Count to five.

**Narrator**: The first infusion sat in the pot, amber with a touch of red.

**Narrator**: She could pour it away, as always.

**Narrator**: Or she could speak first.

**Xiao Man**: (thinking) He said I don't count.

**Xiao Man**: (thinking) So does what I say count?
