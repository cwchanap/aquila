# Act 3: Forty Minutes

**Narrator**: Forty minutes is a long time.

**Narrator**: Long enough for her to read the note six more times, for the dying tube to flicker maybe three hundred times, and for the back across the street not to turn around once.

**Yining**: (thinking) He's giving me time. Or he's just too scared to come back. Both sound like him.

**Narrator**: She fished a pen out of her scrubs pocket. A nurse's pocket always has a pen, the way some people always have a lighter. It was printed with the name of some pharmaceutical company, long since rubbed illegible.

**Narrator**: She had the cap between her teeth before she realized that was his habit. She took it out immediately.

**Yining**: (thinking) Six months. I know his cardigan has a hole in it. I know his clothes can stand to be a little damper. I know he swears at sentences under his breath when he edits.

**Yining**: (thinking) I don't know his name.

**Yining**: (thinking) He doesn't know mine.

**Narrator**: No. 4 dinged.

**Narrator**: Her clothes were done. Hot and dry, like something just lifted out of someone's arms.

**Narrator**: On the folding table: one grey sock, one note, one pen.

**Narrator**: Across the street, in the white light, the back was still standing there.

**Yining**: (thinking) Write something. Or don't.
