# Art bible, Shinjuku platform, train carriage, Jishui station, 幽冥之都, 迷霧之森, the cabin in the fog, the second fork and the left-hand trail (acts 1-14), and branch 1b's forest footpath, the cabin and 亡者峽谷 (acts 9-11), the cabin by night and the glade in the forest (acts 12-14), and the Moonlight Glade village and its guest house (branch 1a/2a/3a acts 15-17)

The train carries exactly 16 passengers, the cast on the character sheet. No extras anywhere inside the train. A scene shows only the people its beat needs; nobody has to appear in every background. Ling Mo is gone from act 4 on in both branches; who else stays aboard depends on the branch (see Acts 4-6 below).

## Shinjuku platform (act 1)

Act 1 opens on the platform before the train arrives. Every platform shot uses this set, matching act1_s0:

A late-night Chuo Line platform at Shinjuku, 23:50: an elevated platform under a steel canopy roof with exposed lattice girders and long rows of white fluorescent tubes; a row of square brushed-steel pillars; a glossy light-grey tiled floor that reflects the lights; a yellow tactile strip along the platform edge; the tracks run into a black tunnel mouth at the far end, with small red and white signal lights in the dark; a black departure board hangs from the canopy, its lines shown only as glowing amber bars with no readable characters; silver metal benches against a low back wall topped by a steel railing; beyond it the Tokyo night city, office towers with lit windows. Cold white light, no advertisements, no text.

- **Reference** (`platform_master.png`, empty): eye level, looking down the platform toward the tunnel. Benches and railing on the left, the yellow strip and the tracks on the right, the departure board at the upper middle, the tunnel mouth at the far right end.
- **Where people stand**, seen from the reference angle: Li Jie behind the yellow line in the foreground; the four students in a loose circle mid-platform near the yellow line (Kenta, Asuka, Misaki, Takumi), with Saito standing alone a few metres further down the platform toward the tunnel, clear of their circle until he cuts in (act1_platform_saito); Emi alone at the end of a bench against the back wall on the left; Ling Mo leaning in the shadow of the pillar just beyond Emi's bench.
- **Commuters**: unlike the train, the platform has a scattered late-night crowd. Keep it to a few people, small, far off and out of focus, with no visible faces, never in front of the cast.
- **No train body.** As with the carriage exterior, the arriving train is shown only as headlights in the tunnel mouth.

## Carriage (identical in every in-train scene)

A single Chuo Line commuter carriage: continuous side-facing bench seats along both walls in dark navy patterned fabric, split into seat groups by silver stanchion poles: each seven-seat section has exactly two, rising from the front lip of the seats between the 2nd and 3rd seats and between the 5th and 6th seats counted from either partition (2-3-2, as in act3_s0), plus the grab bar on each partition; no pole stands out in the aisle, behind the seats against the windows or in front of the glass, and this count wins over the empty reference images, which show extra poles; silver overhead grab rails with white hanging hand straps; flat white LED ceiling lights; large rectangular windows above the benches; the benches come in seven-seat sections (`carriage_side.png`), each ending in a curved grey partition panel, with a sliding door with yellow edging between sections and at each end, so no seat ever stands in front of a door and every doorway is clear floor; both end walls have the same plain grey connecting door with a small window; no advertisements, no text. Lighting and what shows through the windows change per scene, the carriage does not.

## Seating plan

It is a near-empty last train, so the passengers sit group by group with empty seats between the groups. Inside a group people sit side by side. Directions below are seen from the door end looking down the carriage (angle A).

- **Left bench, near to far:** Emi in the end seat against the partition, Li Jie beside her; a few empty seats; Yamamoto alone; a few empty seats; Ito Makoto and Ito Nana; several empty seats; Ling Mo alone in the far corner, always.

Emi and Li Jie sit side by side but are strangers in acts 1-3: he only glances at her, never leans in, and she never looks back at him.

## Camera angles and framing

Vary both the camera position and the distance from scene to scene; never run the same framing twice in a row. The empty reference images under `packages/assets/media/train_adventure/backgrounds/_reference/` fix the platform and carriage design: pass the closest one first, then the portraits of the people in the shot (the four students can go in as one strip you assemble from their portraits, left to right Kenta, Asuka, Misaki, Takumi, so a crowded shot stays within about five images; no group sheet is kept in the repo). When the shot shows legs or feet, pass each visible person's full-body image from the same folder in place of their portrait (see Outfits above): one image per character, each 1536x1024 with the front view on the left and the back view on the right, the same face and upper body as the portrait plus the bottoms and shoes, so a crowded shot should keep to the people the beat needs. A full-body image counts against the five-image limit exactly as a portrait does (the empty set first, then at most about four people): in a wider shot, send the images for the people nearest the camera and give everyone else's bottoms and shoes in words from the Outfits table. When a character is seen from behind, say so in the prompt and the back view is the one to copy (Li Jie's backpack, the Misaki, Emi and Yamamoto bag straps, the Asuka and Tomoko hair, Ling Mo's hood). The files are `outfit_tanaka_kenta.png`, `outfit_suzuki_asuka.png`, `outfit_takahashi_misaki.png`, `outfit_sato_takumi.png`, `outfit_saito_ren.png`, `outfit_nakamura_hayato.png`, `outfit_ito_makoto.png`, `outfit_ito_nana.png`, `outfit_yamamoto_koji.png`, `outfit_ling_mo.png`, `outfit_kobayashi_yudai.png`, `outfit_kobayashi_tomoko.png`, `outfit_kobayashi_hina.png`, `outfit_kobayashi_shota.png`, `outfit_li_jie.png` and `outfit_shimizu_emi.png`.

- **A, down the length** (`carriage_master.png`): camera at the door end looking toward the far end.
- **B, reverse** (`carriage_reverse.png`): camera at the far end looking back. The right bench is on the left of the frame, near to far: Shota, Tomoko, Hina, Yudai; Nakamura; Takumi, Misaki, Asuka, Kenta; Saito. The left bench is on the right of the frame, near to far: Ling Mo; Ito Nana, Ito Makoto; Yamamoto; Li Jie, Emi.

## Acts 4-14

After the act 3 choice the story splits: in branch 1a Li Jie gets off at Jishui and the art moves to the platform and the waiting room; in branch 1b he stays aboard, the doors lock and the train runs on along the coast to 幽冥之都, where he gets off in acts 7-8. The carriage, seating plan and window views above still hold inside the train.

Everything in this section comes from the act 15-17 text; the one thing the text adds that the art follows is that the glow, not the moons, lights the village (act 15: 一種介於月光與螢火之間的銀藍色輝光).

- **Palette and sky:** a pale silver-blue glow with deep violet-black shadows, as in act 14's last shot, but now with warmth: the village is calm, safe and a little dreamlike. Above the valley rim the sky is the same deep violet night as everywhere in this world, with the two faint thin sickle moons (the large one on the left, as always); a thin silver haze hangs low over the roofs and never hides them (act 17: 月光林地常年籠罩在濃霧之中, but act 15 shows the whole valley). It is always night here (act 17: 永夜之地), so there is no daylight in any shot.
- **The village** (act 15): a quiet village cradled in a ring-shaped valley. The dwellings look grown from the earth, built of white rock and thick ancient trees intertwined; some hang among the branches of the great trees, reached by stairways woven of vines. Flat white stone slabs make the paths (act 16: 平整的白色石板), with glowing plants on both sides: some hang down like wind chimes and sway, some bloom like open flowers and light the air around them, some are planted in clay pots. A clear stream winds through the middle over smooth stones and shows the glowing plants in its water; a small sturdy footbridge woven of vines crosses it (act 16: 藤蔓編織而成, 卻堅固穩當), with pebbles and little silver fish visible below. Every village prompt starts with this sentence:

the elven village of 月光林地 (Moonlight Glade), a tranquil village cradled in a ring-shaped valley of dark forested hills: dwellings that look grown from the earth, built of white rock and thick ancient trees intertwined, some of them hanging among the branches of huge old trees with stairways woven from vines down to the ground; flat white stone slab paths wind between them with glowing plants on both sides, some hanging down like wind chimes and swaying gently, some blooming like open flowers that cast soft light all around, some growing in fine clay pots; a clear stream winds through the middle of the village over smooth stones, its surface reflecting the glowing plants; everything is bathed in a dreamy silver-blue glow, a light between moonlight and firefly light, under a thin low silver haze and a deep violet night sky with two faint thin sickle moons, the large one on the left; no lamps, no fires, no signposts, no fences, no text.

- **The guest house, outside** (act 16 L11-L12): the last house at the village edge, beside one enormous ancient tree, a few minutes' walk from the centre (about five minutes). Simple and elegant, the same white rock and pale timber as the village, a roof covered in silver-green moss, a rounded plank front door with a few glowing flowers planted in front, semicircular windows glowing warm. It looks as if it grew out of the land. Every outside prompt starts with this sentence:

a small guest house at the edge of the elven village of 月光林地, standing beside one enormous ancient tree: simple, graceful walls of white rock and pale timber, a low roof covered in silver-green moss, a rounded plank front door with a few glowing flowers planted in front of it, and semicircular windows glowing with a warm light, so that the house looks as if it grew out of the land; behind it the village glows silver-blue under a thin low haze and a deep violet night sky; glowing plants along the white stone slab path; no signs, no fences, no text.

- **The guest house, inside** (act 16 L13-L16 and L25-L26): one spacious, bright, elegant open space with the kitchen in it, much grander than the cabin in the fog. The layout is fixed, seen from just inside the front door: the kitchen along the left wall; two soft sofas facing each other across a low table of one block of clear crystal with petals frozen inside, on a rug of fine woven pattern, in the middle; on the far wall a bookshelf of finely bound books and small delicate ornaments, the back door (a rounded plank door) at the left end beside the kitchen corner, and the picture of pressed leaves and petals at the right end; three rounded plank bedroom doors in the right wall (the near one is Li Jie's, the middle one is Emi's, the far one is the spare room Ailaru comes out of in act 17); the front door is behind the camera, a heavy rounded plank door with a semicircular window on each side. The ceiling is a canopy of living woven vines with small glowing flowers like an upside-down starry sky (L16), the walls are timber interwoven with white stone and set with soft glowing crystals, the floor is polished white stone. Every inside prompt starts with this sentence:

inside the guest house in the elven village of 月光林地: one spacious, bright, open living space of polished smooth white stone floor and walls of timber interwoven with white stone, set with soft glowing crystals, seen from just inside the front door. Along the left wall a neat kitchen of pale wooden cupboards, a plank counter and a small clay stove; across the middle of the room, on a rug of fine woven pattern, two soft sofas covered in silky pale cloth face each other across a low table made from one block of clear crystal with petals frozen inside; on the far wall a bookshelf of finely bound books and small delicate ornaments, a rounded plank back door at its left end beside the kitchen corner, and a picture made of pressed leaves and petals at its right end; in the right wall three rounded plank bedroom doors, near, middle and far; semicircular windows; the ceiling is a canopy of living woven vines dotted with small glowing flowers like an upside-down starry sky; glowing plants in fine clay pots on the windowsills and the table; warm, elegant and tidy, no electric light, no text.

- **A bedroom** (act 16 L43-L47, act 17 L1-L14): the two bedrooms are alike. A small calm room of timber and white stone with a rounded plank door, a soft bed with a carved pale wooden frame and clean white bedding that smells of sun and flowers (L43), one semicircular window looking out on the quiet village where the glowing plants shine like stars (L43), a small wardrobe, one soft crystal on the wall turned low at night, a ceiling of woven vines with a few faint glowing flowers. Each has its own bathroom behind a door; the bathroom is never drawn. Every bedroom prompt starts with this sentence:

a bedroom in the guest house in the elven village of 月光林地: a small, calm room of timber and white stone with a rounded plank door, a soft bed with a carved pale wooden frame and clean white bedding on one side, one semicircular window beside the bed looking out on the quiet village where the glowing plants shine like stars, a small pale wardrobe, one soft glowing crystal on the wall turned low for the night, a ceiling of woven living vines with a few faint glowing flowers; no text.

- **The fire** (act 17): the same village, set on fire in the night by someone nobody sees (the attackers are never drawn). Flames climb the houses and the great trees one after another, the glowing plants burn and burst with a crackle, thick grey-black smoke rolls over the valley and hides the moons, the vine bridge burns and snaps, the stream reflects the flames like molten lava, half the sky is orange. The palette is orange-red firelight against violet-black night and grey-black smoke. The villagers (see below) run out of the houses in panic, some carrying small children or helping the old, all the same way, north; they are silhouettes against the flames with no faces. Non-graphic: no bodies, no wounds, no burning people, no attacker. Inside the guest house the fire starts at the front door (L5: 木門的下半部分已經開始燃燒), so the smoke is grey and thin in the bedrooms and thicker in the living room; the back door is clear until they open it (L15-L17). Every fire prompt for the village starts with this sentence:

the elven village of 月光林地 exactly as in the reference image (the ring-shaped valley of dark forested hills, the dwellings of white rock and ancient trees, the white stone paths, the stream and the vine footbridge), now burning in the night: orange-red flames climb the houses and the great trees one after another, the glowing plants burn and burst in showers of sparks, thick grey-black smoke rolls over the valley and hides the moons, the vine bridge burns, and the stream reflects the flames like molten lava; half the sky is lit orange. Tall slender elves run out of the houses in panic, some carrying small children or helping the old, all running the same way, small backlit silhouettes with no faces visible. Nothing graphic: no bodies, no wounds, no burning people, no attackers in view.
- **The trail north** (act 17 L27, L45): the way out of the valley toward 銀月湖 is rougher and more rugged than the way they came in (L27: 比他們來時的路更加崎嶇): bare packed earth with roots and loose stones, climbing between huge ancient trees whose trunks throw long strange shadows in the firelight from behind (L27: 投下詭異的影子). It is the primitive wild forest of the other routes, not a paved road: no paving, no markers. Some of the fleeing elves hold glowing stones (L45: 發光石), which are the only light apart from the fire behind. Every trail prompt starts with this sentence:

the forest trail leading north out of the ring-shaped valley of 月光林地: a rough, narrow trail of bare packed earth, rocky and uneven, with tree roots and loose stones across it, climbing between huge ancient trees whose gnarled trunks and tangled roots throw long strange shadows in the light of a distant fire behind; no paving, no markers, no lamps, no buildings, no fences; the night sky above the canopy is deep violet-black.

- **Ailaru (艾拉如):** an elf who looks about eighty and carries it well (act 15 L18-L19): straight waist-length silver-white hair worn loose behind his shoulders, long pointed ears, a still handsome, refined face with fine dense wrinkles, gentle wise pale green eyes (the text gives the elves pale green or blue eyes; his are green, matching his gem), a straight back and a slow, graceful, steady step, a long white robe embroidered with silver vine patterns, and a polished green gem pendant on his chest. His voice is warm and calm; in the fire (act 17) his hair is disordered, his robe dusty (L6) and he presses a wet cloth over his nose and mouth (L15). He is not frightened but shaken, and he is evasive when asked where his son is (L34-L36). His portrait is the face reference, and `outfit_ailaru.png` (front and back) fixes the robe. The text does not name any other elf in these acts.
- **The villagers:** unnamed, drawn only as small, far-off figures with faces too small to read or turned away, never in front of the cast and never close to the camera (the same rule as the commuters on the Shinjuku platform). They are tall and slender (about 170 cm), with long silver or gold hair, pointed ears, pale skin, and light long robes in white, pale green or silver-grey (act 15 L7-L9). In peace they fetch water at the stream, weave under a tree, and a few small children chase each other on the path (L9); in the fire they run. The young elves, the mother and the child of act 18 are named beats of the next batch and are not drawn here. There is no villager reference image.
- **Robes:** after their baths Li Jie and Emi each wear a simple long white elf robe from the guest-house wardrobe (act 16 L31 and L34: 簡單的白色長袍, 精靈長袍, light and soft): a plain ankle-length white linen robe with soft sleeves, a loose cloth belt and a thin line of silver thread at the collar, the same for both. Emi's hair is still damp from L31 until she goes to sleep; Li Jie's too at L35. They sleep in the robes and run out in them (act 17 L1-L14 show no change). The robes are a scene wardrobe state: they override the Outfits table, and the portraits and full-body images then fix only faces and hair. The text never mentions shoes in the fire; on the rough trail they wear their own (Li Jie's black dress shoes, Emi's white canvas sneakers), which is the only way they could climb it; in the bedrooms and the house their feet are out of frame.

### The footpath north of 幽冥之都 (branch 1b/2a, acts 9-10)

The path from the crossroads outside the station (the map, act 9) to 亡者峽谷. Hapadona's direction (2026-11-22, pinned on act10_alone_path): not a well-paved road, a primitive wild forest. So it is a narrow trail of bare, hard-trodden earth, fairly level but a little uneven, with leaf litter, roots and a few half-buried stones, its edges ragged where ferns and undergrowth creep in; no gravel bed, no kerb or edging stones, nothing built. The forest around it is primeval old growth: huge gnarled mossy trunks, some leaning or fallen, vines, ferns and thick undergrowth, with moonlight falling through the leaves in patches (act 9). No mist and no fog, unlike 迷霧之森. It takes them one to two hours to the cabin and about three hours alone from the cabin to the canyon (act 10). Every footpath prompt starts with this sentence:

the forest trail north of the station of 幽冥之都, leading toward 亡者峽谷: a narrow, rough trail of bare, hard-trodden earth about one to one and a half people wide, fairly level but a little uneven, with leaf litter, fallen twigs, a few half-buried stones and tree roots crossing it, its edges soft and ragged where ferns, moss and low undergrowth creep over them (no paving, no gravel bed, no kerb stones, no stone edging, no squared or cut stones, no rows of stones, no markers, nothing built); it runs through a primeval, wild old-growth forest of huge gnarled mossy trunks, some leaning, some fallen and rotting, with tangled roots, hanging vines, thick ferns and dense undergrowth pressing in from both sides, the branches meeting overhead in a dense canopy that hides the sky and the two moons; only thin threads and scattered patches of cold violet moonlight slip through gaps in the leaves, dappling the bare earth with light and long soft shadows; no mist and no fog; no lamps, no buildings, no signposts, no fences.

### 林中木屋, the cabin in the forest (branch 1b/2a, acts 9-10)

Not the 霧中小屋 of branch 1a: that one is a single-storey cabin in thick fog; this one stands in a clearing under the moons, two storeys high with a porch (act 9: 雙層的木屋, a small porch with a few wooden steps, a pointed roof, a straight chimney). Hapadona's direction (2026-11-22, pinned on act9_porch): more primitive, a plain wooden house. So it is a hand-built log cabin of rough-hewn, unpainted logs notched at the corners and chinked with clay, a roof of hand-split shakes, a crude fieldstone-and-clay chimney, a rough plank porch with log steps and small framed windows with old glass panes; no paint, trim or carving. It is still sound, swept clean and obviously looked after, and nobody is there, but the hearth is still warm (act 10). Every outside prompt starts with this sentence:

a primitive two-storey log cabin standing alone in a small clearing of rough grass and bare earth in the forest north of 幽冥之都: hand-built walls of rough-hewn, unpainted dark logs laid horizontally, knotty and uneven, notched at the corners with the log ends sticking out, the gaps chinked with clay and moss; a steep pointed roof of rough hand-split wooden shakes (uneven boards, no neat shingles) with a crude chimney of stacked fieldstone and clay rising from the ridge; a small rough porch of unplaned planks across the front with three log steps up to a plain heavy plank door; two small square windows on the ground floor, one either side of the door, and two small ones upstairs, each a rough framed opening with small old glass panes, all dark, no light inside and no smoke; sound and swept clean but plainly home-made, with no paint, no carving, no trim, no fence, no sign, no lamp and no other building; the dense forest stands behind it like a black wall; above the clearing a patch of open black sky shows two thin violet crescent moons in the black sky, the large one on the left and the small one on the right, their discs dark; cold violet moonlight on the roof and the ground.

Inside is one ground-floor room (act 10: a sturdy table with four chairs, a kitchen counter with clay bowls and plates, a stone fireplace with firewood stacked beside it, a single bed with a clean blanket, a closed door that looks like a toilet). The upper floor and any staircase are never mentioned and never drawn. The inside is as primitive as the outside: bare rough-hewn log walls chinked with clay, heavy rough beams, a floor of wide uneven hand-sawn planks, a heavy rough-sawn table with four simple handmade chairs, a coarse plank counter, a hand-built fieldstone fireplace and a plain homespun blanket on the bed. Every inside prompt starts with this sentence, which fixes the layout seen from the front door:

inside the primitive log cabin in the forest north of 幽冥之都, the ground-floor room: one room with walls of bare rough-hewn logs chinked with clay, a low ceiling of heavy rough beams with split boards above, a floor of wide uneven hand-sawn planks, swept clean and dry, no dust and no cobwebs; no lamp is lit and there is no electric light: the only light is cold violet moonlight falling through the front door and the small framed windows in long pale shafts across the floor. ### 亡者峽谷 and the railway to it (branch 1b/2a, acts 10-11)

The platform "extends over the canyon like a bridge" (act 10), and the rails run along a roadbed with a drop on both sides (act 11: 軌道兩側是深不見底的峽谷,只有窄窄的路基可以行走).
