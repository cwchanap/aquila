# Act 3: Jakkai Station

**Narrator**: The train passed through the eerie blue-black curtain of light. In an instant, everything changed. The shaking of the car faded to almost nothing, as if the train were floating on rails no one could see.

**Narrator**: The glare drained away. Before every passenger's eyes lay a world that could not possibly exist — and yet was utterly, undeniably real.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) This is...

**Narrator**: The view outside was no longer the Tokyo night they knew. To the left of the train lay a deep, dark forest. Every tree stood impossibly tall and straight, the canopy so dense that not a sliver of moonlight could get through. Within the woods, there was only dead silence.

**Narrator**: To the right stretched a black sea without end, its surface eerily calm, like an enormous mirror.

**Narrator**: In the sky hung two violet crescent moons — one large, one small, like a pair of cold eyes. And between the two violet moons were stars no one had ever seen, set in strange patterns, slowly turning.

**Bubbly Student**: Wh... where are we? Isn't Shinjuku inland? What's with this ocean?

**Loudmouth Student**: This isn't Tokyo... No way is this Tokyo! Look at the moon! There's two of them!

**Chatterbox Student**: What do we do, what do we do, is this a dream? It has to be a dream, right?

**Narrator**: She pinched her own arm, hard.

**Chatterbox Student**: Ow... Why does it hurt...

**Steady Student**: This is impossible... It breaks every law of physics...

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: I-it must be some kind of promotional event... Yes, a mirage, created through atmospheric reflection... A hologram...

**Irritable Husband**: What kind of crap are you spouting now?! Your hologram can change the smell of the air too, can it?! Somebody call the police! Does anyone's phone have a signal?!

**Narrator**: He yanked out his phone and refreshed the screen frantically, over and over. It still said "No Service." All around him, other passengers pulled out their own phones and found, in despair, the very same thing.

**Narrator**: The words "cut off" came down like a sledgehammer, smashing whatever hope anyone had left.

**Innocent Little Brother**: Mommy! I'm scared! I wanna go home! I wanna go home!

**Gentle Mother**: Shh, sweetheart... Don't be scared, don't be scared... We'll get home...

**Anxious Wife**: Are we dead? Is this hell?

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: Drive! Get this thing moving! Take us back! Where's the driver?!

**Narrator**: Screams, sobs and prayers tangled together into chaos. Someone knelt on the floor with palms pressed together; someone curled up in a seat, clutching their head; someone pounded on a window like a madman.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked around at face after contorted face. Then his eyes fell on the girl.

**Narrator**: The girl who had sat sketching so quietly on the platform was now huddled in the corner of her seat, shaking violently. Her sketchbook had slipped from her hands to the floor. She was gripping her own arms tight.

**Narrator**: Her face was as white as paper, her eyes wide and fixed on the black sea beyond the window. Her lips trembled as if she wanted to say something, but no sound came out.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) She looks... more frightened than anyone. And that look in her eyes... It's almost as if she recognizes this place?

**Narrator**: Just as the panic reached its height, the train began to slow. The sound of the wheels on the rails grew slower and softer, until it could barely be heard at all.

**Narrator**: Up ahead on the shoreline, out of the darkness, a solitary station came into view.

**Generous Older Man**: There's a station up ahead! Look, everyone! A station means there are people!

**Narrator**: For a moment, his words quieted the chaos in the car.

**Narrator**: The station was tiny — just a bare platform and a battered waiting shelter. The train glided slowly in and came to a perfect stop alongside the platform.

**Narrator**: On the platform stood a station sign, badly eaten away by the sea wind. Two characters in an archaic script gave the station's name: "Jakkai."

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Jakkai... The silent sea...

**Narrator**: Inside the car, everyone held their breath, waiting for an announcement. But there was nothing. Only a deathly silence.

**Narrator**: In that suffocating silence, the doors gave a soft hiss and slowly slid apart.

**Narrator**: A rush of cold air poured in from outside, thick with salt and the scent of unfamiliar plants. Beyond the platform lay a bottomless dark. No lights. No one in sight.

**Narrator**: Only the two violet crescent moons, and the dead-still black sea, watching them in silence.

**Narrator**: No one moved. The passengers who had been wailing a moment ago now stared at the open doors in terror.

**Generous Older Man**: Should we... go out and take a look? Maybe there's someone in the station...

**Irritable Husband**: Are you out of your mind? What the hell kind of place is that out there?! God knows what we'd run into!

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: Right! We're safer on the train! What if the doors close and it leaves without us?!

**Bubbly Student**: But... we don't know what'll happen if we stay here either...

**Loudmouth Student**: I... I don't know what to do either...

**Chatterbox Student**: What do we do, what do we do, what are we supposed to do...

**Narrator**: The car dissolved into frantic argument.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Stay, and we're shut inside a steel box nobody's in control of. Go, and we're stepping into a world none of us can understand... What... are we supposed to do?

**Narrator**: The cold wind kept blowing in through the open doors. The two violet moons hung quietly in the sky, as if waiting for their decision.

**Narrator**: Time seemed to stand still. But the moment of choice was coming all the same.
