# Act 5: An Unexpected Companion

**Narrator**: On the platform of Jakkai Station, Li Jie had just set foot on the cold concrete when hurried footsteps sounded behind him. He turned, and there, to his surprise, stood the quiet girl who had been sketching on the platform.

**Narrator**: Her face was pale, both hands clutching the sketchbook tight. Her eyes darted this way and that, as though she had made a decision she couldn't even understand herself.

**Li Jie**: (doubtful, a hint of wariness) Why did you... follow me down? Wouldn't it be safer to stay on the train?

**Quiet Girl**: (head down, voice very small) I... I don't know... On the train... it was so loud... Everyone was arguing...

**Quiet Girl**: (after a pause) And then... I saw you get off... and I thought... it would be better to follow you...

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Her voice is quiet, but she's not as panicked as I expected. It's just... why follow a total stranger?

**Quiet Girl**: (lifting her head, a flicker of resolve in her eyes) You... you said you were coming down to look for answers, right...? I want to know too... where this place is...

**Quiet Girl**: (her voice still very small) And... if I were alone... I'd be scared...

**Li Jie**: (thinking) She says she's scared, but that look in her eyes... it doesn't seem like just fear. Never mind — this isn't the time to think about it.

**Li Jie**: (sighing, his tone softening) I see. Well, you're already down here, so let's go together. What's your name?

**Quiet Girl**: (her voice a little clearer) I... I'm Shimizu Emi...

**Li Jie**: (nodding, holding out his hand) Shimizu. I'm Li Jie.

**Narrator**: Emi shook his hand. Her hand was cold, but her grip was steady — a subtle contrast with the timid way she spoke.

**Narrator**: In the brief exchange between Li Jie and Emi, another commotion broke out inside the train. Li Jie's getting off, and Emi's following him, seemed to have broken the deadlock among the passengers.

**Loudmouth Student**: (shouting from inside the car) Hey! They got off! We can't just sit here forever either, right? This train doesn't look like it's going anywhere!

**Bubbly Student**: (her voice shaking) But... outside... it's so dark out there... And those two moons... Don't you guys think it's creepy...?

**Steady Student**: (analyzing it calmly) Staying here isn't a solution either. There's no food, no water, and no telling what'll happen. Better to act than to sit and wait.

**Chatterbox Student**: (talking fast) Right, right, and what if the train suddenly leaves? What if it just keeps going farther and farther? Maybe there are people in the station outside, and we could get in touch with the outside world...

**Generous Older Man**: (standing up, shouting to the people in the car) They're right! This train isn't moving anymore! Are we supposed to wait here for the rest of our lives?! We can't just sit here and let ourselves die!

**Narrator**: The older man moved first. He grabbed his bag and strode for the doors. His determined back looked strangely tall under the dim carriage lights, and it seemed to lend the others a little courage.

**Generous Older Man**: (looking back over his shoulder, calling into the car) Who else is coming? Don't hesitate now!

**Generous Older Man**: (stepping out through the doors, to Li Jie and Emi on the platform) Son! Miss! Wait for me! Let's go together! There's strength in numbers!

**Narrator**: The four university students followed on his heels. They jostled one another, hesitating a few seconds at the doors. In the end, youthful curiosity and the pressure of the group won out over fear.

**Loudmouth Student**: (gritting his teeth, making up his mind) Let's go! We all go together! Better than staying here waiting to die!

**Bubbly Student**: (clutching the loudmouth student's sleeve) Then... then I'm coming too... Don't leave me behind...

**Steady Student**: (the last to step out of the car, calmly) Once you've decided, don't regret it.

**Chatterbox Student**: (muttering as she climbs down) Ugh... I'm so scared... But I guess I'll go with them... Staying on the train alone would be even scarier... And if something does happen, at least we can look out for each other...

**Narrator**: Just when everyone thought that all those willing to get off had done so, the timid guy in glasses suddenly rose from his seat and hurried toward the doors.

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: (pushing up his glasses with a sneer as he steps down, the cold sweat on his forehead betraying his fear) Hmph. A flock of foolish sheep, all thinking they've made the right choice.

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: (pretending calm) I'm not following you! I'm simply going out to look for a more scientific explanation! A supernatural phenomenon like this must have a rational explanation in physics!

**Narrator**: Inside the car, the selfish middle-aged man hunched in his seat, watching more and more people get off. A look of struggle crossed his face. His hand went to the armrest; he made to rise, then sank back down.

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: (muttering under his breath, his voice full of fear) No... it's too dangerous out there... I'm not going... It's definitely safer on the train...

**Narrator**: Eight people soon stood gathered on the platform: Li Jie, Emi, the older man, the four university students, and the timid guy in glasses, who had still followed them down despite his sharp tongue.

**Narrator**: They stood at the edge of the platform, looking back at the passengers left in the train — the middle-aged couple holding each other tight, the family with children huddled in a corner, and a few passengers sitting alone, their faces blank.

**Narrator**: Inside the car, the remaining passengers stared out at the group with pale faces. In some eyes there was envy, in others incomprehension, in others fear; in a few, a flicker of doubt about the choice they had made.

**Narrator**: The silence lasted only a few seconds. Before these "forerunners" had even found their footing — before they had a chance to discuss what to do next —

**Narrator**: A shrill "hsss—" cut through the dead silence of Jakkai Station without warning.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) (his head snapping around) That's... the doors!

**Narrator**: The doors! The train doors, open until a moment ago, had begun to slide shut — with no announcement, no warning at all!

**Loudmouth Student**: (crying out) The doors... the doors are closing!

**Bubbly Student**: (grabbing the loudmouth student's arm) Wh-what's going on?!

**Narrator**: The doors met with a hard click and sealed shut. Through the windows, Li Jie saw the passengers inside flinch, their bodies shrinking back on instinct.

**Narrator**: But a moment later, wild joy broke across their faces — some clung to each other in their excitement, some raised their hands toward the ceiling in thanks, some even burst out laughing.

**Narrator**: The train began to pick up speed, wheels grinding against the rails with a heavy roar. The engine climbed from a low growl to a high, keening howl, like a beast waking up.

**Narrator**: Through the windows, Li Jie saw the passengers waving at the eight on the platform. Some wore smiles of relief; others looked openly gleeful at their misfortune.

**Narrator**: The selfish middle-aged man was shouting something through the glass, a smug grin on his face. The sound didn't carry, but his lips and expression made it easy enough to guess — he was mocking the choice they had made.

**Narrator**: The train drew farther and farther away, faster and faster. The lights inside the car shrank into the night, like a firefly drifting off.

**Narrator**: At last that small light vanished in the direction they had come from — deep into that dark forest, swallowed whole by the night.

**Narrator**: Silence fell over the platform. There was only the howl of the sea wind and, far off, the low boom of waves breaking against the rocks.

**Narrator**: The eight survivors stood rooted where they were. They had been abandoned at this unknown station called Jakkai, facing an endless dark, an unnatural forest, and a dead, still sea.

**Narrator**: And overhead, the two strange violet crescent moons, watching them like eyes.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) (slowly turning his head toward where the train had vanished) It... just left... It's really gone...

**Shimizu Emi**: (her voice soft, but calmer than before) Are we... never going home...?

**Narrator**: Li Jie noticed that Emi's voice, though still quiet, no longer trembled the way it had. She stood there quietly, gazing after the vanished train, and something unreadable flickered in her eyes.

**Generous Older Man**: (drawing a deep breath, his voice grave) What's done is done. Complaining won't help. We have to find a way to survive.

**Narrator**: The cold sea wind swept across the platform, carrying the scent of unfamiliar plants and the salt tang of the sea. The eight of them stood in the empty station, facing a fate none of them knew.
