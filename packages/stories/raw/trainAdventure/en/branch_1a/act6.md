# Act 6: The Divided Survivors

**Narrator**: The train's roar faded completely into the darkness, and a deathly silence settled over the platform. There was only the sound of the cold sea wind brushing their cheeks, and the eerie light the two violet crescent moons cast down from overhead.

**Narrator**: The eight of them had been abandoned here, their faces written over with shock and bewilderment.

**Generous Older Man**: (breaking the silence, his voice hoarse) ...So... what do we do now?

**Bubbly Student**: (breaking down, pointing at Li Jie through her tears) It's all your fault! If you hadn't gone first, we'd be on our way home right now!

**Loudmouth Student**: (backing her up) That's right! There was nothing wrong with that train! You tricked us all!

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: (pushing up his glasses, his tone acid) I said from the start we shouldn't act rashly. And now look — dumped in this godforsaken place! Feeling pleased with yourself, are you? How do you intend to take responsibility?

**Narrator**: Li Jie paid their accusations no mind. Arguing in a situation like this was meaningless — it would only waste energy and time. He turned away and began to study the abandoned station carefully.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) The waiting room... the timetable... and this station sign...

**Narrator**: He walked over to the old wooden station sign that read "Jakkai." Years of sea wind had rotted away the edges of the board. He noticed that beneath the two large characters of the name, there was another line of small writing, almost worn away.

**Narrator**: He leaned in, and by the strange moonlight he just managed to make it out.

**Narrator**: [← the Eternal Forest | the City of the Netherworld →]

**Li Jie**: (thinking) The Eternal Forest... the City of the Netherworld... The last stop wasn't Shinjuku, and the next isn't anywhere we know. It looks like we really have come to another world.

**Narrator**: A vague guess about the shape of this world was forming in his mind, but he had no proof. He turned back to the others, who were still arguing, and spoke.

**Li Jie**: Instead of standing here arguing, we'd do better to look for clues. I'm going to check the waiting room.

**Generous Older Man**: The young man's right. I'll come too.

**Shimizu Emi**: (walking up beside Li Jie, very quietly) I... I'll come too...

**Narrator**: Li Jie, Emi and the older man walked toward the dark mouth of the waiting room, leaving behind the students and the timid guy in glasses, still trading blame.

**Narrator**: A thick layer of dust had gathered in the waiting room, and the air was rank with rotting wood and dirt. Cobwebs draped the rows of wooden benches.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took out his phone and switched on the flashlight, sweeping the cramped space. On the far wall he found a parchment map, half peeling away.

**Li Jie**: Mister, Shimizu — come take a look!

**Narrator**: The two of them came over, and the beam of the phone lit up the old map.

**Generous Older Man**: (frowning, peering at it closely) What's all this writing? All twists and squiggles... I can't make out a single one?

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Can't read it? These are just ordinary Chinese characters, aren't they? The style is a little old-fashioned... but every character is perfectly clear. Why can't he read them? That's strange.

**Narrator**: Li Jie was about to explain when his eyes, almost by habit, went to Emi beside him. She was staring at the map, her gaze clear and sharp — plainly understanding everything on it.

**Narrator**: But the moment she noticed his gaze, she dropped her eyes like a startled small animal, gave a small shake of her head, and put on an expression of equal confusion.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Hm? That look she just had... she didn't look like someone who couldn't read it. Is she hiding something? Never mind — this isn't the time to press her on it.

**Li Jie**: (explaining to the older man and Emi) This... is Jakkai Station.

**Narrator**: He pointed at the map. A clear main path led west from the station, running deep into an area labeled "the Misty Forest."

**Narrator**: About halfway through the forest, a smaller side path branched off the main one, marked as leading toward "the City of the Netherworld."

**Narrator**: At the map's edges were a few more faint, indistinct paths that seemed to point toward unlabeled regions; all of them vanished where the paper was torn away.

**Narrator**: Just as Li Jie was trying to tease more clues out of the ruined map, the argument outside the waiting room grew louder, drowning out the wind and carrying in clearly.

**Generous Older Man**: (his face darkening) No, I'd better go out and see. Those kids are too hot-headed. If something happens, it'll be a real mess!

**Narrator**: The older man hurried out of the waiting room. Li Jie and Emi stayed where they were, watching through the battered windows. They could only hear the furious back-and-forth.

**Generous Older Man**: (his voice carrying from the platform) Calm down, all of you! Splitting up now won't do anyone any good!

**Loudmouth Student**: Mister, stay out of this! We're not staying with the guy who got us stuck here! We're going to find our own way out!

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: I told you it's dangerous out there! Can't you idiots understand plain words?! Waiting here for daybreak is the only rational choice!

**Bubbly Student**: You're the coward! We're leaving!

**Loudmouth Student**: That's right! We're going! Whoever wants to stay can stay!

**Narrator**: A clatter of footsteps broke out. Through the window, Li Jie watched the four students walk off toward the forest.

**Narrator**: The timid guy in glasses stood where he was, staring at their retreating backs. Fear, hesitation, and a flicker of resentment crossed his face.

**Timid Guy in Glasses**: (suddenly shouting) Wait... wait for me! Don't leave me on my own!

**Narrator**: He scrambled after them, stumbling along behind the students. The five figures shrank into the distance and disappeared toward the forest.

**Narrator**: The argument died away completely, leaving only the howl of the sea wind.

**Narrator**: After a while the older man came back into the waiting room, his face helpless, and picked up his backpack.

**Generous Older Man**: (sighing, his tone firm) Ah... They've all gone into the forest, even that kid in glasses. I don't feel right about it — young people don't know when to stop. I'll follow them and keep an eye on things. At least then there'll be someone to look after them if something goes wrong.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Should I follow them... or stay and keep looking into this station...
