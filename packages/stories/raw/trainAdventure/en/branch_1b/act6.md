# Act 6: The City of the Netherworld

**Narrator**: The train ran slowly and steadily along the coastline. The view outside was the same uncanny scene — the deep forest on the left, the still black sea on the right, and the two violet crescent moons hanging quietly in the sky.

**Narrator**: But the people left in the car seemed determined to forget all of it. The relief of having made it through had lulled them into a giddy, unrealistic optimism.

**Anxious Wife**: (letting out a long breath, patting her chest) Phew, at least that's over. When we get home, the first thing I'm doing is taking a nice long hot bath! That scared me half to death. I'm definitely taking tomorrow off work!

**Irritable Husband**: (his tone a lot softer too) Once we're back, let's grab something to eat first. Find an izakaya and drink till we drop! Damn, never in my life have I been through anything this messed up.

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: (clearing his throat, smug) What did I tell you? It's obviously just some little scheduling glitch — we took a detour, that's all. Look at you lot, scared out of your wits. You've really never seen anything of the world.

**Gentle Mother**: (gently, to the children) When we get to the station, Mommy and Daddy will take you home to bed. You've got school tomorrow.

**Innocent Little Brother**: Mommy, I want ice cream when we get home!

**Gentle Mother**: (smiling) All right, all right, you can have some when we get home.

**Sensible Big Sister**: (whispering) It's really late already. Why don't you have it tomorrow, okay?

**Narrator**: The car filled with chatter, children's laughter and plans for what came next. As if the terror of a moment ago had been nothing but a nightmare, and now they had woken up and were on their way home.

**Narrator**: Only Li Jie sat silent in a corner, gazing out at the endless black sea. He couldn't bring himself to share in their cheap optimism.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Home... Do they really believe that? Or are they just too scared to face reality?

**Narrator**: The scenery outside never changed. No city lights, no familiar landmarks — only the eternal dark forest and the deathly silent sea. The sound of the wheels on the rails kept its steady rhythm — clack, clack, clack.

**Narrator**: The minutes ticked by. Ten. Fifteen. Twenty. The train kept following the coastline, and the view outside did not change at all.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's eyes never left the window. He watched the violet moons drift slowly across the sky, watched the black shapes of the forest stream past the glass. His unease didn't fade; if anything, it grew stronger the farther the train went.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Where... does this line actually go?

**Narrator**: Just then, the train began to slow. The sound of the wheels on the rails grew slow and heavy.

**Anxious Wife**: (uneasily) Huh... is the train slowing down?

**Irritable Husband**: (looking out the window, frowning) Looks like there's something up ahead...

**Narrator**: The chatter in the car slowly died away. Sensing the change, the passengers turned to the windows one after another. But outside there was still only the dark forest and the sea, without the faintest sign of Tokyo.

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: (forcing himself to sound calm) We must be... we must be almost there, right? Maybe there's a station up ahead...

**Narrator**: On the dark horizon, the outline of a station appeared.

**Narrator**: But it was not the Tokyo station they had hoped for. This station was huge — far bigger than Jakkai. Its architecture was classical and uncanny, like a ruin left behind by some distant age.

**Narrator**: Towering stone pillars held up an enormous roof, and the walls were carved all over with incomprehensible symbols and patterns. There were no lights on the platform, only the eerie glow of the two violet moons, which bathed the entire station in a gloomy violet-black.

**Narrator**: The train glided slowly in alongside the platform and came to a smooth stop. The sound of the wheels faded away, leaving only a suffocating silence.

**Narrator**: Holding their breath, the passengers looked out at the enormous station sign on the platform. Carved into it in an ancient, unfamiliar script were four large characters —

**Narrator**: "City of the Netherworld."

**Irritable Husband**: (his voice shaking) Wh... what?

**Narrator**: "Hssss—"

**Narrator**: The doors slid open on cue. Cold air flooded the car once more, carrying a stale, rotting smell.

**Narrator**: The car that had been full of laughter only a second before fell instantly into a deathly silence.

**Anxious Wife**: (the first to break, her voice trembling with fear) C-City... of the Netherworld? What the hell kind of place is this?

**Anxious Wife**: (dissolving into sobs) This isn't the way home... We're never getting back...

**Irritable Husband**: (face livid, slamming his fist into the window in a rage) Dammit! What the hell is this?! What is going on?!

**Selfish Middle-Aged Man**: (white as a sheet, shrieking in panic) It's over! We're finished! Is this the gate to hell? The ghosts are going to drag us away! I don't want to die!

**Innocent Little Brother**: (bawling) Mommy! Mommy! I'm scared!

**Gentle Mother**: (holding him tight, her voice trembling) Don't be scared, don't be scared... Mommy's right here...

**Sensible Big Sister**: (starting to cry too) Are we... are we really never going home?

**Narrator**: Despairing wails, furious curses and screams of terror wove together once more into a chaotic chorus. The children's crying and their parents' attempts to soothe them were mixed in, making it all the more hopeless and helpless.

**Narrator**: The people who had just been planning what they'd do when they got home had all fallen apart. With their false hope shattered, the fear was stronger than ever.

**Li Jie**: (thinking, thoroughly fed up) ...So loud.

**Narrator**: He looked at these grown adults coming apart at the seams. A moment ago they had sworn they were going home; now they were crying harder than the children. The fear in his chest gave way, instead, to a fierce irritation.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) At this point, what good is crying and complaining? Never mind the unknown dangers past those doors — the racket these people are making is a lot harder to take.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and steadied himself. His gaze went to the open doors, and to the eerie station that bore the name City of the Netherworld.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) If I stay here, I'll just end up like them, slowly falling apart from fear. Better to go out and look for clues than rot inside this steel box. Maybe... maybe I can find a way out of here.

**Narrator**: Amid the clamor, Li Jie stood up. Without hesitating, without looking back, he walked resolutely toward the open doors.

**Irritable Husband**: (spotting Li Jie, loudly) Hey! Where do you think you're going?!

**Li Jie**: (calmly) To see what's out there.

**Narrator**: He stepped through the doors and onto the eerie platform of the City of the Netherworld. Cold air closed around him, and the ancient stone pillars cast long shadows beneath the violet moons.

**Narrator**: Behind him, the wailing in the car grew distant. And ahead lay an unfathomable darkness.
