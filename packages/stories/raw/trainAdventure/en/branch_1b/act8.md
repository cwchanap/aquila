# Act 8: The Sacrificial Train

**Narrator**: Sword in hand, Li Jie ran out of the station hall behind the frantic brother and sister. His footsteps echoed across the empty platform, and a sense of foreboding rose in his chest.

**Narrator**: But the moment he stepped out of the hall, the sight before him sent a jolt through his whole body and stopped him dead.

**Narrator**: At the platform, the train they had been riding — its doors already shut tight — was slowly pulling away, heading off into the distant darkness.

**Li Jie**: (stunned) What...

**Narrator**: Behind a window, a man was pounding frantically on the glass of a door. His fists slammed into it again and again with dull thuds. His face was twisted with despair and fury, his mouth wide open as if he were shouting something.

**Narrator**: But through the glass, over the roar of the train, nothing could be heard.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) That's... the kids' father!

**Narrator**: And on the platform, a woman was struggling up off the ground. Her knees were scraped raw, blood trickling down her shins. Her clothes were smeared with dust and blood.

**Narrator**: She reached out toward the departing train as if trying to catch hold of something. But it kept pulling farther away, faster and faster.

**Gentle Mother**: (hoarse, crying out in despair) Wait... please, wait...

**Narrator**: The moment the children saw their mother, they ran to her, sobbing.

**Sensible Big Sister**: (throwing herself at her mother, crying) Mom!

**Innocent Little Brother**: (bawling) Mommy! Mommy!

**Gentle Mother**: (sinking to her knees, opening her arms to hold them both) I'm all right... I'm all right... There, there, sweetie...

**Sensible Big Sister**: (crying) You're bleeding! Mom, you're bleeding!

**Gentle Mother**: (her voice shaking) It's just a scrape... It's fine... Just a scrape...

**Narrator**: She held both children close, but her eyes were fixed on the distance. The train had become a tiny black speck, and at last it vanished where the tracks ran out of sight.

**Gentle Mother**: (sobbing too hard to speak) Your daddy... your daddy's...

**Narrator**: Li Jie walked over to the shattered family. Planting the rusty sword on the ground, he spoke in a low voice.

**Li Jie**: What on earth happened?

**Narrator**: The sensible older sister looked up and scrubbed hard at her tears. She took a deep breath, struggling to calm herself, and spoke in a voice still thick with crying, but clear.

**Sensible Big Sister**: It was... it was the people on the train... Not long after you left, Mister, the scaredy-cat man suddenly started yelling.

**Sensible Big Sister**: He said he'd figured it out. He said the reason the doors closed and the train left at the last stop was because seven people got off.

**Sensible Big Sister**: He thinks... the train might need people to get off as a "sacrifice" before it'll keep going.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) What...?

**Sensible Big Sister**: (going on) The scary fat man agreed with him completely. They... they ganged up and said our family had four people, and that was too many.

**Sensible Big Sister**: (her voice trembling) The fat man tried to push me and my brother off the train... Dad started fighting him to protect us...

**Sensible Big Sister**: (wiping her tears) I... I remembered you got off before, Mister, and I thought you could help... so I took my brother and ran out to find you...

**Innocent Little Brother**: (crying) Sis ran so fast... I couldn't keep up...

**Narrator**: The girl's words chilled Li Jie to the bone. He looked at the mother.

**Li Jie**: And how did you end up...

**Gentle Mother**: (choking back tears, struggling to stay composed) When I saw them fighting, I wanted to go and pull the children away... so I got off after them...

**Gentle Mother**: (her voice shaking) But I was in such a hurry, I lost my footing and fell on the platform... and then... then the doors closed...

**Gentle Mother**: (weeping) Just like before, the doors suddenly closed... My husband is still on the train...

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's silence) ...I see.

**Narrator**: Her sobs echoed across the empty platform, desolate and forlorn. The two children clung to their mother, crying with her. The violet moonlight fell over the broken family, stretching their shadows long, long across the ground.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood to one side, silently watching. He tightened his grip on the rusty sword, feeling the cold of its hilt.

**Li Jie**: (slowly) ...So that cowardly man said it takes a "sacrifice" to make the train move on?

**Sensible Big Sister**: (nodding) Mm... He said seven people got off at the last stop, so somebody has to get off at this one too...

**Li Jie**: And on the train... how many people are left now?

**Gentle Mother**: (looking up, wiping her eyes) Besides my husband... there's that couple who were always arguing, and the man who came up with the sacrifice theory. Four in all.

**Li Jie**: (nodding, lost in thought) I see...

**Li Jie**: (thinking) That coward's insane theory... cruel as it is, could it actually... be right?

**Narrator**: He went back over everything that had happened. When they first boarded, how many people had been in the car? He counted them off in his head, one by one.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) When we first got on... counting me, there were fifteen of us.

**Narrator**: Then came Jakkai. The quiet girl, the steady older man, the guy in glasses, and the four university students — seven people in all had gotten off.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Fifteen people, seven got off, which left eight on the train.

**Narrator**: And now, at the City of the Netherworld, this family of four had been left on the platform. How many were still on the train?

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Eight minus four... leaves four people on the train.

**Narrator**: A terrifying pattern took shape in his mind. The numbers were so clear they made him shudder.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) 15 → 8 → 4... No, wait. It's not random. Counting from Jakkai, 8 → 4. The number dropped by exactly half.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's heart began to race. If the pattern held —

**Li Jie**: (thinking) The next station is the Canyon of the Dead. If the pattern holds there... half of them will be forced off again. That leaves two.

**Narrator**: His fists clenched until his knuckles went white. A terrible truth rose up before his eyes.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) This train... it's cutting down the number of passengers at every stop. And the people forced off... what happens to them? The older man, the girl, and now these kids' father...

**Narrator**: He remembered the dead silence of Jakkai and the desolation of the City of the Netherworld. Neither looked like a place where anyone could survive.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Stay on the train and who knows where it takes you; get off and who knows what you'll face... It's a trap with no way out.

**Narrator**: He pictured the faces of the people still in the car — the selfish, cowardly middle-aged man, the short-tempered husband, and his anxious, fretful wife. Who was left of them now? Who would be left behind at the next stop?

**Li Jie**: (quietly, so only he could hear) Good thing I got off...

**Narrator**: Just then, the mother braced her hands against the ground and slowly got to her feet. The cuts on her knees were still bleeding, but she no longer seemed to feel the pain.

**Gentle Mother**: (taking a deep breath, her voice shaking but firm) We can't... we can't just give up like this...

**Sensible Big Sister**: (worried) Mom, your legs...

**Gentle Mother**: (crouching down, wiping her eyes dry) Mommy's fine. Listen to me. We're going to go find Daddy now.

**Innocent Little Brother**: (looking up, eyes red) Where are we gonna find Daddy?

**Gentle Mother**: (pointing to where the tracks disappeared) The train went that way. What was the next station called... the Canyon of the Dead, wasn't it? Daddy will be waiting for us there, I know he will.

**Sensible Big Sister**: (frightened) But... that sounds so scary...

**Gentle Mother**: (hugging both children, her eyes utterly resolute) Don't be scared. We're a family, and we're going to be together. Mommy promises, we'll find Daddy.

**Narrator**: She stood and took both children by the hand. Her knees were still bleeding and she was covered in dust, but she held her back perfectly straight.

**Gentle Mother**: (looking at Li Jie) Thank you for listening to us... We have to go now.

**Li Jie**: (looking at the mother and her children) ...You're going to follow the tracks?

**Gentle Mother**: (nodding) Yes. The train went that way, so that's the way we'll go. No matter how far it is, no matter how dangerous, I have to find my husband.

**Innocent Little Brother**: (holding his mother's hand) We're gonna find Daddy, right?

**Gentle Mother**: (stroking his hair) That's right. We will.

**Narrator**: Now the decision lay before Li Jie. He touched the map in his pocket, then looked again at the mother and her two children.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Go with them? Follow the tracks to the Canyon of the Dead... It's the train's next stop. If their father really is still alive, that's where he'll be.

**Narrator**: But he also remembered what the sign had said — "Canyon of the Dead." The name alone told him it was no good place. And the train would stop there; there was no telling what might happen.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Or... go it alone and follow the map into the Misty Forest, or to Silver Moon Lake? Those places might be dangerous, but at least I wouldn't run into that eerie train again...

**Narrator**: One choice was to travel with the mother and her children — a clear goal, but perhaps more danger. The other was to explore the unknown alone — no companions, but more options.

**Narrator**: The two violet moons hung silently in the sky, gazing coldly down at the four people on the platform. In the distance, the black sea shimmered an eerie silver, and the deep forest stood like an everlasting black wall.

**Narrator**: Wind swept across the empty platform with a low moan. The tracks stretched away into the far darkness, leading who knew where.

**Gentle Mother**: (looking at Li Jie again) Will you... come with us?

**Narrator**: Li Jie didn't answer right away. He looked at the rusty sword in his hand, at the map in his pocket, and at the mother and her two children before him.

**Narrator**: In this uncanny world, every choice could be the difference between life and death.

**Narrator**: Li Jie had to choose, and now.
