# Act 10: Shelter in the Mist

**Narrator**: Inside the cabin, Asuka had finally calmed down a little. She wiped the tear tracks from her face with the back of her hand and took a deep breath, trying hard to pull herself together.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) So... Mr. Li, what are you going to do now?

**Li Jie**: (glancing at the map in his hand) I'm planning to head west, to Silver Moon Lake. Going by the map, that's the most likely place to find a way out.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (hesitantly) Um... could I come with you? I... I really can't stay here by myself anymore...

**Narrator**: There was a note of pleading in her voice, and her eyes were full of anxiety. After that horrifying attack and all those hours lost, she couldn't bear to face this forest alone any longer.

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's silence, nodding) All right. But I can't promise it'll be safe. And... you'll have to keep up with me. Try not to be too much of a burden.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding quickly) I will! I'll keep up, I promise! I won't slow you down!

**Li Jie**: (looking around the room) In that case, we'll rest here tonight. We'll set out first thing tomorrow.

**Narrator**: Li Jie got up and began to look the cabin over carefully. It wasn't large, but it had everything it needed.

**Narrator**: In the corner against the wall stood a double bed; the sheets had yellowed a little but were clean enough. On one side of the room was a simple kitchen area, with a few clay pots and some dishes on a wooden counter.

**Narrator**: On the other side was a half-open door, and through the gap he could see a bathroom. A wooden table and two chairs stood quietly by the window.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) Why don't you go wash up first? Looks like there's still water here we can use. Then see if there's anything in the kitchen we can cook.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Okay... What about you?

**Li Jie**: I'm going outside to shore up the door and windows. In a place like this, it pays to be careful.

**Narrator**: Asuka nodded and headed for the bathroom. Li Jie took the sword, pushed open the wooden door and went outside.

---

**Narrator**: The forest outside was still shrouded in that eerie violet moonlight. Li Jie searched around the cabin and found a few sturdy branches.

**Narrator**: Back at the front of the house, he wedged the thickest one at an angle between the door handle and the ground and shoved it in tight. That way, even if something tried to ram the door open from outside, the branch would hold it.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) At least it's one more layer of protection.

**Narrator**: Next, he jammed the other branches into the gaps in the window frames so the windows couldn't easily be pushed open from outside. When he was done, he brushed the dirt off his hands and pushed the door open to go back in.

---

**Narrator**: Just as Li Jie pushed the door open, the bathroom door opened too. A faint wisp of steam drifted out, carrying a fresh, clean scent.

**Narrator**: Asuka stepped out wrapped in a white towel, her hair still dripping. Her long, wet hair clung to her shoulders, and her cheeks were faintly flushed from the hot water.

**Narrator**: Their eyes met in midair.

**Li Jie**: (freezing, his face going hot) Uh...

**Narrator**: Li Jie quickly looked away, his heart inexplicably racing. He was twenty-eight and had never once had a girlfriend. A scene like this was far more excitement than he could handle.

**Li Jie**: (hurriedly) I... I'll go take a shower!

**Narrator**: He all but fled into the bathroom, shutting the door so clumsily that it closed with a dull bang.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (startled for a moment, whispering) Oh... Did I scare him...?

**Narrator**: She glanced down at herself, and a shy blush rose to her cheeks too.

---

**Narrator**: In the bathroom, hot water poured from the showerhead onto Li Jie's shoulders. The warm stream washed the weariness from his body, but couldn't settle the turmoil in his mind.

**Li Jie**: (thinking, taking a deep breath) Calm down... calm down... This is no time to be thinking about that.

**Narrator**: He closed his eyes and let the hot water run over his face. Little by little his thoughts drifted away from that awkward moment and turned to more important things.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Those three students... I wonder how they're doing now. Misaki took an arrow to the leg — that has to be bad. And Kenta and Takumi were hurt too...

**Narrator**: He thought of the elf-like creature Asuka had described, and of that ever-thickening fog. Wounded and running for their lives in conditions like that — their chances didn't look good.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Still... the older man, the quiet girl and the guy in glasses headed into the forest too. If they ran into each other, maybe they could help...

**Narrator**: The thought eased his mind a little. At least Misaki and the others weren't entirely alone and helpless.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) But the man in black...

**Narrator**: He remembered the blurred figure he had seen from the fifth car — the one who had left the platform alone and disappeared deep into the forest. From start to finish, that man had never said a word to anyone; he had been there like a ghost.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Who is he? Why go off on his own? And... it was like he knew exactly where to go right from the start...

**Narrator**: A creeping unease spread through him. The man in black was like an unsolved riddle, one he couldn't ignore.

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head, under his breath) Forget it. No use thinking about it. I'd better look after myself first.

**Narrator**: He turned off the water and toweled himself dry. Then, with a deep breath, he pushed open the bathroom door.

---

**Narrator**: When Li Jie came out of the bathroom, a faint aroma already filled the cabin. It was the smell of food, warm and inviting.

**Narrator**: Asuka had changed into clean clothes — who knew where she had found them; they looked as though the cabin's former owner had left them behind. She stood in the kitchen area, carefully stirring something in a clay pot.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (turning around with a smile) Mr. Li, you're out! I found some ingredients in the kitchen... They look like some kind of dried herbs and grains.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (going on) I tried making a pot of soup. I don't know if it's elf food or what, but it smells okay, I think?

**Li Jie**: (sitting down at the table) Thanks for going to the trouble.

**Narrator**: Asuka set two steaming bowls of soup on the table. The broth was a pale gold, with a few green leaves floating on top, and it gave off a fresh, gentle fragrance.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (a little nervous) I... I'm not sure how it tastes. Do you want to try it first?

**Narrator**: Li Jie brought a spoonful to his mouth. The warm broth slid down his throat with an indescribable herbal freshness and a faint sweetness. It went down surprisingly easily, a small comfort to his exhausted body.

**Li Jie**: (nodding) It's really good. Better than I expected.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (breathing a sigh of relief, smiling) Thank goodness... I was worried it'd be awful.

**Narrator**: The two of them ate their soup in silence. Outside, violet moonlight spilled through the window, laying soft light and shadow across the table. In this moment of peace, it was almost possible to forget the dangers of the world outside.

**Narrator**: Firelight played across their faces, warm and soft. Asuka stole a glance at Li Jie, a faint smile on her lips. And in that rare calm, Li Jie, too, felt a sense of ease he hadn't known in a long time.

**Narrator**: Outside, the forest was still full of danger, and an unknown journey still lay ahead. But for this one night, at least, they had found a place to shelter.

**Narrator**: Two strangers brought together by fate, sharing a warmth both fragile and precious in this little cabin in the mist.
