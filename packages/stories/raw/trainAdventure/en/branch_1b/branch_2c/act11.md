# Act 11: Warmth in the Mist

**Narrator**: After dinner the two of them stayed at the table, and little by little the mood eased. Outside, the forest still lay under that eerie violet moonlight, but the warmth inside let them forget, for a while, the dangers beyond the door.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) Mr. Li... what do you usually do? I mean, back in Tokyo.

**Li Jie**: (thinking it over) When I'm not working? Mostly video games, I guess. Some weekends I go hiking, to get out of the city and breathe a little.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (brightening) Games! What kind do you play?

**Li Jie**: (smiling) RPGs, mostly. I like the open-world ones you can explore at your own pace. Hiking's kind of the same thing, really. You walk along by yourself, nice and slow, and take in the scenery.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (with a laugh) So you're the type who likes being alone, Mr. Li. I'm the total opposite!

**Li Jie**: (curious) What about you?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (laughing) My favorite thing in the world is going shopping with friends! Especially with Misaki. We can shop from morning till night and try on clothes in every single store.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (going on, her voice softening) Misaki has really good taste. She always finds clothes that suit me. And we go to cafés together and talk, and plan where we're going on our next trip...

**Narrator**: Her face fell as she said it. Those happy memories had suddenly grown very heavy.

**Li Jie**: (softly) We'll find a way out of here. And maybe... you'll get to see her again.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking up, her eyes a little red) Do you really think... we can go back? Back to our own world?

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's thought) That train brought us here, so there has to be some kind of connection between this world and ours. If we could come, then in theory we can go back.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (in a low voice) But... we don't even know what this place is...

**Li Jie**: (firmly) That's why we're going to take a look at Silver Moon Lake. If it's marked on the map, there should be some clues there. Even if there's no way back, at least we might find a way to survive here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding, with a small smile) Mm... With you here, I feel a lot safer.

**Narrator**: Their eyes met for a moment. Asuka's cheeks went faintly pink, and she lowered her gaze to the tabletop. Something hard to name hung in the air between them.

---

**Li Jie**: (breaking the silence and getting to his feet) It's getting late. We should get some rest. We've got more walking to do tomorrow.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding, glancing at the double bed) Um... about the bed...

**Li Jie**: (quickly) You take the bed. I'll be fine on the floor.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (shaking her head, whispering) But... I'm... I'm kind of scared to sleep by myself...

**Narrator**: Her voice grew smaller and smaller, and her cheeks had gone bright red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (working up her courage) Mr. Li... could you sleep next to me? I promise I won't bother you... It's just... if I'm by myself, I'll keep thinking about all those horrible things...

**Li Jie**: (freezing, his face burning) Th-that's... um...

**Narrator**: Li Jie's mind went blank. Share a bed with a young woman he had only just met? The thought had never so much as crossed his mind. Twenty-eight years old and never once had a girlfriend, and right now his heart was pounding hard enough to burst out of his chest.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (seeing the look on his face, hurriedly) I'm sorry! I... I didn't mean it like that! I just... I just want someone next to me...

**Li Jie**: (taking a deep breath, then nodding) ...All right. But I'm on the outside. You take the side by the wall.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (smiling with relief) Thank you, Mr. Li.

---

**Narrator**: They climbed into bed and lay down on either side, carefully leaving a gap between them, as if they had drawn an invisible line down the middle.

**Narrator**: Li Jie lay stiff as a board, every muscle tense; even his breathing was careful. He could hear Asuka breathing beside him, could catch the faint scent of her.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Calm down... calm down... We're just getting some rest, that's all. Don't overthink it...

**Narrator**: But the more he told himself that, the faster his heart beat. Every nerve in his body was wound tight, and there was no relaxing at all.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (softly) Mr. Li... good night.

**Li Jie**: (his voice a little dry) Mm... good night.

**Narrator**: Silence settled over the cabin. The minutes crept by. Little by little, Asuka's breathing grew slow and even. She was asleep.

**Narrator**: But Li Jie lay there with his eyes open, staring at the ceiling, as rigid as ever and nowhere near sleep.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) She dropped off fast... And I can't sleep at all...

**Narrator**: Just then, Asuka rolled over.

**Narrator**: Her arm reached across and wrapped around him. The soft warmth of it froze Li Jie where he lay. He even stopped breathing.

**Li Jie**: (thinking, panicking) Wh... what?!

**Suzuki Asuka**: (murmuring in her sleep, in a whisper) Kenta... don't leave me...

**Narrator**: There was a sob in her voice. She held on to him tight, as if searching for something safe to cling to.

**Li Jie**: (thinking, with a sigh) ...So she's dreaming.

**Narrator**: All the tension drained out of him at once. In its place came something more complicated — a little disappointment, and a little laughing at himself.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) What was I even thinking... She just sees me as someone to lean on. And anyway... the one she's thinking about is that Kenta guy.

**Narrator**: He didn't push her arm away. He just lay there quietly. Gradually the stiffness left his body, and at last, worn out, he felt sleep come over him.

**Narrator**: In this eerie world, in this little cabin in the mist, two strangers held on to each other through the long night.

---

**Narrator**: There was no telling how long had passed when Li Jie slowly opened his eyes. His body still felt heavy and his head was foggy, as if he had not slept nearly enough.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Time to get up...? I still feel wiped out...

**Narrator**: He turned his head toward the window. The sky was the same endless violet-black, and the two crescent moons still hung there, unchanged. No sunrise, no morning light. Time seemed frozen in an endless night.

**Li Jie**: (thinking, frowning) No way to tell what time it is... My body clock's completely shot.

**Narrator**: He felt a warmth in his arms and looked down. Asuka was still fast asleep against him, her face calm and peaceful.

**Li Jie**: (carefully sliding his arm free, softly) Asuka... time to get up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (opening her eyes groggily) Mm...?

**Narrator**: The moment she realized how she was lying, she was wide awake, her face flaming red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (flustered) I... I'm sorry! While I was asleep, I...

**Li Jie**: (waving it off and looking away) It's fine. We should get ready to go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (head down, whispering) Okay...

---

**Narrator**: Once they were up, Asuka went to the kitchen and started on a simple breakfast, while Li Jie sorted through their gear, checking the map and the rusty sword.

**Narrator**: Before long, two steaming bowls of porridge sat on the table. Asuka had cooked the grain left over from last night into a thin gruel. Simple as it was, in a place like this it was a rare bit of warmth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (sitting down with her bowl) Mr. Li... last night, did I... do anything weird?

**Li Jie**: (taking a sip of porridge, calmly) No. You slept soundly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (relieved) Good... Sometimes I toss and turn in my sleep, and I talk, too...

**Li Jie**: (looking at her) Did you dream about your friends?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (taken aback, then nodding) Yeah... I dreamed we were still in Tokyo, going shopping together... just like always...

**Narrator**: Her eyes reddened, but she blinked hard and kept the tears from falling.

**Li Jie**: (softly) We'll find them. And we'll find the way home.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking up with a smile) Mm! I believe you.

**Narrator**: They finished breakfast in silence. Li Jie stood, slid the rusty sword into his belt and tucked the map into his pocket. Asuka gathered up her things and followed him.

**Li Jie**: (pushing the door open) Let's go. West, to Silver Moon Lake.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Okay!

**Narrator**: They stepped out of the cabin, and the door swung slowly shut behind them. The forest still lay under the violet moonlight, the mist drifting slowly between the trees.

**Narrator**: Ahead lay a journey into the unknown. Whether it held danger or hope, no one could say. But for now, at least, neither of them was alone.

**Narrator**: Side by side, the two young people walked deeper into the forest, toward the place called "Silver Moon Lake." Their figures slowly faded into the mist, leaving only a faint trail of footprints behind.

**Narrator**: And the little cabin in the mist fell silent once more, quietly waiting for the next lost traveler.
