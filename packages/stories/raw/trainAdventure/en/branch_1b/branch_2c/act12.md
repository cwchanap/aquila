# Act 12: The Secret of Silver Moon Lake

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka left the cabin and set out for Silver Moon Lake. The forest still lay under the violet moonlight and mist still drifted between the trees, but compared with the Misty Forest they had come through, they could see much farther here.

**Narrator**: They followed the route marked on the map, picking their way carefully through the woods. The path underfoot was rough, but not too hard going. Now and then strange sounds carried from far off, but nothing came near them.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (walking behind Li Jie, whispering) Mr. Li, what do you think Silver Moon Lake will be like?

**Li Jie**: (without looking back, sword in hand, keeping watch all around) No idea. But if it's specially marked on the map, it's probably somewhere important.

**Narrator**: Stopping and starting, they walked for about two more hours. Li Jie's legs began to ache again, and Asuka was breathing hard. But neither of them complained; they just kept going in silence.

**Narrator**: At last the trees ahead began to thin. Through the gaps between the trunks, Li Jie glimpsed open ground — and a gleam of silvery-blue light.

**Li Jie**: (stopping) We're here.

**Narrator**: They stepped out of the forest, and the sight before them stopped them both in their tracks.

**Narrator**: Silver Moon Lake lay still in the cradle of the valley like a vast mirror, perfectly reflecting the twin moons in the sky. Its water was a dreamlike silvery blue, glimmering softly in the moonlight.

**Narrator**: Glowing flowers floated on its surface, something like water lilies, giving off a faint silver light. They swayed gently on the water, lending the whole lake an air of mystery and holiness.

**Narrator**: Around the lake spread an open meadow, its grass rippling in the breeze and smelling fresh and green. A faint floral scent hung in the air, clean and soothing, and their weary bodies and minds eased at once.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (in awe) It's so beautiful... It's like something out of a fairy tale...

**Li Jie**: (just as stunned by the view) Yeah... I can't believe a place like this actually exists...

**Narrator**: They stood at the water's edge, quietly taking in the peaceful scene. After so much fear and exhaustion, the lake felt like a miracle, soothing their worn-out hearts.

**Li Jie**: (looking around) Look, there are houses over there.

**Narrator**: Following his gaze, Asuka saw several cabins scattered along the shore. They were built much like the one they had stayed in last night — plain wooden structures, but kept in fairly good repair.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (counting) There's five... six of them. Should we go take a look?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Yeah. We might find something useful.

---

**Narrator**: They walked along the shore to the nearest cabin. Li Jie pushed open the wooden door. The layout inside was almost exactly like last night's — a simple bed, a table and chairs, a kitchen and a bathroom.

**Li Jie**: (starting a careful search) Asuka, check around the kitchen and the table for any food or papers. I'll take the bed and the cupboards.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Got it!

**Narrator**: They split up. In the kitchen Asuka found some dried grain and herbs, much like what they had used last night. On the table sat a few empty bowls and some utensils, but nothing written down anywhere.

**Narrator**: Li Jie rummaged under the bed and through the cupboards, but apart from some old clothes and blankets, he found nothing out of the ordinary.

**Li Jie**: (walking out of the room) Nothing special in this one. On to the next.

**Narrator**: They searched the second cabin, then the third. It was much the same each time — all the basic necessities, but no writing of any kind and nothing unusual.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (a little disappointed) Looks like these houses are just for travelers passing through... No clues at all.

**Li Jie**: (looking toward the fourth cabin) Three left. Let's keep looking.

---

**Narrator**: The fourth cabin looked no different from the others. Li Jie still went over every corner carefully — under the bed, inside the cupboards, beneath the table, even the cracks in the walls.

**Narrator**: As he was checking a wooden cabinet against the wall, he noticed that a board at its base was loose.

**Li Jie**: (crouching down and prying at the board with his fingers) Hm?

**Narrator**: He gave it a hard wrench, and the board came free. Beneath it was a small hidden compartment, and inside lay a thin booklet.

**Li Jie**: (picking up the booklet, quietly) Found something.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (coming straight over) What is it?

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood and laid the booklet on the table. It was a small volume bound in some pale green leather, its cover engraved with intricate patterns and some lettering he couldn't make sense of.

**Li Jie**: (carefully opening it to the first page) Looks like... a book in some kind of language.

**Narrator**: The pages were densely covered in unfamiliar script, its lines graceful and flowing, like some kind of calligraphy. And yet, to Li Jie's surprise, the writing was... something he could understand.

**Li Jie**: (astonished) I... I can actually read this?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (leaning in to look as well, frowning) Huh? But I can't read any of it... What kind of writing is this?

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head, baffled) I don't know why either... But when I look at these letters, it's like I just understand what they mean...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (amazed) That's so weird... Why can you read it, Mr. Li, and I can't?

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's thought) No idea... Maybe it has something to do with how we got to this world? Let's not worry about that for now. I'll try reading what it says.

**Narrator**: He turned the pages, reading the strange script closely. The further he read, the more serious his expression became.

**Li Jie**: (reading aloud in a low voice) "Five thousand years ago, fleeing the flames of war upon the mainland, the forebears of the elves crossed the sea by ship and came to this island, cut off from all the world..."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her eyes lighting up) The elves? So... there really are elves here?

**Li Jie**: (reading on) "They named the island 'the Jade Isle,' and made their home in the forests of its north. That forest teemed with life and was steeped in magic..."

**Li Jie**: (turning the page) "In the very depths of the forest grows a sacred ancient tree — the Tree of Life. It is the core of the whole forest, and the barrier that guards the elven people against all invaders..."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) A sacred ancient tree... It's like something out of a fantasy novel...

**Li Jie**: (nodding, reading on) "The forebears named this forest 'the Eternal Forest.' Under the shelter of the Tree of Life, three principal villages came, in time, to be founded..."

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned to the next page, which held a simple map. At its center was a drawing of an enormous tree, with three small village markers scattered around it.

**Li Jie**: (pointing at the map) Three villages... The first is called 'Dawnlight Court,' in the south of the forest. Its leader is an elf named Orion...

**Li Jie**: (continuing) The second is called 'Moonlight Glade,' in the north of the forest. Its leader is Ailaru...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (curious) And the third?

**Li Jie**: (looking at the third marker on the map) The third is called 'the Vale of Stars,' in the middle of the forest. Its leader is Nisara...

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked up, out of the window at the quiet lake. In his mind's eye rose that mysterious forest — the Eternal Forest — and the sacred ancient tree that watched over the elves.

**Li Jie**: (quietly) The elves... three villages... and a sacred ancient tree...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nervously) S-so... have we wandered into elf territory? If they find us...

**Li Jie**: (silent for a moment) Maybe. But if this booklet was hidden here, it probably means this isn't somewhere the elves come very often.

**Li Jie**: (leafing on) Wait... there's a lot more after this. Whatever this booklet records could be important.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking at the booklet, a little anxious) But I can't read it... Mr. Li, can you tell me what it says?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Of course. But... there's a lot in here. I'll need time to go through it properly.

**Narrator**: He glanced out of the window. The sky was the same endless violet-black. In this world, time had gone hazy; there was no telling whether it was day or night.

**Li Jie**: (standing up) Let's finish checking the rest of the houses first. Then we'll find somewhere by the lake to rest, and I'll read this properly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) All right. Maybe there's something in there about how to get out of here.

**Narrator**: They left the fourth cabin and headed for the remaining two. They quickly checked the fifth and the sixth, but found nothing else of note.

**Narrator**: They left the fourth cabin and headed for the remaining two. The fifth was built much like the others, and turned up nothing special.

**Narrator**: When they reached the sixth and last cabin, Li Jie went over every corner as carefully as ever. In the drawer of a wooden cabinet, he found a folded sheet of parchment.

**Li Jie**: (picking up the parchment) Is this... a map?

**Narrator**: He unfolded it. It was covered in intricate lines and markings. But his mind was still on the elven booklet, and he gave it only a hurried glance.

**Li Jie**: (putting the map away) We'll take it with us. I'll look at it properly later.

**Narrator**: He put the map in his pocket along with the elven booklet, and they left the last cabin.

**Narrator**: With every cabin searched, they made their way down to the water. Silver Moon Lake was as still as a mirror, the glowing flowers swaying gently on its surface, shedding their soft light.

**Li Jie**: (pointing to a patch of grass by the shore) There are a few trees over there we can lean against. Let's rest there for a bit.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Mm.

**Narrator**: They went over to the grass by the lake and sat down. Li Jie leaned back against a tree and opened the elven booklet across his knees. Asuka sat beside him, her eyes fixed on the writing she couldn't read.

**Li Jie**: (taking a deep breath) Okay, I'll keep reading. Listen closely — I'll translate it for you as I go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding earnestly) Okay!

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned back to the page where he had left off and went on reading the mysterious booklet that held the history of the elves. The light from the lake played across their faces, and a soft breeze stirred around them.

**Narrator**: And within that little booklet, more secrets — about this world, about the elves, and perhaps about how they might leave this place — lay waiting to be uncovered.
