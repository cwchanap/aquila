# Act 13: What the Booklet Revealed

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat on the grass by the lake with the elven booklet open across his knees. Asuka sat beside him, quietly waiting for him to translate. The surface of Silver Moon Lake mirrored the violet moonlight, and a soft breeze blew.

**Li Jie**: (turning to the next page) This next part... looks like it's about the elves' everyday lives.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (curious) Their everyday lives?

**Li Jie**: (nodding, skimming) Yeah. Like what they eat... "forest fruit," "moonlight grass," "silver-dew mushrooms"... They all sound like something out of a dream.

**Narrator**: He kept turning pages. They described all manner of elven customs — how they brewed their drinks, how they wove their clothes, how they built their homes.

**Li Jie**: (reading on) There are festivals, too... "the Springbirth Festival," "the Moonglow Festival," "the Night of Stars"... Looks like the elves care a lot about nature, and about the stars and the moon.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (smiling) That sounds so lovely. Like living in a fairy tale.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned a few more pages, which held still more about the elves' daily lives — their wedding ceremonies, their coming-of-age rites, and the ways they celebrated all their traditional festivals.

**Li Jie**: (to himself) This is interesting and all, but it probably won't do us much good...

**Narrator**: He flipped quickly through the pages, looking for something more useful. At last, something on one page made him stop.

**Li Jie**: (his eyes lighting up) Wait... there's something important here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (leaning in at once) What?

**Li Jie**: (reading intently) "This book was set down in the year 5000 of the Elven Calendar..."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (puzzled) The year 5000 of the Elven Calendar?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) It must mean five thousand years since they came to this island. The Elven Calendar starts from the year they landed here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (surprised) Five thousand years... So this book must be really old, right?

**Li Jie**: (reading on) That's not all. It also says... "Our people are long of life; a few among our kin may live as long as ten thousand years. Short-lived humans, by contrast, are granted but a hundred..."

**Narrator**: There was a note of shock in Li Jie's voice as he read the passage aloud.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (eyes wide) Ten thousand years?! Elves can live ten thousand years?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) That's what it says here. Compared with that, humans only get about a hundred... To them, our lives are probably about as brief as a mayfly's.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (in a low voice) No wonder they can keep such ancient history... The elf who wrote this book might even still be alive...

**Narrator**: The thought struck them both as almost too strange to believe. Li Jie took a deep breath and read on.

**Li Jie**: (his voice turning serious) And... it mentions a place here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nervously) What place?

**Li Jie**: (reading aloud) "At the southern tip of the Jade Isle there stands a gate that leads to another world. In the sky of that world hang the twin violet moons, and it lies shrouded in eternal darkness..."

**Narrator**: At that, Asuka and Li Jie both looked up at the same moment, and their eyes met.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (stunned) The twin violet moons... eternal darkness... Isn't that...

**Li Jie**: (nodding, his voice low) It's where we are right now.

**Narrator**: They both looked up at the sky again. The two violet crescent moons still hung against the black heavens, gazing coldly down on the land.

**Li Jie**: (reading on, more gravely still) "This other world is forbidden ground to our people. Whosoever leaves the Jade Isle and passes beyond the gate shall lose the protection of the sacred ancient tree..."

**Li Jie**: (pausing to draw a deep breath) "That land is a deadly poison to the elven people; all who set foot there perish in an instant. Thus our people forbid any to enter the other world..."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (gasping) They die instantly?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) That's what it says. That world... the place we're in now, I mean. It's lethal to elves.

**Narrator**: Li Jie kept turning pages, hoping for more about this other world. At last, a few pages further on, he found another important passage.

**Li Jie**: (frowning) There's some history here, too...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nervously) What kind of history?

**Li Jie**: (reading aloud) "In the year 4000 of the Elven Calendar, Ailaru, leader of Moonlight Glade, committed a grave crime and was banished from the Eternal Forest..."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (surprised) Ailaru... the leader of Moonlight Glade? He was banished?

**Li Jie**: (nodding, reading on) "Some of the folk of Moonlight Glade chose to follow Ailaru into exile. The rest chose Elysia as their new leader and kept watch over the village..."

**Narrator**: Li Jie stopped and looked up at the distant forest, quickly sorting through everything he had learned.

**Li Jie**: (muttering to himself) The year 5000 of the Elven Calendar... Ailaru banished in 4000... So that happened a thousand years ago?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (puzzled) But... don't elves live ten thousand years? Then maybe Ailaru is still alive?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Could be. If he really is still alive, he's been in exile for a thousand years...

**Narrator**: Li Jie leafed on, but most of what followed was about rituals and traditions, with nothing more of real importance. He closed the booklet and sank into thought.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) Mr. Li, what are you thinking about?

**Li Jie**: (frowning) I'm thinking... about the contradictions in all this.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (puzzled) Contradictions?

**Li Jie**: (looking at her) Remember what you told me? You and your friends were attacked in the forest, and Misaki was hit by an arrow...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding, her voice trembling) Mm... I'll never forget it...

**Li Jie**: (in a low, steady voice) You said you started running when you heard the screaming. But before that, Kenta and the others must have seen whoever attacked you, right?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (thinking back) I... I guess so. I remember when Kenta yelled "Run!", he sounded really terrified...

**Li Jie**: (reasoning it through) Logically, something in the forest that shoots arrows and attacks with a sword... is most likely an elf.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Right... That's what I thought, too, back then. Whatever attacked us must have been an elf...

**Li Jie**: (frowning harder) But that's exactly the problem. According to this booklet, this world is deadly to elves. Once they leave the Jade Isle and enter this world of twin violet moons and eternal darkness, they die on the spot.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (as it dawns on her) That's right... So... so why would there be elves here attacking us?

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head) I don't get it either. There are a few possibilities...

**Narrator**: He raised his eyes to the deep, dark forest in the distance — the place called "the Misty Forest."

**Li Jie**: (slowly) One: what's written in this booklet is wrong. Maybe this place isn't as deadly as all that, or the elves found some way to survive here.

**Li Jie**: (pausing) Two: whatever attacked you wasn't an elf at all, but some other creature that only looks like one.

**Li Jie**: (his tone growing heavy) And there's a third possibility... The elves really can't survive here, but they came anyway. Maybe... they had some reason they had to come, even if it meant dying.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (frightened) A reason they had to come... What could it be?

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head) I don't know. But whichever it is, it means this world is more complicated than we thought.

**Narrator**: They fell silent. Silver Moon Lake was still as a mirror, the glowing flowers swaying gently on the water. But now, somehow, the stillness felt eerie.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) So... what do we do now?

**Li Jie**: (looking at the booklet in his hands, thinking) This booklet says there's a gate at the southern tip of the Jade Isle that leads to this other world... to the place we're in now.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (brightening) Then could we go through that gate to the Jade Isle?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) In theory, I think so. If elves can come over from that side, the gate should work both ways.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (excited) Th-then if we go to the Jade Isle, does that mean we can go home?

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head, cautious) No, it's not that simple. The Jade Isle isn't our world. It's elf territory — somewhere completely different from Earth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (deflating) Oh... right...

**Li Jie**: (thinking it through) But... the Jade Isle might give us some clues. The elves have lived here for five thousand years. They must know far more about this world than we do.

**Li Jie**: (looking at Asuka) If we can get to the Jade Isle, we might learn more about this world. Maybe... we'll even find a way back to ours.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her hope rekindled) Yes! The elves are so ancient, they must know all kinds of things!

**Li Jie**: (nodding, though still cautious) The problem is... right now we're not even sure where we are, let alone exactly where that gate is. All we know is that it's at the southern tip of the Jade Isle...

**Narrator**: He stood and carefully tucked the booklet into his pocket. Asuka got to her feet as well.

**Li Jie**: (looking out over the lake) At least now we know a few key things. This world is connected to the Jade Isle, and the elves know things about this place...

**Narrator**: They stood side by side at the water's edge, gazing out over the mysterious silver lake. In the distance, the violet moonlight lay across the water, glimmering with an eerie beauty.

**Narrator**: And here, on the peaceful shore of Silver Moon Lake, a mystery of elves and other worlds, of survival and death, was only just beginning to surface.
