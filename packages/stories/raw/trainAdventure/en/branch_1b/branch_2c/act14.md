# Act 14: Where the Map Points

**Narrator**: Just then, something occurred to Li Jie. He reached into his pocket.

**Li Jie**: (suddenly remembering) Oh, right! We found a map in that last house. I was so caught up in the booklet I nearly forgot.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (delighted) A map? Quick, let's see it!

**Narrator**: Li Jie pulled the folded parchment from his pocket and carefully spread it out on the grass. He also took out the old map he had found earlier at the City of the Netherworld station.

**Li Jie**: (laying the two maps side by side) Let me compare these two...

**Narrator**: The two maps were drawn in a similar style, both labeled in Elvish script. The old map showed a few main routes leading out from the City of the Netherworld, while the new one was far more detailed.

**Li Jie**: (comparing them closely) This new map... covers a much wider area. Looks like Silver Moon Lake is right around the middle.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (leaning in) What does it say?

**Li Jie**: (pointing to the north of the map) To the north... there's a place called "the Altar of the End."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (frowning) The Altar of the End? That sounds terrifying...

**Li Jie**: (nodding, then pointing to the south) And to the south... it says...

**Narrator**: His voice suddenly broke off. His eyes were fixed on the writing on the map.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (curious) What is it?

**Li Jie**: (sounding surprised) Down south... it's labeled "Moonlight Glade."

**Suzuki Asuka**: (taken aback) Moonlight Glade... That name...

**Li Jie**: (picking up the elven booklet and turning to the page about the three villages) It's the one from the booklet! One of the three great elven villages — Moonlight Glade, in the north of the Eternal Forest!

**Suzuki Asuka**: (shocked) Th-then... Moonlight Glade is south of us?

**Li Jie**: (thinking hard) If the map's right... it should be. But the thing is...

**Narrator**: He studied both maps closely, searching for more clues. But neither one marked where the Eternal Forest was, or the gate to the Jade Isle.

**Li Jie**: (frowning) That's strange... This map doesn't show the Eternal Forest, and there's nothing about the gate to the Jade Isle.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (puzzled) Why would that be?

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Maybe... maybe this map only shows part of this other world. The Eternal Forest and the gate could be much farther away.

**Li Jie**: (looking at "Moonlight Glade" on the map) But... if this Moonlight Glade really is the elf village from the booklet...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (finishing his thought) Then that means elves really can survive in this world?

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Or... this "Moonlight Glade" just shares its name with the one in the booklet, and it's somewhere else entirely. After all, we don't know how big an area this map actually covers.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went over the map once more, looking for anything else, but apart from "the Altar of the End" and "Moonlight Glade," it marked no other landmarks of note.

**Li Jie**: (sighing) Looks like this map doesn't tell us much. We know north leads to the Altar of the End and south leads to Moonlight Glade, but...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) But we don't know which way is the right one...

**Li Jie**: (looking at her) Still, at least we've got two options now. If we head south to Moonlight Glade, maybe we can find out whether it really is an elf village.

**Li Jie**: (continuing) And if we go north to the Altar of the End... I don't know what that place is, but if it's marked on the map, it must matter somehow.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (worried) So how do we choose?

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's thought) Moonlight Glade... if it really is an elf village, we might find some answers there. The booklet says Ailaru was banished, but some of his people left with him.

**Li Jie**: (looking at the map) If this Moonlight Glade is a new village that Ailaru and his followers built... that would explain it. They left the Eternal Forest and came to this other world.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (struck by a thought) But the booklet said this world is deadly to elves...

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Right, that's where it doesn't add up. Maybe... maybe they found some way to survive here? Or...

**Narrator**: He paused, his brow furrowing deeper.

**Li Jie**: (in a low voice) Or there aren't any living elves left there at all... and it's just an abandoned village.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (frightened) Th-then... are we going?

**Li Jie**: (staring at the map, sinking into long thought) I need to think this through...

**Narrator**: The two of them sat quietly by the lake. Li Jie stared at the map, turning possibility after possibility over in his head. Asuka gazed out at the water, waiting for him to decide.

**Narrator**: The surface of Silver Moon Lake was still as a mirror, the glowing flowers swaying gently on the water. The violet moonlight fell over the two of them, casting long shadows across the grass.

**Narrator**: The Altar of the End to the north, Moonlight Glade to the south — two very different roads lay before them. And either one might lead to unknown danger, or to the hope of going home.

**Narrator**: The moment of choice was close at hand.
