# Act 15: An Idle Day by the Lake

**Li Jie**: (looking over the two maps, taking a deep breath) I've decided. We'll go to Moonlight Glade first.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Mm, I think that's the better choice too. At least we know it might be an elf village...

**Li Jie**: (putting the maps away) Right. Compared with the Altar of the End, which could be anything at all, Moonlight Glade at least has what's in the booklet to go on.

**Li Jie**: (looking up at the sky) And if there really are elves there... maybe we can ask them about this world.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (a little worried) But... the booklet says elves can't survive here...

**Li Jie**: (nodding) That's why we're going to find out. Whether the booklet's wrong, or there's some other reason. Either way, it's the best choice we've got right now.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (determined) Then I'm coming with you!

**Li Jie**: (smiling) Of course. But...

**Narrator**: He looked around at the calm, lovely expanse of Silver Moon Lake, then back at the route marked on the map.

**Li Jie**: (turning to Asuka) There's no need to rush off right away. According to the map, Moonlight Glade is a fair way from here, and we don't know what we'll run into on the road.

**Li Jie**: (continuing) Why don't we take a proper rest here first and get our strength back? We can set out tomorrow.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (brightening) Yes, let's! Actually... I wanted to enjoy the view here a little longer, too. You hardly ever see a place this beautiful...

**Narrator**: Their eyes turned to Silver Moon Lake together. The glowing flowers on its surface shimmered dreamily in the violet moonlight. The water was as clear as a mirror, perfectly reflecting the twin moons in the sky.

**Li Jie**: (softly) It really is... the most beautiful place I've ever seen.

**Narrator**: They sat back down on the grass, setting their heavy thoughts aside for a while. For now, all they wanted was to enjoy this rare stillness.

---

**Narrator**: Under this endless night, time blurred. They had no idea how long they sat there, quietly taking in the view across the lake.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (suddenly jumping to her feet) Mr. Li, let's go for a walk by the water!

**Li Jie**: (startled by her energy) Uh... sure.

**Narrator**: Asuka grabbed his hand and ran excitedly toward the shore. Li Jie, thrown by how suddenly she had taken his hand, didn't quite know what to do with himself, but he followed her down to the water all the same.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (crouching at the water's edge and lightly touching the surface) Wow... the water's so cold... but it feels nice!

**Narrator**: Her fingers skimmed the surface, sending out ring after ring of ripples. The glowing flowers bobbed gently on the little waves, shining more dreamily than ever.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking up at Li Jie, smiling) You try it too, Mr. Li!

**Li Jie**: (crouching down as well and dipping his hand into the water) Mm... it really is cold. But in a night that never ends, a chill like this actually helps clear your head.

**Narrator**: All of a sudden Asuka slapped the water with her palm. Spray flew up, and a few drops landed on Li Jie's face.

**Li Jie**: (taken aback) Hey...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (laughing) Sorry, sorry! I just... suddenly felt like doing it!

**Narrator**: Her smile was so radiant, as if for the moment she had forgotten all her fear and unease. Watching her laughing face, Li Jie felt the weight in his own chest grow lighter without his even noticing.

**Li Jie**: (with a resigned laugh) Honestly, you...

**Narrator**: He gave the water a light slap of his own, sending a small splash toward Asuka. She shrieked, and laughed even harder.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (laughing) So you can joke around too, Mr. Li! I thought you were serious all the time!

**Li Jie**: (wiping the water off his face) I'm just... not used to messing around like this.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (standing up and shaking the water off her hands) Then get used to it! Being wound up so tight all the time must be exhausting.

**Narrator**: She held out her hand and pulled him to his feet. They strolled slowly along the shore, the grass soft and comfortable underfoot.

---

**Narrator**: After walking a while, they sat down on a flat rock. The view was wider here; they could see the whole of Silver Moon Lake.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (softly) Mr. Li... do you think we really can go back?

**Narrator**: The mood suddenly grew heavier. Li Jie was silent for a moment.

**Li Jie**: (seriously) I don't know. But I'll do everything I can to find a way back.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking at him) You won't leave me on my own, will you?

**Li Jie**: (shaking his head) No. We've come this far together, so I'll stay with you, until we find our way home.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes reddened, but she blinked hard and kept the tears from falling.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (smiling) Thank you, Mr. Li. With you here... I feel a lot safer.

**Narrator**: She leaned gently against his shoulder. Li Jie went rigid, but he didn't push her away; he simply let her lean on him.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) This feeling... I'm not used to it, but... it's not bad.

**Narrator**: They sat there quietly like that, gazing at the lake before them. The glow of Silver Moon Lake lit their faces, gentle and serene.

**Narrator**: In a world full of danger and the unknown, they had found a temporary refuge. Here on the silver shore, time itself seemed to stand still.

---

**Narrator**: There was no knowing how much later it was when tiredness caught up with them both.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (yawning) So sleepy...

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Time to rest. Let's go back to the cabins and sleep. We've got a long walk ahead of us tomorrow.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (getting up) Mm, sleeping outside is way too dangerous.

**Narrator**: They walked back along the shore to the cabins. Li Jie chose the one where they had found the elven booklet — it was the most familiar, and they already knew it was safe.

**Narrator**: Once inside, Li Jie checked the doors and windows again, making sure they were all shut tight. Asuka poured two cups of water in the kitchen.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (handing Li Jie a cup of water) Here.

**Li Jie**: (taking it and drinking) Thanks.

**Narrator**: They both looked at the room's one double bed. A faint awkwardness hung in the air.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (cheeks a little pink) Um... so we...

**Li Jie**: (quickly) You take the bed. I'll be fine on the floor.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (shaking her head, whispering) But... couldn't we just share, like last night? I'm... I'm still kind of scared to sleep by myself...

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's silence, nodding) ...All right. Then I'll take the outside.

**Narrator**: They lay down on either side of the bed, carefully leaving a gap between them. The violet moonlight from the window cast soft light and shadow across the room.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (softly) Mr. Li... I had a really fun day. Thanks for keeping me company.

**Li Jie**: (gazing at the ceiling) ...Me too. I haven't relaxed like this in a long time.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (smiling) I'm glad. Good night, Mr. Li.

**Li Jie**: (softly) Good night.

**Narrator**: Asuka was soon asleep, her breathing slow and even. Li Jie lay staring at the ceiling, thinking about the journey ahead.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Moonlight Glade... I hope we find the answers we need there.

**Narrator**: Little by little, weariness won out over his thoughts. Li Jie closed his eyes too, and in the stillness of the cabin he sank into a deep sleep.

---

**Narrator**: When Li Jie opened his eyes again, the sky was the same endless violet-black. He had no way of telling how long it had been; he only knew his body felt rested.

**Li Jie**: (sitting up and rubbing his eyes) Time to get up...

**Narrator**: He turned to look at Asuka. She was still fast asleep, her face peaceful. Li Jie didn't wake her right away. Instead he slipped quietly out of bed and went to the window.

**Narrator**: Through the window he could see Silver Moon Lake, as calm and beautiful as the day before. The glowing flowers still swayed gently on the water, as if dancing an endless dance.

**Li Jie**: (softly) I really don't want to leave this place...

**Narrator**: But he knew they couldn't stay here forever. An unknown journey still lay ahead, and the hope of home was still out there, waiting for them to find it.

**Li Jie**: (going back to the bed and gently shaking Asuka awake) Asuka, time to get up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (opening her eyes groggily) Mm... is it morning already...?

**Li Jie**: (with a wry smile) There are no mornings here. But it's time we headed out.

**Narrator**: Asuka sat up and rubbed her eyes. She went to the window and looked out at the lake, a hint of reluctance on her face.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (softly) I wish we could stay here forever...

**Li Jie**: (standing behind her) Once we've found the way home, maybe we can come back and visit.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (turning around, smiling) Mm! It's a promise, then!

**Narrator**: They tidied up a little. Asuka found some dry rations in the kitchen and packed them into a small bag, while Li Jie took out the map to check their direction.

**Li Jie**: (pointing at the map) We head south from here. It shouldn't be too far, but we need to be careful.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding) Got it!

**Narrator**: Li Jie slid the rusty sword into his belt, and Asuka shouldered the small bag of food and water. They took one last look around the cabin that had sheltered them.

**Li Jie**: (heading for the door) Let's go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (following) Okay!

**Narrator**: They pushed open the door and stepped outside. A morning breeze brushed past them — though there was no true morning breeze here, its coolness still cleared their heads.

**Narrator**: They took one last look at beautiful Silver Moon Lake.

**Narrator**: Then they set out on the road to Moonlight Glade. Behind them, Silver Moon Lake lay as tranquil as ever, its glowing flowers still swaying gently on the water.

**Narrator**: And ahead lay the unknown forest, the mysterious village called "Moonlight Glade," and the answers waiting for them there — or the danger.

**Narrator**: Side by side, the two young people walked south, their figures slowly disappearing at the edge of the forest. On the shore of Silver Moon Lake, only a faint trail of footprints remained, bearing witness that they had once stopped there.

**Narrator**: A new journey had begun.
