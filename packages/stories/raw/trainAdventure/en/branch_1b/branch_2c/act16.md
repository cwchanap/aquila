# Act 16: The Bloodstained Village

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka left Silver Moon Lake behind and headed south, following the route marked on the map.

**Narrator**: The forest still lay under violet moonlight, but it was nothing like the Misty Forest they had come through before — there was no fog here, and the trees grew even thicker.

**Narrator**: The path twisted and wound. Here they had to skirt a fallen giant of a tree; there they had to scramble up a steep slope.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went first to clear the way, glancing back every so often to make sure Asuka was keeping up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... how much farther is it?

**Li Jie**: We should be almost there. Hang in there a little longer.

**Narrator**: They went on like that, walking and resting by turns, for about another four hours.

**Narrator**: At last the trees ahead began to thin, and through the gaps between the trunks they saw a soft glow.

**Li Jie**: We're here.

**Narrator**: The two of them stepped out of the forest, and the sight before them took their breath away.

---

**Narrator**: It was a tranquil village cradled in a ring of valley walls, like a hidden paradise beyond the reach of the world.

**Narrator**: Its buildings were like nothing they had ever seen — white rock and massive ancient trees twined together into dwellings that seemed to have grown out of the earth on their own.

**Narrator**: Some houses even hung among the boughs of the great trees, joined to the ground by stairways woven from vines.

**Narrator**: Most breathtaking of all were the softly glowing plants, dotted among the buildings like lanterns.

**Narrator**: A clear stream wound through the heart of the village, its rippling surface shimmering with the reflections of the glowing plants.

**Narrator**: The whole village was wrapped in a dreamlike silver-blue haze of light.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's beautiful... It really is an elf village...

**Li Jie**: Looks like it. Let's be careful.

**Narrator**: But as they drew closer, they sensed something strange in the air.

**Narrator**: The elves coming and going still moved with grace, but their faces were heavy with sorrow.

**Narrator**: There was no laughter in the village — only low, murmured voices and, now and then, the sound of someone sobbing.

**Narrator**: Many of the elves wore plain white robes, as though in mourning.

**Li Jie**: Something's wrong here...

---

**Narrator**: Just then, a young elf woman noticed them.

**Narrator**: Her pale gold hair fell to her waist, the long points of her ears showing through the strands.

**Narrator**: Her eyes were a pale blue, and there were fresh traces of tears around them.

**Narrator**: She stopped and looked at the two unfamiliar human visitors, a weary smile touching her face.

**Narrator**: The moment Asuka saw her, she went rigid.

**Narrator**: That long pale gold hair... those pale blue eyes... that tall, slender frame...

**Narrator**: An image flashed through her mind — back in the thick fog, as she ran for her life, she had glanced back, just for an instant.

**Narrator**: Through the blur she had seen a figure with pale gold hair, a bow in hand — and Misaki lying on the ground...

**Narrator**: Asuka's breathing quickened, and her fingers clutched at Li Jie's sleeve.

**Lila**: Are you two... travelers from the outside?

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand a word of the elf's language. All she could do was stand close at Li Jie's side.

**Li Jie**: We are. My name is Li Jie, and this is Asuka. May I ask... is this Moonlight Glade?

**Lila**: Yes, this is Moonlight Glade. My name is Lila. You are welcome here... though I'm afraid this is not a good time...

**Narrator**: Asuka's hands were shaking. She leaned in close to Li Jie's ear, her voice so low it was barely audible.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... it's her...

**Li Jie**: What?

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's this elf... She's the one who shot Misaki... I remember her face...

**Narrator**: Li Jie stiffened slightly and stole a glance at Lila.

**Narrator**: The elf girl's face was streaked with tears, her eyes full of grief.

**Narrator**: All around them the elves wore plain white robes, and the air was thick with mourning.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and gave the back of Asuka's hand a gentle pat, a signal to stay calm for now.

**Li Jie**: (quietly, to Asuka) This place has just been attacked. The village is mourning its dead...

**Li Jie**: Now's not the time to settle that. Let's find out what's going on first.

**Narrator**: Asuka bit her lip, her eyes reddening, but she nodded.

**Li Jie**: (turning to Lila) Has something happened? Everyone seems so sad...

---

**Narrator**: Lila's eyes reddened again. She drew a deep breath, struggling to keep her composure.

**Lila**: Our village... was attacked, only a few hours ago.

**Lila**: Many of our people were wounded in the fighting... and some... have left us forever...

**Narrator**: Asuka stood to one side. She couldn't understand Lila's words, but she could feel the grief in her voice.

**Li Jie**: Attacked? Who attacked you?

**Lila**: A young man with a black sword... His power was terrifying.

**Lila**: Our swordsmen... could not stand against him at all...

**Narrator**: Li Jie's heart sank like a stone, and the figure of the man in black rose in his mind.

**Li Jie**: A black sword... a young man...

**Lila**: Our leader, Lord Ailaru... to protect the village, to protect us... he gave his life...

**Narrator**: Li Jie's heart dropped.

**Narrator**: Ailaru — the elf leader the booklet said had been banished, the very one he had come here looking to for answers — was dead.

**Li Jie**: Ailaru... he's...

**Lila**: We are holding a funeral for our fallen, and... for a human as well.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru and the other brave warriors will all be laid to rest today...

**Li Jie**: (turning to Asuka) She says a human died in the attack too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: A human? There are humans here?

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) That human... when did he come here?

**Lila**: About two hours ago, I believe.

**Lila**: When he reached the village, he said his companions had been hurt and needed our help.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru agreed at once to help him, and we were just preparing to send some of our people back with him...

**Lila**: It was then... that the young man with the black sword appeared.

**Narrator**: In an instant, the passengers who had ridden the train with him flashed through Li Jie's mind.

**Narrator**: The steady older man. The timid guy in glasses. The quiet girl.

**Narrator**: And the university students who had gotten off at the very first stop — Kenta, Takumi, and the other two girls.

**Li Jie**: That human... how did he get here?

**Lila**: We... we don't know either. He had no time to tell us much.

**Lila**: He said only that his companions were hurt and needed help... and then the attack came.

**Li Jie**: And then?

**Lila**: He... he fell on our swordsmen like a madman.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru and the human fought with all they had to protect us... but in the end...

**Narrator**: Lila's voice broke, and the tears came again.

**Narrator**: Asuka watched Lila weep. She couldn't understand her language, but she could feel her pain.

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) Could you... take us to the funeral?

**Li Jie**: I'd like... I'd like to see this human. I might know him.

**Lila**: Of course. The funeral is in the square at the center of the village. Please, follow me.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) Come on. She's taking us to the funeral.

---

**Narrator**: Lila turned and led the two of them along the village paths toward its center.

**Narrator**: The elves they passed watched them with mournful eyes. Some held glowing flowers cupped in their hands.

**Narrator**: Asuka noticed the elves murmuring among themselves. She couldn't follow a word, but she could feel how heavy the air around them was.

**Narrator**: Soon they came out into a wide, open square.

**Narrator**: In the middle of the square lay several bodies draped in white cloth.

**Narrator**: Dozens of elves stood around them in silence, some of them murmuring prayers.

**Lila**: The human... is over there.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and stepped forward.

**Narrator**: His hand trembling, he gently lifted the white cloth from the larger of the bodies.

---

**Narrator**: It was a face he knew — the middle-aged man who had tried to calm everyone down in the train car.

**Narrator**: His expression was still peaceful, as if he were only asleep.

**Narrator**: But the fatal wound across his chest told plainly what had become of him.

**Li Jie**: It's him... the older man...

**Suzuki Asuka**: He... he's the one who got off at the station...

**Li Jie**: Yeah... He should have been with the other passengers...

**Li Jie**: So why did he come here all alone?

**Narrator**: He carefully drew the white cloth back over the body and stepped back a few paces.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes were red. Li Jie's fists were clenched tight.

**Li Jie**: He was just an ordinary person, same as us. All he wanted was to survive, to get home...

**Li Jie**: And this is where he lost his life.

**Lila**: He was very brave. When the attack came, he did not run.

**Lila**: He fought beside Lord Ailaru, trying to protect us...

**Lila**: Did you... know him?

**Li Jie**: In a way. We rode the same train...

**Li Jie**: I just never thought we'd meet again like this.

**Narrator**: Lila nodded, her eyes full of sorrow and respect.

---

**Narrator**: The elves in the square began to sing, low and slow — the requiem of the elven people.

**Narrator**: The song was like a breeze, tender and sorrowful, echoing over the village.

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand the words, but the sorrow in the melody was unmistakable.

**Narrator**: Before she knew it, tears were running down her face.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka stood quietly to one side, looking at the white-draped bodies.

**Narrator**: The twin moons still hung coldly indifferent in the sky, spilling their silver-blue light over this grieving land.

**Narrator**: They had thought that in Moonlight Glade they would find answers — find Ailaru, and learn more of this world's secrets.

**Narrator**: But all they had found was death and grief.

**Narrator**: And the elf leader who held the key to it all would never answer their questions now.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gazed at the distant forest, his mind full of questions.

**Narrator**: The attacker with the black sword... who was he? Why would he strike at a peaceful village like this?

**Narrator**: And the other passengers who had gotten off with the older man... where were they now?

**Narrator**: Mystery upon mystery, and in that silver-blue light, each one only grew deeper.
