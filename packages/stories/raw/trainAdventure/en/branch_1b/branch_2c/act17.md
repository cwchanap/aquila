# Act 17: Ailaru's Plan

**Narrator**: When the funeral was over, the elves slowly drifted away.

**Narrator**: The silver-blue light still lay over the village, but the grief in the air had only grown heavier.

**Narrator**: Li Jie watched the elves leave the square, then turned and walked over to Lila.

**Li Jie**: Lila, could I talk with you in private?

**Narrator**: Lila froze for a moment, a flicker of unease in her eyes, but she soon nodded.

**Lila**: All right... Please, come with me.

**Narrator**: She led Li Jie and Asuka down a narrow path toward the edge of the village.

**Narrator**: They came to a clearing ringed by giant trees. It was quiet here, with no other elves around.

**Narrator**: In the distance the stream murmured softly, and the glowing plants swayed all around them.

**Lila**: No one will disturb us here. What did you wish to ask?

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and looked Lila in the eye.

**Li Jie**: In the forest — you were the one who shot Asuka's friend, weren't you?

---

**Narrator**: Lila flinched, but she didn't look away from his gaze.

**Narrator**: She was silent for a moment, then slowly nodded.

**Lila**: Yes... It was me.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned to Asuka and translated in a low voice.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) She admitted it... She's the one who shot Misaki.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Why? Why did she attack us?

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice was full of anger and anguish, and the tears welled up again.

**Narrator**: She demanded answers in Japanese, but Lila only looked at her in confusion.

**Li Jie**: (relaying to Lila in Elvish) She's asking why you attacked them. They hadn't done anything.

**Li Jie**: Her friends were just ordinary students. All they wanted was to survive.

**Lila**: I know... I am so sorry. But I... I was acting on Lord Ailaru's orders.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) She says... she was acting on Ailaru's orders.

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) Ailaru's orders?

**Lila**: Yes. Lord Ailaru sent me to patrol the forest, and he told me...

**Lila**: that if I came across humans from the station, I was to use force to drive them apart.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru said... it was only to frighten you, to make you scatter.

**Lila**: He was very clear that no one was to be killed. You were only to be made afraid...

---

**Li Jie**: Why? Why do that?

**Lila**: I... I don't know.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru never explained why.

**Lila**: He said only that he already knew passengers from the train would be coming into this forest.

**Lila**: And he ordered me to find a way to break you apart — to send you fleeing in different directions, so that you would not band together in a large group.

**Li Jie**: How could he have known the train's passengers would come?

**Lila**: I don't know... Lord Ailaru never explains his plans in detail.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... what did she say?

**Li Jie**: (turning to Asuka) Ailaru knew all along that passengers from the train would come.

**Li Jie**: He ordered Lila to split us up, so we wouldn't form a big group.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And what about Misaki? She was hit by an arrow! What's happened to her?

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice shook with anger and worry.

**Li Jie**: (asking Lila) Where is her friend now?

**Lila**: I... I am so sorry. My arrow did strike her.

**Lila**: But Lord Ailaru promised me that someone would go to help any who were hurt.

**Lila**: Lord Ailaru never lies. If he said someone would go to save her, then someone will have gone.

---

**Li Jie**: (translating for Asuka) She says Ailaru promised to send someone to save Misaki.

**Suzuki Asuka**: How can she believe something like that? Misaki could already be dead!

**Narrator**: Asuka said it furiously, in Japanese. Lila couldn't understand, but she could feel the emotion behind it.

**Lila**: No... Lord Ailaru would never let an innocent die.

**Lila**: I know what I did was wrong. I know I hurt your friend...

**Lila**: But please, believe me — Lord Ailaru must have had his reasons. He never does such things without cause.

**Li Jie**: So you have no idea at all why he wanted the train's passengers scattered?

**Lila**: No... I truly don't. He never told us why.

**Narrator**: Li Jie fell deep into thought.

**Narrator**: Ailaru seemed to have known about the train all along — known that passengers would come into this forest.

**Narrator**: He had deliberately sent Lila to scatter them, to keep them from forming a large group.

**Narrator**: But why? What kind of danger called for that?

---

**Li Jie**: Did Ailaru say anything else? About the train, or about us passengers?

**Lila**: No... he said little more than that.

**Li Jie**: And you don't know why he did it, either?

**Lila**: I'm sorry... I truly don't know. I only did as he commanded.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... ask her if she knows where Misaki is.

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) Do you know where her friend was taken?

**Lila**: I... I don't know where Lord Ailaru took her.

**Lila**: But I can tell you where it was that I attacked you. Perhaps... perhaps you will find some clue there.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) She's willing to tell us where the attack happened.

**Li Jie**: Maybe there'll be some trace of Misaki there.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?

**Lila**: It is what I can do... so that at least you have a chance to search for your friend.

**Narrator**: Lila paused, as if weighing something.

**Narrator**: She looked at the tear tracks on Asuka's face, and guilt flickered in her eyes.

**Lila**: I can show you how to get there.

**Narrator**: From a small pouch at her waist she took a sheet of parchment and a stick of charcoal.

**Narrator**: Lila crouched down and began to sketch a map on the parchment.

---

**Lila**: From Moonlight Glade, first follow the main road east.

**Narrator**: She drew a thick line on the parchment to mark the main route.

**Lila**: After about thirty minutes, you will come to a fork in the road.

**Lila**: Turn right there, onto a smaller side path.

**Narrator**: She marked the fork on the map and drew an arrow to show the way.

**Lila**: That path runs through a stretch of denser woods.

**Lila**: Keep to it for about three hours, and it will join another main road.

**Narrator**: On the map she showed where the side path met the other main road.

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand what Lila was saying, but she could make sense of the marks on the map.

**Lila**: The place where I attacked your friends is near where those two roads meet.

**Lila**: There should also be two houses there — outposts for our elven patrols. You can use them as landmarks.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took the map and studied the route Lila had marked.

**Li Jie**: Thank you. This helps us a lot.

**Lila**: It is the very least I can do to make amends...

**Narrator**: Lila lowered her head, her voice heavy with apology.

**Lila**: If you truly mean to go there, please be careful. The Misty Forest is dangerous.

**Lila**: And... I don't know how things stand there now.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) She's given us a map, with the spot where the attack happened marked on it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you...

**Narrator**: Asuka looked at Lila. They had no language in common, but she could feel how sorry the elf was.

**Narrator**: Her thank-you had been soft, and in Japanese, but Lila seemed to understand it, and nodded.

---

**Li Jie**: Lila, do you really believe what Ailaru told you?

**Lila**: I believe in him. Lord Ailaru is our leader. He has kept us alive in this world for so very long.

**Lila**: He has never betrayed us. He has never once lied to us.

**Lila**: If he said your friend would be all right, then she will be all right.

**Li Jie**: But he's dead. If his plan wasn't finished yet...

**Lila**: I know... Lord Ailaru is gone.

**Lila**: But I believe he must already have arranged everything.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the certainty in Lila's eyes, and only grew more confused.

**Narrator**: What had Ailaru been planning? Why scatter the train's passengers?

**Narrator**: Why attack them, and yet not kill them?

**Narrator**: And who was the attacker with the black sword? Why had he struck at Moonlight Glade?

---

**Li Jie**: Lila, the man who attacked the village... did Ailaru ever say anything about him?

**Lila**: No... The young man with the black sword appeared out of nowhere. We were completely unprepared.

**Lila**: When Lord Ailaru saw him, his face went dark.

**Lila**: As if... he were looking at someone who should never have been there.

**Lila**: But the young man never said a word. He simply fell upon our swordsmen.

**Li Jie**: So Ailaru knew the attacker?

**Lila**: I believe so... From the way Lord Ailaru reacted, he must have known him.

**Lila**: But not a word passed between them. The fighting broke out in an instant.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath. The questions in his mind kept multiplying.

**Narrator**: Ailaru had known the black-sword attacker.

**Narrator**: Ailaru had known about the train, known that passengers would come.

**Narrator**: He had deliberately scattered them, and yet promised that someone would go to tend the wounded.

**Narrator**: What truth lay hidden behind all of it?

---

**Li Jie**: Is there anything else you can tell us? Anything Ailaru ever mentioned?

**Lila**: I'm sorry... Lord Ailaru truly told me nothing more.

**Lila**: He always carried everything alone. He never wished us to know too much.

**Li Jie**: I see. Thank you for telling us all this.

**Lila**: I was only following orders.

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand their conversation, but she could sense it was drawing to a close.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... did she say anything else?

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) Ailaru didn't tell her anything more.

**Li Jie**: But she says she believes he must have arranged everything.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I just hope... Misaki's still alive...

**Narrator**: With that, Lila turned and left the clearing.

**Narrator**: Only Li Jie and Asuka remained, standing where they were, looking up at the two violet moons.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... do you believe what she said?

**Li Jie**: I don't know. But at least... we've got a few leads now.

**Li Jie**: Ailaru knew about the train all along. He knew we'd come.

**Li Jie**: He had some kind of plan. But now that he's dead, we may never know the whole truth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: So what do we do now?

**Li Jie**: First we rest here in the village and restock our supplies.

**Li Jie**: Then... we need to find a way to track down the other survivors, or a way out of this world.

**Li Jie**: This map Lila gave us... it might help us find out what happened to Misaki.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If Misaki really is still alive... I have to find her.

**Li Jie**: We will. But right now we need to keep calm and take it one step at a time.

**Narrator**: The two of them stood side by side beneath the trees, gazing out at the dark forest in the distance.

**Narrator**: Li Jie carefully tucked Lila's map into his pocket. The route marked on it might yet lead them to answers.

**Narrator**: Ailaru's plan remained a mystery.

**Narrator**: No one knew where the attacker with the black sword had gone.

**Narrator**: And in this other world, so full of danger, they had to find a way to survive.

**Narrator**: And... some hope of going home.
