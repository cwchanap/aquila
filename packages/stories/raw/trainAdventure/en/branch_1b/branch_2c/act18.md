# Act 18: The Cruel Truth

**Narrator**: Morning — though there were no true mornings in this world, Li Jie and Asuka had grown used to calling it that: the moment they woke after resting.

**Narrator**: In their guest room at Moonlight Glade, the two of them packed their things and got ready to set out for the place where Lila had said the attack happened.

**Narrator**: The elves of the village were still deep in mourning, but life had to go on.

**Narrator**: Li Jie carefully put away the map Lila had given them and checked that the rusty sword was still in its place at his hip.

**Li Jie**: Ready?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... I'm ready.

**Narrator**: There was resolve in Asuka's eyes, but a hint of unease hid behind it.

**Narrator**: She didn't know what she would find there, but she had to go and find out.

---

**Narrator**: At the entrance to the village, Lila stood quietly, waiting for them.

**Lila**: Are you... truly going?

**Li Jie**: We are. Thank you for the map.

**Lila**: Please be careful. That forest is dangerous...

**Lila**: And I do not know what you will find there.

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand Elvish, but seeing the concern in Lila's eyes, she spoke softly too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Whatever we find, I'm going. Misaki is my friend...

**Narrator**: Lila nodded. She couldn't understand Asuka's Japanese, but she could read the determination in her eyes.

**Lila**: May the moonlight watch over you.

**Li Jie**: Thank you. We'll be on our way.

**Narrator**: The two of them turned and left Moonlight Glade, the village fading behind them into its silver-blue glow.

**Narrator**: Ahead lay the deep, dark forest — and a truth they did not yet know.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie took out the map and headed east along the route Lila had marked.

**Narrator**: The main road was fairly level, lined on both sides by tall, dense trees.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight filtered down through the gaps in the leaves, dappling the ground with light and shadow.

**Narrator**: They walked in silence, broken only by their footsteps and the occasional sigh of the wind.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... do you think Misaki's really still alive?

**Li Jie**: I don't know. But we'll find out soon.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm so scared... scared of finding something I don't want to see...

**Li Jie**: Whatever we find, I'll be right there with you.

**Narrator**: Asuka nodded and stayed close behind him.

**Narrator**: About thirty minutes later, they reached the fork.

**Li Jie**: This is it. We turn right onto the side path.

**Narrator**: The side path was far narrower than the main road, and the trees pressed in more thickly on either side.

**Narrator**: The light grew dimmer too; only scattered patches of violet moonlight made it through the canopy.

---

**Narrator**: They pressed on along the side path, the ground growing rough and uneven underfoot.

**Narrator**: Here they had to skirt a fallen trunk; there, push through thick undergrowth.

**Narrator**: The three-hour walk felt endless.

**Narrator**: Asuka was breathing hard, but she gritted her teeth and kept going.

**Li Jie**: If you're tired, we can take a break.

**Suzuki Asuka**: No... I'm fine. Let's keep going.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at her pale face, but he knew this was no time to argue.

**Narrator**: At last, the woods ahead began to thin.

**Narrator**: Through the gaps in the trees, they saw another, wider road.

**Li Jie**: We made it. This is where the side path meets the main road.

**Narrator**: The two of them stepped out of the trees and stood at the junction.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked around and soon spotted the buildings Lila had described.

---

**Li Jie**: There... one house on the left, and one on the right.

**Narrator**: Both were built in the same style as Moonlight Glade, white rock twined together with trees.

**Narrator**: But these were smaller, more like outposts than homes.

**Narrator**: Everything around them was silent, with no sign of any elven patrol.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Which... which one do we try first?

**Li Jie**: Let's check the one on the left first.

**Narrator**: The two of them crept cautiously toward the house on the left.

**Narrator**: Li Jie kept his hand on the hilt of his sword, on guard.

**Narrator**: As they drew near, the faint smell of blood drifted toward them.

**Li Jie**: Careful... I can smell blood.

**Narrator**: Asuka's face went paler still.

**Narrator**: Li Jie pushed the door open. It gave a faint creak.

**Narrator**: The room inside was dim, lit only by the faint violet light seeping in through the window.

---

**Narrator**: When their eyes adjusted to the dark, the sight before them stopped their breath.

**Narrator**: In the middle of the room lay two bodies.

**Narrator**: One was a young woman; the other, a young man.

**Narrator**: When Asuka saw those two familiar faces, she froze where she stood.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki... Takumi...

**Narrator**: Her voice shook so badly it was barely audible.

**Narrator**: Li Jie recognized them too — two of the university students who had gotten off at the very first stop.

**Narrator**: Takahashi Misaki, with a deep, fatal wound across her throat.

**Narrator**: Sato Takumi, run clean through the chest, the blood long since clotted.

**Li Jie**: These wounds... they're from a sword.

**Narrator**: Li Jie crouched down to look more closely, his brow knotted.

**Li Jie**: The wound on Misaki's neck — one stroke, and she was gone. The cut is very clean. It had to be an extremely sharp sword.

**Li Jie**: And the wound through Takumi's chest... that was a sword too.

**Narrator**: He paused, his expression growing even grimmer.

**Li Jie**: Wounds like these... they look a lot like the ones I saw on the elf swordsmen.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Was it... was it that black-sword man?

**Li Jie**: Very likely. Cuts this clean, and enough force to go straight through...

**Li Jie**: At Moonlight Glade, the elf swordsmen who died had wounds just like these.

**Li Jie**: It must have been the same person.

**Narrator**: Asuka's tears poured down unchecked, and she began to tremble.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Why... why did this happen...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki... she just wanted to live... We just wanted to go home...

**Narrator**: Her voice broke. Her legs gave way, and she sank to her knees on the floor.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went to her side and gently steadied her by the shoulders.

**Li Jie**: Asuka...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I thought... I thought Ailaru had saved Misaki...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Lila said Ailaru would send someone to save her... So... so why...

**Narrator**: She was shaking violently, the tears flowing without end.

**Li Jie**: Maybe... Ailaru really did send someone to save her. But the black-sword man came too.

**Li Jie**: From the wounds, it looks like they were attacked right here.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked around the room, searching for more clues.

**Narrator**: The floor showed signs of a struggle. Some of the furniture had been knocked over, and there were sword marks on the walls.

**Li Jie**: There was a fight here. From these marks, someone was trying to protect them.

**Li Jie**: But... they couldn't.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Takumi... why was he even here...

**Li Jie**: Maybe he was with Misaki. Maybe he was trying to protect her.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked down at Takumi's body. That steady young man had chosen, even in the last moments of his life, to protect his friend.

**Li Jie**: He was brave.

**Narrator**: Asuka's sobs echoed through the little room.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stayed quietly at her side, giving her time to let her grief pour out.

**Narrator**: A long while passed before her sobbing slowly died away.

**Suzuki Asuka**: We... we can't just leave them like this...

**Li Jie**: I know. We'll give them a proper burial.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them left the house and found a fairly level patch of open ground nearby.

**Narrator**: Li Jie dug into the earth with his sword, while Asuka cleared away stones with her bare hands.

**Narrator**: Neither of them spoke. They simply worked in silence.

**Narrator**: When they had dug two graves, they carefully carried out the bodies of Misaki and Takumi.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently laid them in the graves, then began to fill them in.

**Narrator**: Asuka knelt before the graves, palms pressed together, murmuring a prayer.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki... I'm sorry... I couldn't protect you...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Takumi... thank you for staying with Misaki, right to the end...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I hope... you both find peace in the next world...

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood silently to one side, his head bowed in respect.

**Narrator**: Under the violet moonlight, the two rough graves looked unbearably lonely.

---

**Narrator**: When the burial was done, the two of them stood before the graves for a long time.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes were red and swollen from crying, but she was fighting to hold herself together.

**Li Jie**: We should go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm...

**Narrator**: She took one last look at the two graves, then followed Li Jie away.

**Narrator**: When they got back to the junction, Asuka suddenly stopped.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... are we still going to check the house on the right?

**Li Jie**: ...Yes. There might be other clues there.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then let's go.

**Narrator**: She took a deep breath, trying hard to steel herself.

**Narrator**: The two of them turned right and headed toward the other house.

**Narrator**: In the sky, the twin moons still gazed coldly down on the land.

**Narrator**: And in this endless dark, the shadow of death seemed to be everywhere.
