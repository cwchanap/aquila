# Act 19: Quiet Comfort

**Narrator**: The two of them went on along the main road, both of their hearts unbearably heavy.

**Narrator**: The moment they had found Misaki's and Takumi's bodies would not leave their minds.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes were still red and swollen, but she was no longer crying.

**Narrator**: She simply followed Li Jie in silence, one step after another.

**Narrator**: About thirty minutes later, the house on the right came into view.

**Li Jie**: Here we are.

**Narrator**: This house looked almost exactly like the other one, built in the same elven style of white rock twined with trees.

**Narrator**: But there was no smell of blood here, and all around was silence.

**Li Jie**: I'll go in first and take a look. You wait out here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: ...Okay.

**Narrator**: Li Jie carefully pushed the door open, his hand on the hilt of his sword, on guard.

**Narrator**: Inside, the room was neat and clean, with no sign of any struggle.

**Narrator**: Simple furniture stood in tidy order, and in one corner were some supplies left behind by an elven patrol.

**Li Jie**: (stepping back outside) It's safe. No one's here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then... let's stay here tonight.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. It's getting late, and we need rest.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them went inside and shut the door.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight spilled in through the window, casting soft light and shadow across the room.

**Narrator**: In the corner Asuka found some stored food — dry rations, air-dried meat, and a few pieces of what looked like elf-made dried fruit.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'll make us something to eat.

**Li Jie**: You don't have to push yourself...

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's okay. I... I want something to do. Otherwise I'll just keep thinking about...

**Narrator**: Her voice caught a little, but she did her best to pull herself together.

**Li Jie**: Then I'll leave it to you. Thanks.

**Narrator**: Asuka set to work in the little kitchen, while Li Jie sat to one side and quietly watched her.

**Narrator**: Firelight danced in the hearth, lighting up her tired face.

---

**Narrator**: Before long, a simple dinner was ready.

**Narrator**: Toasted dried meat with some dried fruit, and rations softened in warm water.

**Narrator**: It was plain fare, but in this world, it was a rare bit of warmth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's ready. It's not much of a feast, though...

**Li Jie**: It's plenty. Thank you.

**Narrator**: They sat across from each other at the little table and ate in silence.

**Narrator**: No one spoke. There was only the occasional sound of chewing and the crackle of the fire.

**Narrator**: The mood was heavy and hushed, like two wounded hearts seeking comfort in each other's company.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li...

**Li Jie**: Hm?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you... for always being here with me.

**Li Jie**: It's only what I should do.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If it weren't for you... I probably would have fallen apart a long time ago.

**Narrator**: Her eyes reddened again, but this time she managed to hold back the tears.

**Li Jie**: We'll keep going together. Whatever's ahead, I won't leave you behind.

**Suzuki Asuka**: ...Okay.

---

**Narrator**: After dinner, Asuka cleared away the dishes.

**Narrator**: Li Jie, meanwhile, found a simple bathroom in the house.

**Li Jie**: There's a bathroom here. You go wash up first. I'll wait out here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay... I'll go first, then.

**Narrator**: Asuka went into the bathroom and closed the door.

**Narrator**: Soon came the sound of running water.

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat in the room, gazing at the violet moonlight beyond the window, his thoughts in a thousand places at once.

**Narrator**: Misaki's and Takumi's deaths had brought home to him just how dangerous this world was.

**Narrator**: No one knew where the attacker with the black sword had gone, and he could appear again at any moment.

**Narrator**: And they were just two ordinary people, all but defenseless.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) I have to protect her... I can't let her end up like Misaki...

---

**Narrator**: About twenty minutes later, the bathroom door opened.

**Narrator**: Asuka came out, her hair still a little damp, her cheeks flushed from the bath.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Your turn, Mr. Li.

**Li Jie**: Okay.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went into the bathroom and gave himself a quick rinse.

**Narrator**: The warm water washed over his weary body, but it couldn't wash away the weight on his heart.

**Narrator**: When he came out, he found Asuka sitting on the edge of the bed, as if she'd been waiting for him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... you look really tired.

**Li Jie**: I'm all right. You should get some rest too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Um... could I give you a massage?

**Narrator**: Asuka's cheeks went faintly pink, her voice a little shy.

**Suzuki Asuka**: You've been taking care of me these past few days, protecting me...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I want to do something for you too...

**Li Jie**: You don't have to push yourself...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm not pushing myself. Please let me... at least let me do this much.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the earnestness in her eyes and nodded.

**Li Jie**: Then... if you don't mind.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat on the edge of the bed, and Asuka knelt behind him.

**Narrator**: She rested her hands lightly on his shoulders and slowly began to knead.

**Narrator**: Her technique was hardly professional, but there was tenderness and care in it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: You're... really tense here.

**Li Jie**: Yeah... it's been a tense few days.

**Narrator**: Asuka's fingers pressed gently along his shoulders and neck.

**Narrator**: The warmth of her touch carried a comfort words couldn't describe.

**Narrator**: The room was quiet but for their breathing and the occasional rustle of cloth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... have you ever thought about... what we'd do if we really can't go back?

**Li Jie**: ...I have. But I'm not willing to give up hope.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If... if we really can't go back...

**Suzuki Asuka**: At least... at least with you here, I won't be so scared.

**Narrator**: Li Jie could feel her hands trembling slightly.

**Li Jie**: Asuka...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Seeing Misaki and Takumi today... that's when I realized how close death is to us.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Maybe tomorrow... maybe the very next moment, we'll...

**Li Jie**: We won't. I'll protect you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: ...Thank you.

---

**Narrator**: The massage went on for about ten minutes before her hands slowly came to rest.

**Suzuki Asuka**: There... do you feel a little better?

**Li Jie**: Yeah, much better. Thank you.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned around and looked at her.

**Narrator**: Her eyes were tired, but there was a touch of warmth in them, too.

**Li Jie**: You must be tired too. Time to sleep.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm...

**Narrator**: They both looked at the one and only bed in the room.

**Narrator**: They'd grown used to the arrangement over the past few days, but every time there was still a flicker of awkwardness.

**Suzuki Asuka**: So... should we just share it... like before?

**Li Jie**: ...Sure.

**Narrator**: They lay down on opposite sides of the bed, leaving a gap between them.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight from the window fell across the bed, casting soft light and shadow in the space between them.

---

**Narrator**: The room was quiet except for the sound of their breathing.

**Narrator**: But Asuka tossed and turned, unable to sleep, Misaki's face rising again and again in her mind.

**Narrator**: That sunny, cheerful girl, the friend who was always laughing...

**Narrator**: Now her eyes were closed forever.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (softly) Mr. Li... are you asleep?

**Li Jie**: Not yet. What's wrong?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I can't sleep.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Every time I close my eyes, I think of Misaki...

**Narrator**: Her voice trembled, thick with tears she was holding back.

**Li Jie**: ...Come here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Huh?

**Li Jie**: If you're scared, come a little closer. Maybe that'll help you feel safer.

**Narrator**: Asuka hesitated for a moment, then slowly shifted over toward him.

**Narrator**: She leaned lightly against his shoulder and could feel the warmth of his body.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm sorry... I lean on you too much...

**Li Jie**: It's okay. In this world, all we've got is each other to lean on.

**Narrator**: His voice was gentle, with a steadying strength to it.

**Narrator**: Asuka closed her eyes, and tears slid silently down her face.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... if we really do make it back...

**Suzuki Asuka**: will you... still remember me?

**Li Jie**: ...Of course I will. You're the most important companion I have in this world.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Just a companion...?

**Narrator**: Her voice was so small it was barely audible.

**Narrator**: Li Jie was silent for a moment. Then he said softly:

**Li Jie**: ...More than that.

**Narrator**: Asuka's heart leapt.

**Narrator**: She wanted to say something, but found her throat had closed up.

**Narrator**: In the end, she only pressed closer to his side.

---

**Narrator**: Gradually, Asuka's breathing grew even.

**Narrator**: With Li Jie beside her, she finally fell asleep, at peace.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked down at her tired, sleeping face, a tangle of emotions welling up inside him.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) In a world this full of danger...

**Li Jie**: (thinking) at least... at least let me keep you safe.

**Narrator**: He gently wrapped an arm around her, to help her feel that much safer.

**Narrator**: Outside the window, the violet moonlight shone on, as cold as ever.

**Narrator**: But in this small house, two wounded hearts had found warmth in each other.

**Narrator**: Amid grief and fear, beneath the shadow of death...

**Narrator**: a small and precious feeling was quietly taking root.

**Narrator**: That night was quiet, and gentle.
