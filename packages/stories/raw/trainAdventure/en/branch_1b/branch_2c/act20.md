# Act 20: Fading Hope

**Narrator**: When Li Jie woke again, Asuka was still nestled against him, sleeping soundly.

**Narrator**: Being held last night seemed to have given her the sense of safety she needed.

**Narrator**: Li Jie didn't move. He just quietly watched the violet sky beyond the window.

**Narrator**: There were no sunrises in this world, but he had learned to tell the time by how tired his body felt.

**Li Jie**: (softly) Time to get up.

**Narrator**: Asuka opened her eyes groggily, found she was still leaning on Li Jie, and instantly turned red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: S-sorry... I...

**Li Jie**: It's fine. Did you sleep okay?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... I slept really well. Thank you.

**Narrator**: She slowly sat up and smoothed down her tousled hair.

**Narrator**: Li Jie got out of bed and went to the little kitchen.

**Li Jie**: I'll make breakfast. My turn today.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Oh... okay.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie made a simple breakfast from what food was left.

**Narrator**: Toasted rations with some dried fruit, and herbal tea steeped in warm water.

**Narrator**: They sat across from each other and ate their breakfast in silence.

**Li Jie**: Let's head back to Moonlight Glade today.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... I was thinking the same thing.

**Li Jie**: There aren't many supplies left here. And... we need to talk to Lila.

**Li Jie**: Maybe she's heard something more about the other survivors.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta... I don't know where he is now...

**Narrator**: There was worry in Asuka's voice.

**Narrator**: Of the friends who had gotten off the train with her, Kenta was the only one still unaccounted for.

**Li Jie**: We'll find some lead on him somehow.

---

**Narrator**: Once they had packed up, the two of them left the outpost.

**Narrator**: They went back the way they had come, and when they passed the two new graves, Asuka stopped.

**Narrator**: She stood silently before them for a while, palms pressed together.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki... Takumi... I'll live for you both.

**Narrator**: Li Jie, too, bowed his head beside her.

**Narrator**: Then the two of them went on toward Moonlight Glade.

**Narrator**: On the way back, their steps were heavier still.

**Narrator**: Not from exhaustion, but because the grief in their hearts kept growing deeper.

---

**Narrator**: Several hours later, the silver-blue glow of Moonlight Glade came into view once more.

**Narrator**: The village was still wrapped in its dreamlike haze of light, but the mood seemed a little lighter than before.

**Narrator**: The elves were slowly recovering from Ailaru's death and had begun to rebuild their daily lives.

**Narrator**: At the entrance to the village, a young elf swordsman caught sight of them.

**Elf Swordsman**: You've returned. Did your journey go well?

**Li Jie**: Well enough. Do you know where Lila is? We'd like to see her.

**Elf Swordsman**: Lila is in the square at the center of the village. I'll take you there.

**Narrator**: The elf swordsman led them along the village paths.

**Narrator**: The elves they passed cast curious looks their way, but none were hostile.

**Narrator**: Soon they reached the square.

---

**Narrator**: Lila was standing at the edge of the square, apparently in discussion with several other elves.

**Narrator**: When she saw Li Jie and Asuka, her face lit up with glad surprise.

**Lila**: You're back! Thank goodness... I was afraid you might have met with danger.

**Li Jie**: We're fine. Thanks for worrying about us.

**Lila**: Did you... find your friends?

**Narrator**: Asuka couldn't understand Lila's Elvish, but from her expression and tone she could tell what she was asking.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes went red at once, and she bit her lip.

**Li Jie**: Misaki and Takumi are both dead. We found their bodies at the place you told us about.

**Lila**: ...I'm sorry. I... I believed Lord Ailaru had truly sent someone to save them...

**Narrator**: Lila's eyes were full of guilt and sorrow.

**Lila**: I'm so sorry... It's all because of me...

**Narrator**: Asuka looked at Lila's guilt-stricken face. She couldn't understand her words, but she knew she was apologizing.

**Suzuki Asuka**: No... it's not your fault.

**Narrator**: Asuka said it in Japanese. Lila couldn't understand it, but she heard the comfort in her tone.

**Li Jie**: It's not your fault. The one who killed them was the black-sword man.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... could you ask Lila something for me?

**Li Jie**: What is it?

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Ask her if she knows where Kenta is...

**Suzuki Asuka**: He got off the train with us. A guy who talks a lot...

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) She wants to know what became of another friend of hers.

**Li Jie**: A talkative guy who kept yelling the whole time he ran. Did you see which way he went?

**Narrator**: Lila frowned, as if trying hard to remember.

**Lila**: You mean... the young man who kept shouting as he fled?

**Lila**: I'm sorry... I don't know. Everything was in chaos then. All I saw was all of you running in different directions.

**Lila**: But...

**Narrator**: Lila paused, her expression growing heavier.

**Lila**: Recently we sent patrols out to search the area, to make sure no danger remained...

**Lila**: They found the bodies of two humans nearby. A man and a woman.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) She doesn't know where Kenta is...

**Li Jie**: But... she says they found two human bodies nearby.

**Narrator**: Asuka's face went white.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Wh... what?

**Narrator**: Her voice shook, and she began to sway.

**Li Jie**: (catching her) Asuka!

**Suzuki Asuka**: No... it can't be... it can't be Kenta...

**Narrator**: Her breath came fast and shallow, and the tears spilled out beyond her control.

---

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) Could we see the bodies?

**Lila**: Of course... Please, come with me.

**Narrator**: Lila led them out of the square, toward the far side of the village.

**Narrator**: Asuka clung tightly to Li Jie's arm, trembling all over.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) Please... please don't let it be Kenta...

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently patted her hand, but said nothing.

**Narrator**: He knew that in this world, miracles were rare.

**Narrator**: They came to a small hut at the edge of the village.

**Narrator**: Lila pushed open the door. Inside lay two bodies draped in white cloth.

**Lila**: These are the ones... We found them on the western side of the Misty Forest.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and stepped forward.

**Narrator**: He reached out and drew back the white cloth from the first body.

---

**Narrator**: It was a young woman's face, her long black hair spread out beside her, matted with blood.

**Narrator**: She wore a light beige dress and a plain jacket. Her face was as white as paper, her eyes shut tight.

**Narrator**: Li Jie recognized her — the quiet girl who had gotten off at the station along with the others.

**Li Jie**: It's that... quiet girl...

**Narrator**: He drew back the cloth from the second body.

**Narrator**: A young man in glasses, an obvious wound in his chest, a look of terror frozen on his face.

**Li Jie**: And... the timid guy in glasses...

**Narrator**: When Asuka saw that it wasn't Kenta, relief went through her whole body.

**Narrator**: But a moment later, grief and guilt came flooding in.

**Suzuki Asuka**: That girl... and the guy with the glasses...

**Narrator**: Her voice broke, and the tears came again.

**Suzuki Asuka**: They're... they're dead too...

**Narrator**: Li Jie examined the two bodies closely.

**Narrator**: The girl had a deep sword wound in her neck, the cut clean and precise, her black hair splayed beside her, caked with dried blood.

**Narrator**: The guy in glasses had a wound clean through his chest — plainly made by a sharp blade driven straight through.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's gaze lingered on the wounds for a moment, his brow furrowed.

**Li Jie**: These wounds...

**Narrator**: He paused, thinking back carefully to the wounds he had seen before.

**Li Jie**: They're the same as the sword wounds on Misaki and Takumi...

**Li Jie**: Cuts this clean, and that much force behind the thrust... It has to be the same person.

**Suzuki Asuka**: You mean... the same person killed them too?

**Li Jie**: Very likely. From where the wounds are and how much force went into them, it has to be the same person.

**Narrator**: Asuka went paler still.

**Lila**: When we found them, they were lying deep in the forest. They seemed to have been dead for some time.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) Lila says they were found deep in the forest. They'd already been dead for a while.

**Li Jie**: (nodding) These wounds... someone did this to them.

**Narrator**: Li Jie drew the white cloths back over them, his face grave.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: At least... at least it's not Kenta...

**Narrator**: Her voice was small, touched with relief — and then at once swallowed up by guilt.

**Suzuki Asuka**: No... I shouldn't think like that... They were innocent too...

**Suzuki Asuka**: And... if it really was that black-sword man... Kenta might also...

**Narrator**: Her voice broke; she couldn't bring herself to finish.

**Li Jie**: You don't need to blame yourself. You just want your friend to be alive.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently took Asuka's hand.

**Li Jie**: But... Asuka, we both know...

**Narrator**: He paused, and his voice grew heavier.

**Li Jie**: If Kenta ran into that black-sword man...

**Li Jie**: the chances he's still alive... are slim.

**Narrator**: Asuka bit her lip, the tears streaming down without stopping.

**Narrator**: What Li Jie said was the truth — a cruel truth, but one there was no escaping.

**Narrator**: The eight who had gotten off at the first stop — Asuka, Kenta, Misaki, Takumi, the quiet girl, the timid guy in glasses, the generous older man, and the man in black...

**Narrator**: Now only Asuka was still alive, and no one knew what had become of Kenta.

**Narrator**: The other six were all dead.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Why... why did it turn out like this...

**Suzuki Asuka**: All we did was get on a train... All we wanted was to go home...

**Narrator**: Her voice was full of despair and grief.

**Li Jie**: I know... None of this is fair.

**Narrator**: He pulled Asuka into his arms and gently patted her back.

**Narrator**: Lila stood to one side. She couldn't understand what they were saying, but her eyes were full of sympathy and guilt.

**Lila**: We will keep sending out search parties. If we find your friend...

**Li Jie**: Thank you. If you find Kenta, alive or dead... please, be sure to tell us.

**Lila**: I will. I promise.

**Narrator**: Asuka wept in Li Jie's arms, her whole body shaking.

**Narrator**: Hope was fading, bit by bit.

**Narrator**: Every companion's death made this world a little darker.

**Narrator**: And beneath this endless night, the survivors grew fewer and fewer.

**Narrator**: Holding Asuka tight, Li Jie made a silent vow.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) At least... at least I'll keep you safe. I can't let you end up like them...

**Narrator**: Outside the window, the violet moonlight shone on, as cold as ever.

**Narrator**: In this cruel world, survival had become their only hope.
