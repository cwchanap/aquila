# Act 21: Alongside the Elves

**Narrator**: After that night of grief, Li Jie and Asuka decided to stay in Moonlight Glade.

**Narrator**: They had no other choice — in all that dangerous forest, this village was the only safe haven.

**Narrator**: The elves took them in, and Lila found them a small cabin beneath the trees at the edge of the village.

**Narrator**: The cabin wasn't large, but it was warm and comfortable. Outside the window, luminous plants swayed gently, shedding a soft silver-blue glow.

---

**Narrator**: The days went by one after another — though this world had no true daytime, they were slowly growing used to the endless violet night.

**Narrator**: To repay the elves for taking them in, Li Jie and Asuka began helping with the work of the village.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went out with the elf swordsmen to learn how to patrol, while Asuka helped tend the luminous plants.

**Narrator**: Now and then they also went along with Lila's patrol, searching the forest for any other survivors.

---

**Narrator**: The morning of the third day — or rather, the third cycle since they had woken.

**Narrator**: Li Jie was on the village training ground, practicing swordplay with a young elf swordsman.

**Narrator**: Not far away, Asuka was by the flower beds with a few elf women, tending the glowing blossoms.

**Narrator**: Lila came over from the center of the village, a trace of weariness on her face.

**Lila**: Li Jie. Asuka.

**Narrator**: The two of them put down their work and went over to her.

**Li Jie**: What is it?

**Lila**: We're patrolling near the City of the Netherworld station today. Would you... like to come with us?

**Li Jie**: (turning to Asuka) She says they're patrolling around the City of the Netherworld station today. She's asking if we want to come along.

**Suzuki Asuka**: The City of the Netherworld? Isn't that...

**Li Jie**: Where I got off the train.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Let's go together. Maybe we'll find someone else.

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) We'll come with you.

**Lila**: Then... get yourselves ready. We leave in an hour.

---

**Narrator**: An hour later, a patrol of five elves and two humans set out.

**Narrator**: Lila walked at the front, a long silver bow in her hand.

**Narrator**: The rusty sword hung at Li Jie's hip, and Asuka carried a small pack stuffed with supplies.

**Narrator**: They followed the main road north through the dense woods.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight sifted down through the leaves, scattering dappled light and shadow over the ground.

**Narrator**: About three hours later, a familiar outline of buildings rose up ahead.

**Li Jie**: The station...

**Narrator**: The City of the Netherworld station stood there in silence, just as it had when they left.

**Narrator**: The platform was deserted. Only the eternal violet moonlight lay across its cold flagstones.

**Lila**: We'll split up and search. Be careful.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gave Asuka a short nod: stay close.

---

**Narrator**: The patrol fanned out and began combing the area around the station.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka followed Lila into the station's main building.

**Narrator**: The waiting hall was exactly as they had left it: the benches in neat rows, the clock on the wall stopped forever at the same moment.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight spilled in through the high windows, stretching long shafts of light across the floor.

**Narrator**: When they reached the middle of the hall, Lila stopped short.

**Lila**: Over there...

**Narrator**: She pointed to a row of benches in the corner of the hall.

**Narrator**: Li Jie followed her gaze, and his heart sank.

**Narrator**: On the floor beside the benches lay the body of a young man.

**Narrator**: He was in casual clothes, curled in on himself, as if in his last moments he had tried to hide.

**Narrator**: A deep sword wound split his chest. The blood had long since dried into a dark red stain on the floor.

**Narrator**: The instant Asuka saw his face, she froze.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta...

---

**Narrator**: Her voice shook, and the tears came all at once.

**Narrator**: She stumbled toward Kenta's body. Her legs gave out, and she dropped to her knees.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta... Kenta...

**Narrator**: She reached out a trembling hand to touch his face, but it stopped in midair. She couldn't bring herself to touch him.

**Narrator**: That face, which had almost always been smiling, was so pale now. His eyes were closed, as if he were only asleep.

**Narrator**: But the horrific wound in his chest was a cruel reminder that he was gone.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm sorry... I'm sorry...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I should have stayed with you... I shouldn't have left you behind...

**Narrator**: Her voice was choked with sobs, her whole body shaking violently.

**Narrator**: Li Jie crouched down and carefully examined Kenta's body.

**Li Jie**: A sword wound to the chest... A killing blow. The same wound as the others.

**Narrator**: He looked at Kenta's hands and noticed traces of blood and fiber under the fingernails.

**Li Jie**: He tried to fight back... but his attacker was too strong.

**Lila**: It was the black-sword man...

**Li Jie**: Most likely.

---

**Narrator**: Asuka knelt beside Kenta, the tears flowing unchecked.

**Narrator**: She remembered that day in the Misty Forest, Kenta shouting in a panic that they had to run.

**Narrator**: She remembered his face, always smiling, and the way he had tried to get everyone to loosen up on the train.

**Narrator**: Every one of those memories had become a blade in her heart.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta... you were so scared...

**Suzuki Asuka**: So why... why did you try to fight back...

**Narrator**: Her voice grew smaller and smaller, until it dissolved into silent weeping.

**Narrator**: Li Jie laid a gentle hand on her shoulder, but said nothing.

**Narrator**: He knew that right now, any words of comfort would ring hollow.

---

**Narrator**: Lila gave the other swordsmen their orders in Elvish.

**Lila**: Bring a stretcher. We're taking him back to the village.

**Narrator**: A young elf took a simple folding stretcher from his pack.

**Narrator**: Carefully, they lifted Kenta's body.

**Narrator**: Asuka got to her feet, but her eyes never left Kenta.

**Narrator**: Her hand brushed the edge of the stretcher, as if she wanted to touch him one more time.

**Narrator**: But in the end she only stepped back and let the elves carry it away.

---

**Narrator**: No one spoke on the way back.

**Narrator**: There was only the sound of the stretcher moving over the ground, and the rustle of wind through the leaves.

**Narrator**: Asuka walked close behind the stretcher, her steps heavy.

**Narrator**: Back in Moonlight Glade, the elves prepared a grave for Kenta.

**Narrator**: When the simple funeral was over, Asuka knelt before his grave and would not leave for a long time.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood behind her, keeping her quiet company.

**Narrator**: The night grew deeper — though the night here never really changed.

**Narrator**: Lila came to stand beside Li Jie and spoke softly.

**Lila**: Let her cry. It helps to let it out.

**Li Jie**: Yeah.

**Narrator**: The two of them stood there, watching Asuka's back as she wept.

---

**Narrator**: That night, Asuka cried for a long time.

**Narrator**: Back in the cabin, she leaned against Li Jie, her tears soaking through his clothes.

**Narrator**: Li Jie said nothing. He only stroked her hair gently.

**Narrator**: The next day, Asuka was still lost in grief.

**Narrator**: She wouldn't eat and wouldn't speak. She only sat by the window, staring at nothing.

**Narrator**: Li Jie let her be, and went to help with the village's work on his own.

---

**Narrator**: Asuka sat by the window, gazing out at the glowing plants.

**Narrator**: Beyond the glass there was still only the eternal violet moonlight, cold and remote.

**Narrator**: Scenes from the past kept rising in her mind.

**Narrator**: Toward evening, Li Jie came back to the cabin.

**Narrator**: He sat down quietly beside Asuka, careful not to disturb her.

**Narrator**: After a long while, Asuka finally spoke.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... would you like to hear about me and Kenta and the others?

**Li Jie**: If you want to tell me, I'd like to hear it.

**Narrator**: Asuka hugged her knees, her eyes on the window.

**Narrator**: Her voice was very soft, with a slight tremor in it.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: I remember... it was last spring.

**Suzuki Asuka**: The four of us had a picnic under the cherry trees at our university.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta was super excited that day, because the convenience store had just come out with a limited-edition strawberry sandwich.

**Li Jie**: Sounds just like him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yeah... that's the kind of guy he was.

**Suzuki Asuka**: He came running over with this huge bag of sandwiches and said they were his treat.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki laughed and thanked him, but Takumi reminded him he still hadn't finished his report.

**Narrator**: A faint smile rose at the corners of Asuka's mouth, but tears slid down with it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And Kenta said... "We finally get a break, so no talking about reports!"

**Suzuki Asuka**: So we sat there under the cherry trees, eating sandwiches and chatting.

**Suzuki Asuka**: The sun was so warm that day... and the cherry petals kept drifting down onto us...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Misaki was laughing so happily... and even Takumi relaxed and laughed, for once...

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie didn't say anything. He just listened.

**Narrator**: Asuka paused, took a deep breath, and went on.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then... another time, we were all working on a report together at my place.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta and Takumi sat at the low table in the living room, and Misaki and I went into the kitchen to make dinner.

**Suzuki Asuka**: By the time we finished the report it was really late, so we all lay down on the living room floor to rest.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And then Kenta suddenly asked Takumi...

**Suzuki Asuka**: He asked if the four of us would always be together like this.

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice grew fainter and fainter, and the tears kept falling.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Takumi said... "Wherever we end up, we'll still be friends."

**Suzuki Asuka**: And Kenta was so happy. He said, "Then it's a promise. We all get together every year from now on."

**Suzuki Asuka**: Back then... we really thought... we really thought we could always be together...

---

**Narrator**: Her voice broke, and she began to tremble all over.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently drew her into his arms.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... but now...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta's dead... Misaki's dead... Takumi's dead too...

**Suzuki Asuka**: We promised... we promised we'd always be together...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Why... why did it have to turn out like this...

**Narrator**: She buried her face in Li Jie's chest, her tears soaking his clothes.

**Narrator**: Li Jie patted her back gently, without a word.

**Narrator**: He knew that right now, all she needed was a shoulder to lean on.

**Narrator**: Outside the window, the violet moonlight shone on, as indifferent as ever.

**Narrator**: All those happy memories had become knives in her heart.

**Narrator**: Every smile, every word they had shared, only made the pain worse.

**Narrator**: Because those people were never coming back.

---

**Narrator**: Asuka cried in Li Jie's arms until she wore herself out and fell asleep.

**Narrator**: Li Jie carefully carried her to the bed and tucked the blanket around her.

**Narrator**: He sat at the bedside, looking down at her exhausted, sleeping face.

**Li Jie**: (softly) Asuka... I'll keep you safe.

**Narrator**: He held her hand lightly, giving her the sense of safety she needed.

---

**Narrator**: On the third day, Asuka was no better.

**Narrator**: She began to eat a little, but her eyes were still empty.

**Narrator**: At night she would cry again, until she wore herself out and fell asleep.

**Narrator**: Every night Li Jie stayed with her, letting her lean on him, giving her the warmth she needed.

---

**Narrator**: Early on the morning of the fourth day.

**Narrator**: Asuka finally spoke again.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... thank you for staying with me all this time.

**Li Jie**: It's the least I could do.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I want to go and see Kenta's grave.

**Li Jie**: All right. I'll come with you.

**Narrator**: The two of them went to the graveyard, and Asuka laid a bouquet of glowing flowers on Kenta's grave.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta... I'm going to live for you, too. I'll live well.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'll go back to our world and tell your family... that you were brave, right to the very end.

**Narrator**: Her voice still trembled, but it was a little steadier than before.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood beside her, leaving her to say goodbye to her friend in peace.
