# Act 22: Calling Off the Search

**Narrator**: In the days that followed, Asuka slowly emerged from the depths of her grief.

**Narrator**: She went back to helping with the work of the village, and now and then a smile even returned to her face.

**Narrator**: But Li Jie knew those scars were still carved deep in her heart.

---

**Narrator**: Outside the window, the violet moons still hung high, bathing the village in silver-blue light.

**Narrator**: Asuka stood at the window, looking out at the glowing plants.

**Narrator**: She thought of Kenta's smiling face, and Misaki's, and Takumi's.

**Narrator**: Those memories still stung, but she knew she had to be strong and go on living.

**Narrator**: For the friends she had lost, she had to go on living.

**Narrator**: Li Jie came back in from outside and saw her standing at the window, her back to him.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, you should get some rest.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (turning around with a smile) Mm, I know.

**Narrator**: There was still something forced about her smile, but at least she was willing to keep going.

**Narrator**: In this endless night, the will to survive was still burning.

---

**Narrator**: In the quiet days at Moonlight Glade, Li Jie and Asuka gradually settled into life there.

**Narrator**: Smiles had returned to Asuka's face, though a look of sorrow still crossed it now and then.

**Narrator**: But at least she no longer spent her days in tears.

**Narrator**: The peace, however, did not last long.

---

**Narrator**: A week later, Lila came to find them with a report from the patrol.

**Lila**: Li Jie, our patrol has found something.

**Narrator**: Asuka turned at the sound of Lila's voice. She couldn't understand Elvish, but she could sense something in the air.

**Narrator**: A faint shiver ran through her.

**Li Jie**: Found what?

**Lila**: In an abandoned house north of the station, we found the mother of that family.

**Lila**: And... along the tracks toward the Canyon of the Dead, we found the father.

**Li Jie**: ...Both dead?

**Lila**: (nodding) Yes. Judging by the wounds, it must have been the black-sword man, both times.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned to Asuka, his expression grave.

**Li Jie**: (to Asuka) They found that couple... Both of them are dead.

**Narrator**: Asuka said nothing. She only sat there, very still.

**Narrator**: Her eyes were empty, as if she had already gone numb.

**Li Jie**: (to Lila) I understand. Thank you for telling us.

---

**Narrator**: Three days later, Lila came to their cabin again.

**Lila**: Li Jie... at the Canyon of the Dead station, we found another body.

**Lila**: Another middle-aged man.

**Li Jie**: ...I see.

**Narrator**: Li Jie glanced at Asuka by the window. She was still sitting quietly, without any reaction at all.

**Narrator**: She no longer cried at news like this.

**Narrator**: Li Jie said something to her, and she only gave a small nod.

---

**Narrator**: A few days later, there were three new graves in the Moonlight Glade graveyard.

**Narrator**: The parents of that family, and the irritable middle-aged man, had all found their resting place here.

**Narrator**: That night, Li Jie went to find Lila.

**Narrator**: Lila was up on the village lookout, gazing out over the distant forest.

**Li Jie**: Lila.

**Lila**: (turning around) Li Jie. Did you need something?

**Li Jie**: I wanted to... It's about the search for other survivors.

**Lila**: What about it?

**Li Jie**: I'd like the elves to stop searching.

**Lila**: (puzzled) Why? There are still several people unaccounted for...

**Li Jie**: I know. That family's two children, and the other middle-aged man.

**Li Jie**: But... I think they've probably already...

**Narrator**: He didn't finish, but Lila understood what he meant.

---

**Lila**: You mean... they may already be dead?

**Li Jie**: It's been so long. If they were still alive, they'd have found their way here by now.

**Li Jie**: I don't want your people risking themselves on this search any longer. There are other dangers in the forest.

**Lila**: But...

**Li Jie**: Even if you did find them, it would only be more bodies.

**Li Jie**: Asuka has already been through too much. I don't want her to see any more of the dead.

**Narrator**: Lila was silent for a moment, gazing at the distant forest.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight lay across the treetops, and the whole forest was wrapped in gloom.

**Lila**: I understand. I'll tell the patrol to stop searching.

**Li Jie**: Thank you.

---

**Narrator**: Lila turned to look at Li Jie, a hint of concern in her eyes.

**Lila**: Li Jie... is Asuka all right?

**Li Jie**: ...Honestly, I don't know.

**Li Jie**: She seems calm, but I know it's only on the surface.

**Li Jie**: The pain she's carrying inside... I can't even imagine it.

**Lila**: She's lucky to have you with her.

**Li Jie**: I just... don't want her to face all this alone.

**Narrator**: The two of them stood on the lookout, looking down at the glowing plants of the village.

**Narrator**: Their silver-blue light softly lit every cabin.

**Narrator**: In one of those cabins, Asuka sat alone by the window, staring blankly out.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie left the lookout and went back to the cabin.

**Narrator**: He pushed the door open and found Asuka still sitting by the window.

**Li Jie**: Asuka.

**Narrator**: She turned her head and gave him a strained smile.

**Suzuki Asuka**: You're back.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. I just went and talked to Lila.

**Suzuki Asuka**: About what?

**Li Jie**: I asked her to call off the search.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked startled for a moment, then nodded.

**Suzuki Asuka**: ...Thank you.

**Li Jie**: You don't need to see any more of the dead.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Actually... I've already gone numb.

**Narrator**: Her voice was calm — so calm it hurt to hear.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Every time they find someone, I know there's one less hope that anyone's still alive.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kenta's dead, Misaki's dead, Takumi's dead...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Almost everyone who was on that train with us is dead.

**Li Jie**: Asuka...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, do you think... we'll really make it out of here alive?

**Narrator**: She looked up, a trace of bewilderment in her eyes.

**Li Jie**: ...I don't know. But we'll keep trying, together.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Together... Right. Together.

**Narrator**: She stood and came over to stand in front of him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: At least... I still have you.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and gently took her hand.

**Li Jie**: I'll stay with you.

---

**Narrator**: Outside the window, the violet moons still hung high, bathing the village in silver-blue light.

**Narrator**: In this endless night, the two survivors held on tight to each other.

**Narrator**: There were more and more graves in the graveyard, each one a broken hope.

**Narrator**: But at least they still had each other.

**Narrator**: In this cruel world, perhaps that was the only consolation.

**Narrator**: The search had ended, but survival went on.

**Narrator**: And out in the distant dark, unknown dangers still lay in wait.

**Narrator**: Waiting for their next prey.
