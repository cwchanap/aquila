# Act 23: Quiet Days by Silver Moon Lake

**Narrator**: Little by little, life in Moonlight Glade grew peaceful.

**Narrator**: Asuka smiled more and more, though a look of sorrow still crossed her face now and then.

**Narrator**: But at least she had begun to embrace life again.

**Narrator**: Watching her change, Li Jie felt a measure of relief.

---

**Narrator**: Early that morning, Asuka was by the flower beds, tending the glowing blossoms.

**Narrator**: A young elf woman stood beside her, showing her with gestures how to water them.

**Narrator**: Li Jie came over and saw the two of them together.

**Elf Woman**: Li Jie, these flowers need to be watered with moonlight water. Could you hand her the bucket?

**Li Jie**: Sure.

**Narrator**: Li Jie passed the small wooden bucket to Asuka.

**Li Jie**: Water the flowers with this moonlight water.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Moonlight water?

**Li Jie**: It's water from Silver Moon Lake. It makes the flowers glow brighter.

**Narrator**: Asuka took the bucket carefully and sprinkled the water over the flowers.

**Narrator**: As if the flowers could feel themselves being nourished, their light grew softer and warmer.

**Suzuki Asuka**: So pretty...

**Elf Woman**: She's doing very well. She has a real gift for this.

**Li Jie**: Yeah, she's very careful.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm really happy I can help out here.

---

**Narrator**: Not far away, Li Jie was on the training ground, practicing swordplay with several elven swordsmen.

**Narrator**: His swordsmanship was still clumsy, but the elves were patient teachers.

**Elf Swordsman**: Li Jie, your footwork needs to be steadier. Like this.

**Narrator**: The young elf demonstrated the basic forms, his movements fluid and graceful.

**Li Jie**: Got it. Let me try again.

**Narrator**: Li Jie tightened his grip on the hilt and swung the way the elf had taught him.

**Narrator**: This time the motion was much smoother than before.

**Elf Swordsman**: Very good! You're learning fast. The way humans learn is truly astonishing.

**Li Jie**: It's all thanks to your teaching.

**Narrator**: The elven swordsman smiled and clapped Li Jie on the shoulder.

---

**Narrator**: At midday, long tables were set out in the square at the center of the village.

**Narrator**: The elves had prepared a lavish lunch and invited everyone to eat together.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka sat at one of the long tables, surrounded by warm, welcoming elves.

**Narrator**: The table was crowded with every kind of food.

**Narrator**: Roasted forest fruit, a fresh salad of moonlight grass, and a stew seasoned with silver-dew mushrooms and spices.

**Narrator**: Lila sat across from them and set a fragrant dish in front of Asuka.

**Lila**: Here, try this. It's our village's specialty — star-dew stew.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you! It looks delicious.

**Narrator**: Asuka took a bite, and her eyes lit up at once.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so good!

**Li Jie**: What's in it?

**Lila**: Star-dew herb, which grows only by Silver Moon Lake, stewed with game from the forest.

**Lila**: I'm glad she likes it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's really delicious...

---

**Narrator**: Lunch went on amid laughter.

**Narrator**: The elves swapped funny stories from around the village, and the mood was lively.

**Elf Swordsman**: Li Jie, the progress you made the last time we practiced really surprised me.

**Li Jie**: It's all thanks to your teaching.

**Elf Swordsman**: Humans really do learn quickly. Your lives are short, but you're always so full of energy.

**Narrator**: The elf woman sitting beside Asuka looked at her, then turned to speak to Li Jie.

**Elf Woman**: Li Jie, does your world... have a moon too?

**Li Jie**: It does. But we only have one, and it's silvery white.

**Elf Woman**: Only one... It must be very lonely.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Hmm... I'd never thought of it like that before.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But it's true — seeing the twin moons here, there's something special about it.

---

**Narrator**: After lunch, Lila took Li Jie and Asuka up to a lookout at the edge of the village.

**Narrator**: From here they could see the whole of Moonlight Glade, and the deep, dark forest beyond.

**Lila**: Are you settling in here all right?

**Li Jie**: Very well. Everyone's been so kind to us.

**Lila**: I'm glad. It makes me happy to see Asuka smiling more and more.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Lila, thank you so much... and everyone else too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If it weren't for all of you, I probably would have fallen apart a long time ago.

**Lila**: We're glad we could help you.

**Narrator**: The three of them stood quietly on the lookout, savoring a moment's peace.

**Narrator**: After a while, Li Jie broke the silence.

**Li Jie**: Lila... there's something I want to ask you.

**Lila**: What is it?

**Li Jie**: It's about... a way home. Do you know of any way to leave this world?

---

**Narrator**: Lila was silent for a moment, her expression turning complicated.

**Lila**: To be honest... I don't know.

**Lila**: We elves are born in this world. We have never thought of leaving it.

**Lila**: As for the train that brought you here... it is something even we cannot understand.

**Narrator**: Asuka's face fell.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then... we can never go back?

**Lila**: Don't lose heart. I may not know, but... someone might.

**Li Jie**: Someone might?

**Lila**: North of Silver Moon Lake, there stands an ancient Sacred Tree.

**Lila**: It is the most sacred place in this forest. They say the Sacred Tree possesses boundless wisdom.

**Lila**: Perhaps... the Sacred Tree can give you some guidance.

---

**Li Jie**: The Sacred Tree...

**Lila**: When Lord Ailaru was alive, he once told me that whenever I faced a question I couldn't answer, I should go and ask the Sacred Tree.

**Lila**: That tree has stood for thousands of years. It has witnessed every change this world has seen.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... how do we get there? Is it far?

**Lila**: From here, by way of Silver Moon Lake, it's about half a day's journey.

**Lila**: The way isn't very dangerous. Just keep north along the path by the lakeshore, and you'll get there.

**Li Jie**: Would you be willing to take us?

**Lila**: I... would love to, but I can't be away from the village for long.

**Lila**: Since Lord Ailaru passed away, I have to stay here and look after everyone.

**Lila**: But I can give you a map, and some supplies.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie nodded and looked at Asuka.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, what do you think?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I want to go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If the Sacred Tree really can tell us how to get home...

**Li Jie**: Then we'll go.

**Lila**: When are you thinking of setting out?

**Li Jie**: Give us one more day to prepare. I'd like us to get some proper rest and build our strength back up.

**Lila**: All right. Tomorrow I'll get everything you need ready for you.

**Narrator**: There was a trace of reluctance in Lila's eyes, but she smiled all the same.

**Lila**: If you can find your way home, nothing would make me happier.

---

**Narrator**: Over the next day, Li Jie and Asuka cherished their time in Moonlight Glade all the more.

**Narrator**: They worked with the elves, ate with them, and strolled through the village together.

**Narrator**: Asuka kept learning how to care for the glowing flowers, and learned to weave the garlands only the elves knew how to make.

**Narrator**: Li Jie kept up his sword practice and helped with odd jobs around the village.

**Narrator**: At night they would sit around the bonfire with the elves.

**Narrator**: The elder elves told ancient legends and stories of their festivals.

**Elder Elf**: On the day of the Springbirth Festival, the whole forest awakens. That is when new life is born.

**Elder Elf**: During the Moonglow Festival, the moonlight grows especially bright, and the whole village is bathed in silver.

**Elder Elf**: And on the Night of Stars, we pray to the sky and bless one another with safety.

**Narrator**: The stories were full of mystery and magic, and for a while they let the two of them forget the dangers of this world.

---

**Narrator**: That evening, Asuka sat on the bench outside the cabin.

**Narrator**: In her hands was a garland she had just finished weaving, dotted all over with tiny glowing flowers.

**Narrator**: Li Jie came over and sat down beside her.

**Li Jie**: That's beautiful work.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thanks. The elf lady taught me.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... do you think the Sacred Tree really can tell us how to get home?

**Li Jie**: I'm not sure. But at least... it's the only lead we have right now.

**Suzuki Asuka**: What if... what if the Sacred Tree doesn't know either?

**Li Jie**: Then we keep looking. As long as we're alive, there's always hope.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked down at the garland in her hands and spoke softly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm going to miss this place...

**Suzuki Asuka**: These past few days have been the most peaceful I've had since I came to this world.

**Li Jie**: Me too.

---

**Narrator**: On the morning they set out, a crowd of elves gathered at the village entrance.

**Narrator**: They had all come to see Li Jie and Asuka off.

**Narrator**: Lila handed Li Jie a backpack full of supplies.

**Lila**: There's enough food and water here for a day, and some healing herbs.

**Lila**: And this is the map to the Sacred Tree. Keep north along the lakeshore path and you won't get lost.

**Li Jie**: Thank you, Lila.

**Narrator**: The elf woman who had taught Asuka to care for the plants stepped forward.

**Narrator**: She handed Asuka a tiny glass vial.

**Narrator**: Inside was a luminous liquid that gave off a soft silver-blue glow.

**Elf Woman**: This is moonlight water. If you're ever in danger, pour it on the ground.

**Elf Woman**: It will give off a bright light that can frighten some beasts away.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you... thank you so much.

**Narrator**: Asuka hugged the elf woman tight, her eyes brimming.

**Narrator**: The elf woman gave Asuka's back a gentle pat.

**Elf Woman**: May the moonlight watch over you.

---

**Narrator**: The other elves came forward one after another with blessings and little gifts.

**Narrator**: One gave them waterproof cloaks; another, a small lantern to light their way.

**Narrator**: Lila stood at the very front, watching Li Jie and Asuka.

**Lila**: May the moonlight watch over you on your journey.

**Lila**: If you find a way home... remember to come back and tell us.

**Li Jie**: We will.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Lila... everyone... thank you so much. Really.

**Suzuki Asuka**: All this time, you've been like family to me.

**Lila**: You are our family too. You always will be.

**Narrator**: At last, Asuka's tears spilled over.

**Narrator**: But this time they weren't tears of grief — they were tears of gratitude, and of not wanting to say goodbye.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka shouldered their packs and turned toward the way out of the village.

**Narrator**: Behind them, the elves called out their blessings and waved.

**Narrator**: Ahead lay the path to Silver Moon Lake, and a journey into the unknown.

**Li Jie**: Let's go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm.

**Narrator**: The two of them looked back one last time at Moonlight Glade.

**Narrator**: The glowing plants, the warm, smiling faces — all of it was etched deep in their hearts.

**Narrator**: Then they set out on the road to the Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight fell across the path, lighting the way ahead.

**Narrator**: In this endless night, a new hope was beginning to sprout.

**Narrator**: The Sacred Tree waited in the distance. Perhaps the answer that would take them home lay hidden there.
