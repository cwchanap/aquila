# Act 24: The Sacred Tree's Pronouncement

**Narrator**: After leaving Moonlight Glade, Li Jie and Asuka followed the map Lila had given them toward Silver Moon Lake.

**Narrator**: Under the violet moonlight, the forest path was hushed and lovely, luminous plants swaying gently along the way.

**Narrator**: About two hours later, the trees ahead began to thin, and a familiar silver-blue glow showed through the gaps between the trunks.

**Li Jie**: We're here. This is the place.

**Narrator**: The two of them stepped out of the forest, and Silver Moon Lake spread before them once more.

**Narrator**: The water was as still as a mirror, just as before, reflecting the twin moons in the sky.

**Narrator**: Glowing flowers floated gently on its surface, giving off a dreamlike silver light.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's still just as beautiful...

---

**Narrator**: Gazing out at the lake, Asuka found memories flooding back.

**Narrator**: Back then she hadn't known Li Jie yet. She had just been a university student who got off the train with her friends.

**Narrator**: She, Misaki, Kenta and Takumi had chosen to explore a hidden trail, as if they were playing an RPG and hunting for a hidden route.

**Narrator**: They had still been full of the spirit of adventure then, thinking it was nothing more than a thrilling escapade...

**Narrator**: Until they were attacked in the Misty Forest, and everything changed.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Back then... I was so scared.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I was lost in the fog for hours, hiding all alone in that cabin...

**Li Jie**: And then I found the cabin, and found you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yeah... thanks to you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If you hadn't found me then... I'd probably have...

**Narrator**: A shadow of sadness crossed Asuka's face.

**Narrator**: She was thinking of Misaki, Kenta, Takumi... friends she would never see again.

**Li Jie**: I remember the first time we came here, too.

**Li Jie**: That was when we found the elven booklet together, in one of the cabins by the lake.

**Suzuki Asuka**: That booklet... it ended up helping us so much.

**Li Jie**: It did. Without it, we might never even have found Moonlight Glade.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them strolled slowly along the shore, the grass soft and pleasant underfoot.

**Narrator**: Violet moonlight fell across the lake, glittering in the ripples.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, do you remember?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Last time we rested here, I was splashing around in the water with you...

**Li Jie**: I remember. You got water all over my face.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Sorry about that... I just wanted to lighten the mood a little.

**Li Jie**: I know. We really did need to unwind a bit back then.

**Narrator**: Asuka went to the water's edge, crouched down, and reached out to touch the surface.

**Narrator**: The water was as cold and clear as she remembered.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If only we could stay here forever...

**Suzuki Asuka**: No worrying about danger, no being afraid of losing anyone...

**Li Jie**: I understand. But we have to keep going.

**Narrator**: Li Jie came over and crouched down beside her.

**Li Jie**: The Sacred Tree really might be able to tell us how to get home.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... you're right.

**Narrator**: Asuka stood up and shook the droplets from her hands.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then let's take a break and eat something before we go on.

**Li Jie**: Sure.

---

**Narrator**: They found a flat rock by the lake and sat down.

**Narrator**: Asuka took out the food Lila had packed for them — some roasted dried fruit and elven bread.

**Narrator**: They ate in silence, savoring a moment's peace.

**Narrator**: Overhead, the twin moons still hung high, their silver-blue light gently enfolding the whole lake.

**Narrator**: In a world so full of danger, Silver Moon Lake was something close to a miracle.

**Narrator**: Beautiful, peaceful, far from all the turmoil.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, if... if the Sacred Tree really can send us home...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Will you miss this place?

**Li Jie**: ...I suppose I will. There are a lot of good memories here.

**Li Jie**: There's been a lot of pain and fear too, but...

**Narrator**: He looked at Asuka, his eyes gentle.

**Li Jie**: At least I met you.

**Narrator**: Asuka's cheeks flushed red in an instant.

**Suzuki Asuka**: M-me... me too...

**Suzuki Asuka**: If I hadn't met you, I'd probably have...

**Li Jie**: Don't think about that. We'll go back together.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood and reached out to pull Asuka to her feet.

**Li Jie**: Come on. Time to get moving.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay!

---

**Narrator**: The two of them shouldered their packs and headed north along the shore.

**Narrator**: According to Lila's directions, the Sacred Tree lay about half a day's journey north of Silver Moon Lake.

**Narrator**: They followed the lakeside path, and the grass underfoot gradually gave way to a forest trail carpeted with fallen leaves.

**Narrator**: The trees around them grew taller and taller, their branches so thick they almost blotted out the sky.

**Narrator**: Yet even here, a faint light still found its way through the canopy, lighting the path ahead.

**Narrator**: After about three hours of walking, Li Jie noticed the atmosphere beginning to change.

**Narrator**: The air grew fresher, carrying an indescribable sense of the sacred.

**Narrator**: The trees grew straighter too, as if they were all growing toward one particular place.

**Li Jie**: We're almost there. Can you feel it?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... the air feels different somehow.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It feels... so solemn.

**Li Jie**: The Sacred Tree must be just up ahead.

---

**Narrator**: After about thirty more minutes, the view suddenly opened up before them.

**Narrator**: An immense tree towered at the center of a clearing.

**Narrator**: Its canopy nearly blotted out the entire sky, and a soft silver-blue light glowed among its branches.

**Narrator**: Its trunk was so massive it would have taken a dozen people or more to encircle it, and its bark was densely carved with ancient writing and mysterious symbols.

**Narrator**: Its roots heaved up out of the earth and spread in every direction, gripping the ground like the claws of a great dragon.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's... it's huge...

**Narrator**: Asuka's mouth fell open. She was utterly overwhelmed by the sight.

**Narrator**: The sheer scale of the tree went far beyond anything she could have imagined.

**Narrator**: It was not merely a tree. It was more like the sovereign of this forest, majestic and holy.

**Li Jie**: So this is the Sacred Tree...

**Narrator**: Li Jie, too, was overwhelmed by the sight.

**Narrator**: But unlike Asuka's simple awe, a more complicated feeling welled up inside him.

**Narrator**: The tree certainly gave off an aura of holiness, but...

**Narrator**: Beneath that holiness, something else seemed to be hidden — something he couldn't quite put into words.

**Li Jie**: Come on. Let's go closer.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them approached the Sacred Tree with great care.

**Narrator**: The closer they came, the thicker that sacred aura grew.

**Narrator**: Asuka found herself even breathing carefully, afraid of disturbing the ancient tree.

**Narrator**: They stood before the Sacred Tree, gazing up at its vast canopy.

**Narrator**: Its branches swayed gently in the breeze, rustling softly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: So... what do we do now?

**Li Jie**: Lila said the Sacred Tree possesses boundless wisdom.

**Li Jie**: Maybe... we should try to communicate with it?

**Narrator**: The two of them stood where they were, waiting in silence.

**Narrator**: One minute passed.

**Narrator**: Two minutes passed.

**Narrator**: Five minutes passed.

**Narrator**: Nothing happened.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's... not reacting?

**Li Jie**: Doesn't look like it...

**Narrator**: Li Jie frowned, thinking about what to do next.

**Li Jie**: Maybe... we need to touch it?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Touch it?

**Li Jie**: Yeah. Let me try.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and slowly walked toward the Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: He reached out and pressed his palm to the rough bark.

**Narrator**: The instant his palm touched the trunk—

**Narrator**: The world abruptly changed.

**Li Jie**: What?!

**Narrator**: Silver Moon Lake, the forest, Asuka — everything vanished.

**Narrator**: In their place was an endless grey void.

**Narrator**: The sky was grey, the ground was grey; even the air was thick with a dull grey haze.

**Narrator**: The whole world seemed to have lost every color, leaving nothing but flat shades of grey.

**Li Jie**: What... what's going on?!

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked around in shock.

**Narrator**: Only he and the Sacred Tree behind him still kept their true colors.

**Narrator**: Asuka was gone.

**Li Jie**: Asuka! Asuka!

**Narrator**: Li Jie shouted at the top of his lungs, but no answer came.

**Narrator**: In the grey void, only his own voice echoed.

**Li Jie**: Damn it... What the hell is going on?!

---

**Narrator**: Just then, a voice rang out.

**Narrator**: It was a deep and ancient voice, seeming to come from every direction at once.

**Narrator**: Or as if it were sounding directly inside Li Jie's mind.

**Sacred Tree**: Human... I know all things.

**Narrator**: Li Jie spun around to face the Sacred Tree behind him.

**Narrator**: The trunk stood perfectly still, yet the voice was unmistakably the Tree's.

**Li Jie**: You... you're the Sacred Tree?

**Sacred Tree**: I am. I am the guardian of this forest, the holy tree of the elven people.

**Sacred Tree**: For a thousand years, I have witnessed the birth and passing of countless lives.

**Li Jie**: Then... what about Asuka? Where is she?!

**Sacred Tree**: She is unharmed. She merely cannot see you, for now.

**Narrator**: Li Jie let out a breath of relief, but he didn't let his guard down.

**Li Jie**: You brought me here. What do you want?

**Sacred Tree**: You have come seeking my guidance, pitiful sinner.

**Sacred Tree**: You wish to return to the world where you belong, do you not?

**Narrator**: Li Jie was taken aback, his brow furrowing deeply.

**Narrator**: Pitiful sinner? Why would the Sacred Tree call him that?

**Narrator**: But for now, his longing to go home outweighed the doubt in his heart.

**Li Jie**: ...Yes. I want to go home.

---

**Sacred Tree**: I know all things.

**Sacred Tree**: Your past, your present, and the futures that may yet be yours.

**Sacred Tree**: You are an outsider, carried into this world by that train.

**Sacred Tree**: You long to go home, to leave this perilous otherworld.

**Li Jie**: If you know all that... then can you tell me how to get back?

**Narrator**: The Sacred Tree was silent for a moment.

**Sacred Tree**: I can.

**Li Jie**: Then...

**Sacred Tree**: Keep going north. Through this forest, and over the mountains of the north.

**Sacred Tree**: At the northernmost edge of the continent lies a ruin called the Altar of the End.

**Sacred Tree**: There stands a door.

**Sacred Tree**: A door that joins your world to this one.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's heart began to race.

**Narrator**: At last... at last, a clear answer.

**Li Jie**: The Altar of the End... the door...

**Li Jie**: If we just get there, we can go home?

**Sacred Tree**: Yes, pitiful sinner. But you must understand...

**Narrator**: The Sacred Tree's voice sank lower still.

**Sacred Tree**: That door demands a price.

**Li Jie**: What price?

**Narrator**: But the Sacred Tree did not answer.

**Narrator**: The grey world began to fade, and color slowly returned.

**Li Jie**: Wait! Tell me what you mean!

**Li Jie**: Why do you call me a pitiful sinner?! What did I do?!

**Narrator**: But still the Sacred Tree did not answer.

**Narrator**: The scenery of Silver Moon Lake took shape again.

**Narrator**: The forest, the grass, and the enormous Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: When his sight had fully returned, Li Jie found himself still standing before the Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: His palm still pressed to its bark.

---

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li? Mr. Li!

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice came from beside him.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned his head sharply and saw her looking at him, her face full of worry.

**Suzuki Asuka**: What happened to you? All of a sudden you just... froze. You didn't move at all.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I called you over and over, and you didn't respond...

**Narrator**: Li Jie stared blankly at Asuka.

**Narrator**: She was still here.

**Narrator**: Everything had come back.

**Narrator**: But the Sacred Tree's words kept echoing in his mind.

**Narrator**: "Pitiful sinner"...

**Narrator**: "That door demands a price"...

**Narrator**: What on earth did those words mean?
