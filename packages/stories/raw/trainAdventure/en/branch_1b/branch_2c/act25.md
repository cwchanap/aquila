# Act 25: The Road North

**Narrator**: Li Jie came back to himself from the Sacred Tree's vision, his palm still pressed to the rough bark.

**Narrator**: Asuka's anxious voice rang in his ears, but the Sacred Tree's words were still echoing in his mind.

**Narrator**: "The Altar of the End"... "the door"... and the name it had called him, the one that left him so uneasy.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li! You're finally responding...

**Suzuki Asuka**: What on earth happened to you just now? I called you so many times...

**Narrator**: Li Jie drew back his hand and turned to look at Asuka's worried face.

**Li Jie**: I'm fine. The Sacred Tree... the Sacred Tree gave us an answer.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?! What did it say?

**Li Jie**: It told me that if we keep heading north, through this forest and over the mountains of the north...

**Li Jie**: At the northernmost edge of the continent, there's a ruin called the Altar of the End.

**Li Jie**: There's a door there that can take us back to our own world.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes lit up at once.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?! We can really go home?!

**Li Jie**: Yes. We finally have a clear goal.

**Narrator**: Asuka grabbed Li Jie's arm in excitement.

**Suzuki Asuka**: That's wonderful... that's wonderful...

**Suzuki Asuka**: At last... at last we can go home...

**Narrator**: Her eyes reddened, but this time the tears weren't from grief. They were tears of joy and hope.

**Narrator**: Watching her excitement, Li Jie felt a tangle of emotions well up inside him.

**Narrator**: He didn't tell Asuka that the Sacred Tree had called him a "pitiful sinner."

**Narrator**: Nor did he tell her the last thing the Tree had said — "That door demands a price."

**Narrator**: Those words were too heavy. He didn't want Asuka to lose the hope she had only just found again.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, let's rest for a bit first.

**Li Jie**: It's still a long way from here to the Altar of the End. We need to get our strength back.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay! Whatever you say.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them found a flat patch of grass beneath the Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: Silver-blue light drifted down through the canopy, laying soft patterns of light and shadow on the ground.

**Narrator**: Asuka took the blanket Lila had packed out of her bag and spread it on the grass.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, you sleep first. I'll keep watch.

**Li Jie**: No need. You're tired too. Let's both rest.

**Li Jie**: It should be safe around the Sacred Tree. No beasts or anything else dangerous will come near.

**Narrator**: Asuka nodded and lay down on the blanket.

**Narrator**: Li Jie lay down beside her, keeping a proper distance between them.

**Narrator**: But after a little while, Asuka shifted quietly closer to him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... can I lean my head on your shoulder?

**Li Jie**: ...Sure.

**Narrator**: Asuka rested her head on Li Jie's shoulder and closed her eyes.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you... for staying with me all the way here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'd never have made it this far without you.

**Li Jie**: Same here. Without you, this journey would have been a lot lonelier.

**Narrator**: A faint smile rose at the corners of Asuka's mouth.

**Narrator**: Under the Sacred Tree's protection, in its soft light, the two of them slowly drifted off to sleep.

---

**Narrator**: When Li Jie woke again, much of his strength had come back.

**Narrator**: Asuka was still sound asleep, her breathing slow and peaceful.

**Narrator**: Li Jie didn't wake her. He just quietly watched the Sacred Tree's branches swaying in the breeze.

**Narrator**: The questions surrounding that "pitiful sinner" still circled in his mind.

**Narrator**: Why had the Sacred Tree called him that?

**Narrator**: What price did that door demand?

**Narrator**: Only at the Altar of the End would he find the answers.

**Narrator**: After a while, Asuka woke up too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... good morning.

**Li Jie**: Did you sleep okay?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... really well. I had a dream.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I dreamed we got home... back to our own world.

**Li Jie**: That dream's going to come true soon.

**Narrator**: Asuka sat up and smoothed her slightly tousled hair.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then let's get going!

**Li Jie**: Right.

---

**Narrator**: They packed up their things and shouldered their bags.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took one last look at the Sacred Tree. The vast ancient tree still stood there quietly, silent and mysterious.

**Narrator**: They kept heading north, following the map Lila had given them.

**Narrator**: They crossed the clearing where the Sacred Tree stood and entered the deep forest once more.

**Narrator**: The farther north they went, the taller and denser the trees became.

**Narrator**: The violet moonlight could barely pierce the heavy canopy; only scattered glimmers lit the trail.

**Narrator**: But Li Jie found that even in such gloom, he could still sense the right direction.

**Narrator**: It was an instinct he couldn't explain, as if some invisible force were guiding him.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Is this the Sacred Tree's doing...?

**Narrator**: As they went on along the trail, the ground underfoot slowly changed from grass to gravel and dirt.

**Narrator**: The air grew colder and colder, until they could see their breath misting white.

**Narrator**: After about four hours of walking, Li Jie noticed the terrain beginning to change.

**Narrator**: The forest was as dense as ever, but the ground had begun to rise and fall. They were climbing.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, let's take a break.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay... I was just about to say that.

**Narrator**: They stopped beneath an enormous tree.

**Narrator**: Asuka sat down against the trunk and took a canteen and some food out of her pack.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, have something to eat.

**Narrator**: They ate their rations in silence, building their strength back up.

**Narrator**: After resting for about twenty minutes, they set off again.

---

**Narrator**: The ground grew steeper and steeper, and the trail grew rough and hard going.

**Narrator**: Sometimes they had to clamber over rocks; sometimes they had to push through dense thickets.

**Narrator**: But Li Jie could still feel that pull, and knew they hadn't gone the wrong way.

**Narrator**: Asuka's breathing grew ragged, but she gritted her teeth and kept going.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... how much farther?

**Li Jie**: We should be close. Hang in there a little longer.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked back at Asuka's exhausted face.

**Li Jie**: Want to take another break?

**Suzuki Asuka**: No... no, it's fine. Let's keep going.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I want to get there soon... get home soon.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes were full of resolve.

**Narrator**: Li Jie nodded and kept leading the way.

**Narrator**: After about two more hours, they stopped to rest again.

**Narrator**: This time Asuka all but collapsed onto the ground, her legs trembling uncontrollably.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm sorry... I'm slowing you down...

**Li Jie**: Don't say that. You've been trying really hard.

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat down beside her and handed her the canteen.

**Li Jie**: Drink some water and get some proper rest. We're in no hurry.

**Narrator**: Asuka took the canteen and gulped the water down.

**Narrator**: This time they rested for nearly forty minutes before setting out again.

---

**Narrator**: As they drew closer to their destination, their surroundings began to change.

**Narrator**: The trees gradually thinned out, revealing more and more rock and cliff face.

**Narrator**: The air carried something ancient and heavy.

**Narrator**: After about two more hours, the woods ahead began to thin.

**Narrator**: Through the gaps in the trees, they could make out the outline of a massive structure in the distance.

**Li Jie**: Look. Over there.

**Narrator**: Asuka followed Li Jie's gaze, and her eyes went wide.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Is that...

**Narrator**: In an instant, her exhaustion gave way to excitement.

**Narrator**: They quickened their pace and came out of the forest.

**Narrator**: The sight before them left them speechless.

---

**Narrator**: It was a structure so magnificent it stole their breath.

**Narrator**: It was built entirely of black stone, smooth as a mirror, glinting with a deep sheen in the violet moonlight.

**Narrator**: The building soared into the clouds, its top lost from sight, as if it meant to pierce the sky.

**Narrator**: The black stone it was made of had no rough texture anywhere on its surface.

**Narrator**: Under the violet moons it reflected no light at all; instead it seemed to drink in every bit of it, becoming a darkness deeper than the night.

**Narrator**: The vast arched entrance stood silently open, like the maw of some great beast, waiting for its prey to walk right in.

**Narrator**: Above the arch were carved intricate, twisting patterns, half visible in the faint moonlight.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's... it's huge...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Is this the Altar of the End?

**Li Jie**: It must be. This is the place the Sacred Tree meant.

**Narrator**: The two of them stood before the building, feeling the majesty and age it gave off.

**Narrator**: It didn't feel like something humans had built. It felt like a ruin left behind by something older and more powerful.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It feels... so scary...

**Li Jie**: I know. But we have to go in.

**Li Jie**: The door is inside.

**Narrator**: Asuka took a deep breath and nodded.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... we go in together.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gripped the hilt of his sword, and Asuka stayed close behind him.

**Narrator**: The two of them stepped through the great arch.

---

**Narrator**: The moment they stepped inside, every sound around them vanished.

**Narrator**: The wind in the forest, the birdsong, even their own breathing grew faint.

**Narrator**: In their place was a suffocating, deathly silence.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) It's so quiet...

**Narrator**: The waiting hall was immense.

**Narrator**: Huge stone pillars rose from the floor to a vaulted ceiling lost from sight, each one carved with strange runes.

**Narrator**: The runes flickered in and out of view in the faint violet moonlight, as if slowly breathing.

**Narrator**: The air was cold and thin; every breath stung like inhaling ice.

**Narrator**: The air smelled of ancient stone and ozone, and of something else hard to name — the stale mustiness peculiar to places sealed away for a very long time.

**Narrator**: The floor was paved with polished black stone that clearly reflected them.

**Narrator**: Looking at the lonely figure on the floor, Asuka couldn't tell for a moment which one was the real her.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... this place...

**Li Jie**: Don't be afraid. Stay close to me.

**Narrator**: At the far end of the hall were two exits.

**Narrator**: One led toward the platform; the other was a dark corridor leading deeper into the building.

**Narrator**: The corridor was like a bottomless wound, split open in the building's body.

**Narrator**: There was no light inside at all. The darkness was so thick it seemed solid, and it gave off a chill that set the heart pounding.

**Li Jie**: That corridor...

**Narrator**: Li Jie felt the pull inside him grow stronger.

**Narrator**: The door the Sacred Tree had spoken of lay deep within that corridor.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, stay close. We're going that way.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay...

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice shook a little.

**Narrator**: The two of them headed toward the dark corridor.

**Narrator**: Every footstep rang through the empty hall and came back as an eerie echo.

**Narrator**: It was as if countless invisible versions of themselves were walking in step with them in the dark.

---

**Narrator**: The moment they entered the corridor, the light around them almost completely disappeared.

**Narrator**: Only the faint glow of the hall behind them barely lit the first few steps past the entrance.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so dark... Mr. Li, I'm scared...

**Li Jie**: Hold my hand. Don't let go.

**Narrator**: Asuka gripped Li Jie's hand tight, feeling the warmth of his palm.

**Narrator**: That warm touch was the only thing that made her feel safe in all that darkness.

**Li Jie**: We'll take it slow. Watch your step.

**Narrator**: They made their way slowly along the corridor, Li Jie trailing his other hand along the wall.

**Narrator**: The walls were covered in intricate runes; he could feel how deep the grooves were cut.

**Narrator**: The corridor went on and on, and the air grew colder and colder.

**Narrator**: Every breath Asuka took felt like inhaling needles of ice.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so cold...

**Li Jie**: Bear with it a little longer. We should be close.

**Narrator**: Walking in absolute darkness, time seemed to lose all meaning.

**Narrator**: Asuka had no idea how long they had been walking. All she could do was cling to Li Jie's hand and keep going, one step at a time.

**Narrator**: Just as she was beginning to despair, a faint blue light appeared ahead.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, look! There's light!

**Li Jie**: Yeah. I see it. We're almost there.

**Narrator**: They quickened their pace toward the light.

**Narrator**: In the darkness the blue light seemed especially gentle, giving them hope and a direction.

**Narrator**: As they drew nearer, their surroundings gradually came into focus.

**Narrator**: Ahead lay the end of the corridor.

**Narrator**: And the entrance to the depths of the altar.
