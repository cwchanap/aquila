# Act 26: The Sleeper on the Altar

**Narrator**: The corridor ended at a junction, a slightly wider space.

**Narrator**: A few glowing blue gems were set into the walls, giving off a faint, ghostly light.

**Narrator**: Their glow was soft and remote, like phosphorescence in the deep sea, and it lit the whole area.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Finally... some light...

**Narrator**: Asuka let out a breath of relief, but she still held tight to Li Jie's hand.

**Narrator**: Three paths led away from the junction.

**Narrator**: One was a stone stairway leading down, sinking into even deeper darkness. The steps were steep, and there was no seeing the bottom; it looked like a passage into the depths of the earth.

**Narrator**: One was a stairway leading up, just as steep, climbing higher and higher until it vanished into the gloom.

**Narrator**: The last was a corridor that ran on straight ahead, deep and dark.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Three ways... Which one do we take?

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood in the middle of the junction and closed his eyes.

**Narrator**: He reached for the pull inside him — that invisible force that came from the Sacred Tree.

**Narrator**: It flowed through his body, as if pointing him the way.

**Narrator**: A moment later he opened his eyes, and his gaze settled on the stairs leading down.

**Li Jie**: This way. Down.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Are you sure?

**Li Jie**: I can feel it. The door's down there.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked down the deep stairway and bit her lip.

**Narrator**: The steps looked steep and bottomless, and there was no telling where they led.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay... I trust you.

**Li Jie**: Watch your step. Take it slow.

**Narrator**: The two of them started down the stone stairs.

---

**Narrator**: The stairs were steep, each step narrow and slick.

**Narrator**: Li Jie went first, carefully, with Asuka close behind him.

**Narrator**: The blue gems grew sparser around them, and the light grew dimmer.

**Narrator**: The air turned colder still; every breath brought a bone-deep chill.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so cold...

**Li Jie**: Hang in there a little longer. We're almost there.

**Narrator**: The stairs seemed to go on forever, down and down and down.

**Narrator**: Asuka's legs began to shake, whether from the cold or from exhaustion, she couldn't tell.

**Narrator**: Li Jie slowed down to make sure she could keep up.

**Narrator**: All the way down, he kept an eye on how she was doing.

**Li Jie**: You holding up?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... I'm fine.

**Narrator**: That was what she said, but the exhaustion in her voice was plain.

**Narrator**: After a long while more, a faint golden light at last appeared below them.

**Li Jie**: See that? That's it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Golden light...

**Narrator**: It was a soft, warm golden light, nothing like the blue glow at the junction.

**Narrator**: There was something holy about it, something that stirred a nameless awe.

**Narrator**: The two of them quickened their pace.

**Narrator**: The stairs soon came to an end and opened onto another corridor.

---

**Narrator**: This corridor was much wider than the last.

**Narrator**: The walls on either side were not only set with glowing gems but carved with exquisite reliefs.

**Narrator**: The reliefs showed all kinds of scenes — people kneeling in worship, sacrificial rites, and some mysterious creature drawn blazing with light.

**Suzuki Asuka**: These pictures...

**Narrator**: Asuka slowed down to study the reliefs on the wall.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's like they're telling a story.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. Maybe the history of this altar.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the reliefs too, trying to work out what they meant.

**Narrator**: But the images were too ancient and too arcane for him to fully understand.

**Narrator**: At the end of the corridor stood a massive pair of stone doors.

**Narrator**: They rose several meters high, every inch of them carved with fine reliefs and runes.

**Narrator**: At their center was a great circular design that gave off a faint golden glow.

**Li Jie**: This is it.

**Narrator**: The two of them stood before the stone doors, heads tipped back to take them in.

**Narrator**: The runes on the doors were intricate and delicate, like some ancient script.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Can we... even push them open?

**Li Jie**: Let's find out.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and pushed against the stone doors.

**Narrator**: With a deep, grinding rumble, the heavy doors slowly swung apart.

**Narrator**: Through the gap poured a brighter, holier golden light.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so bright...

**Narrator**: Asuka shielded her eyes with her hand, waiting for them to adjust.

**Narrator**: When the stone doors stood fully open, the sight before them took their breath away.

---

**Narrator**: It was an immense space — no, "sanctum" was the only word for it.

**Narrator**: The vaulted ceiling soared so high that its top was lost from sight.

**Narrator**: Every wall was carved with exquisite murals, scene after scene out of what seemed to be myth.

**Narrator**: The air was heavy with holiness, and awe rose in them unbidden.

**Narrator**: At the very center of the space stood an altar of white marble.

**Narrator**: A faint golden light encircled it, as if some invisible power stood guard over it.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (whispering) It feels so holy...

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice had turned careful, as though she were afraid of disturbing the sanctum.

**Li Jie**: (in a low voice) Let's go take a look.

**Narrator**: The two of them stepped slowly into that hallowed space.

**Narrator**: Here, their footsteps made almost no sound.

**Narrator**: The floor was paved with smooth white flagstones, every one of them spotless.

**Narrator**: Asuka trod carefully, afraid of dirtying the sacred floor.

**Narrator**: As they neared the altar, they finally saw what lay upon it.

---

**Narrator**: A young man lay peacefully on the altar.

**Narrator**: He wore a pure white robe, and his hands were folded on his chest, as if he were asleep.

**Narrator**: Golden light drifted gently around him, gilding him in a holy radiance.

**Suzuki Asuka**: There's someone...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Who is he?

**Li Jie**: No idea. But he looks... like he's sleeping.

**Narrator**: Li Jie stepped up to the altar and studied the man closely.

**Narrator**: He looked about twenty, with a handsome, finely made face.

**Narrator**: His skin was fair, his features even, and his long lashes cast faint shadows on his cheeks.

**Narrator**: The white robe was spotless, and in the golden light it looked all the more holy.

**Narrator**: His chest rose and fell, faintly. He was alive.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Is he... is he still alive?

**Li Jie**: Should be. But he's sleeping awfully deep.

**Narrator**: Asuka went over to the altar and carefully reached out.

**Narrator**: Her fingertips brushed the man's arm, and she felt a trace of warmth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: He's warm... He really is alive.

**Narrator**: She gently shook his shoulder.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Um... excuse me? Can you hear us?

**Narrator**: Nothing.

**Narrator**: His eyes stayed closed and his breathing steady, as if he had sunk into the deepest of sleeps.

**Li Jie**: Looks like we can't wake him.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's gaze moved past the altar, to what lay behind it.

**Narrator**: Asuka followed his eyes.

**Narrator**: Behind the altar was a door.

---

**Narrator**: The door shone with a bright, warm white light.

**Narrator**: It was more dazzling than the golden glow around the altar, full of a holy, gentle power.

**Narrator**: Light seeped out around its edges, casting long shadows across the floor.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, look. That door...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Is that...

**Narrator**: Her voice trembled with excitement.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Is that the door the Sacred Tree talked about?

**Li Jie**: It should be. The door that connects our world with this one.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes lit up at once.

**Narrator**: They were full of hope and joy, and of a little disbelief.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?! We actually found it?!

**Narrator**: She turned to Li Jie, overcome, her eyes going red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: We can really... really go home?

**Narrator**: Watching her, so overjoyed, Li Jie felt a tangle of emotions well up inside him.

**Narrator**: The Sacred Tree's words still echoed in his mind: "That door demands a price."

**Narrator**: But now, looking at the hope in Asuka's eyes, he couldn't bring himself to say it.

**Li Jie**: ...Yes. We can go home.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and took Asuka's hand.

**Narrator**: He could feel her palm trembling — excitement and joy, mixed with a trace of unease.

**Li Jie**: Come on. Let's go together.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm!

---

**Narrator**: The two of them circled around the altar toward the door glowing with white light.

**Narrator**: The man on the altar slept on peacefully, as if he knew nothing of what was happening around him.

**Narrator**: The closer they came to the door, the brighter the white light grew.

**Narrator**: Asuka clung to Li Jie's hand, her heart racing.

**Narrator**: This was the way out they had searched for so long.

**Narrator**: This was the way home.

**Narrator**: After so many dangers, after losing so many companions...

**Narrator**: At last, they had found their hope of going home.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka stood before the door, feeling the warm light that spilled from it.

**Narrator**: Here, in the hallowed space of the altar, with the sleeper as their witness...

**Narrator**: They were about to step out of this world and go back to where they belonged.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at Asuka.

**Li Jie**: Ready?

**Narrator**: Asuka took a deep breath and gave a firm nod.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... I'm ready.

**Narrator**: They both drew a deep breath.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and pushed against the radiant door.

**Narrator**: With a faint sound, it slowly swung open.

**Narrator**: An even brighter white light surged out from behind it, washing the whole chamber in holy white.

**Narrator**: The light rolled over them like a tide, warm and soft.

**Narrator**: Li Jie tightened his grip on Asuka's hand.

**Li Jie**: Whatever happens, I won't let go of your hand.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Me neither...

**Narrator**: They looked at each other and smiled.

**Narrator**: And then, together, they stepped into the light.

---

**Narrator**: In an instant, the white light engulfed them.

**Narrator**: Warmth flowed through every part of them, as if they were being gently held.

**Narrator**: They couldn't feel the ground. They couldn't feel gravity.

**Narrator**: There was only that endless white light, and their hands, clasped tight.

**Narrator**: Behind them, the heavy stone doors slowly swung shut.

**Narrator**: Silence returned to the altar chamber.

**Narrator**: The sleeping man still lay peacefully on the altar.

**Narrator**: Golden light drifted around him, keeping watch over the eternal sanctum.

**Narrator**: As if none of it had ever happened.

**Narrator**: Only the door still glowed with its soft white light.

**Narrator**: Quietly waiting...

**Narrator**: Waiting for the next one to come in search of answers.
