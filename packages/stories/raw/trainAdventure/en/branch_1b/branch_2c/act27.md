# Act 27: The Way Home

**Narrator**: Consciousness slowly returned.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's head was swimming, and his eyelids were almost too heavy to lift.

**Narrator**: He forced his eyes open. Everything was a blur.

**Narrator**: It was a good while before his vision began to clear.

**Li Jie**: Where...

**Narrator**: He found himself lying on a cold floor.

**Narrator**: Around him was a cramped little room, all four walls bare gray-white concrete.

**Narrator**: There was nothing in it but a single heavy metal door.

**Narrator**: A fluorescent light on the ceiling gave off a weak, steady white glow.

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat up. His body felt stiff.

**Narrator**: He turned his head. Asuka was lying on the floor beside him, apparently not yet awake.

**Li Jie**: Asuka!

**Narrator**: He scrambled over to her and gently shook her shoulder.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, wake up!

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyelids fluttered a few times, then slowly opened.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... Mr. Li?

**Narrator**: Her voice was weak, her eyes unfocused.

**Li Jie**: Are you okay? Does anything hurt?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm... I'm fine... Just a little dizzy...

**Narrator**: Asuka sat up and looked around.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Where... are we?

**Li Jie**: I don't know either. We went through that door... and then we blacked out.

**Li Jie**: When I came to, we were in this room.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them got to their feet and looked the room over carefully.

**Narrator**: It was small, only about three meters on a side.

**Narrator**: The walls were concrete, rough and cold to the touch.

**Narrator**: The floor was smooth cement, a little damp.

**Narrator**: The only way out was that heavy metal door.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... do you think we're... back on Earth?

**Li Jie**: Maybe. This room does look... like something built on Earth.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached into his pocket and pulled out his phone.

**Narrator**: The screen was black and dead.

**Li Jie**: Battery's dead...

**Narrator**: He pressed the power button, but the phone still didn't respond.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, what about your phone?

**Narrator**: Asuka took out her phone too and tried to switch it on.

**Suzuki Asuka**: M-mine's dead too.

**Narrator**: She stared at the black screen, crestfallen.

**Suzuki Asuka**: We were in that world for so long... Of course our phones died ages ago.

**Li Jie**: Yeah... Looks like the first thing is to find some way out of this room.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie went to the metal door and took hold of the handle.

**Narrator**: He twisted it hard, but it wouldn't move an inch.

**Li Jie**: It's locked.

**Narrator**: He put more strength into it, but the door still wouldn't move an inch.

**Li Jie**: Damn it...

**Narrator**: Li Jie raised his foot and kicked the door, hard.

**Narrator**: A dull thud echoed around the room.

**Narrator**: But the heavy metal door didn't give at all. There wasn't so much as a dent.

**Li Jie**: Hey! Is anyone out there?!

**Narrator**: He shouted at the top of his voice, pounding on the door with his fist.

**Li Jie**: Anybody?! Let us out!

**Narrator**: The pounding echoed through the empty room, but no answer came.

**Narrator**: Li Jie kept at it for several minutes, until his hands were red.

**Narrator**: But beyond the door there was only silence. Not a sound.

**Li Jie**: (panting) Nothing...

---

**Narrator**: Asuka came to Li Jie's side, her face full of worry.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... why are we trapped in here?

**Suzuki Asuka**: We made it through that door and everything...

**Li Jie**: I don't know either.

**Narrator**: Li Jie frowned, turning over the possibilities.

**Li Jie**: Maybe... maybe this is some kind of isolation facility?

**Li Jie**: If we really are back on Earth, they might need to confirm who we are first.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Isolation...

**Narrator**: Asuka bit her lip, unease in her eyes.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... will they let us out?

**Suzuki Asuka**: What if... what if they just keep us locked up in here...

**Li Jie**: They won't.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned and took Asuka by the shoulders.

**Li Jie**: If this is Earth, then somebody has to be running this place.

**Li Jie**: They'll come sooner or later. All we have to do is wait.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay...

**Narrator**: Li Jie had said so, but the unease in Asuka's eyes never quite went away.

---

**Narrator**: The two of them found a corner of the room and sat down.

**Narrator**: The cold floor was uncomfortable, but it was a little better than standing.

**Narrator**: The minutes ticked by, one after another.

**Narrator**: In a room with no windows, there was no telling whether it was day or night outside.

**Narrator**: The fluorescent light on the ceiling kept up its steady white glow, never changing.

**Narrator**: Asuka leaned against the wall, arms wrapped around her knees.

**Narrator**: Her eyes had gone a little vacant, as if she were lost in thought.

**Li Jie**: What are you thinking about?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I was thinking... if we really are back on Earth...

**Suzuki Asuka**: then my family... how are they doing right now?

**Narrator**: Her voice caught.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I've been gone for so long. They must be worried sick...

**Suzuki Asuka**: Dad, Mom, and my little brother...

**Li Jie**: I'm sure they miss you terribly.

**Li Jie**: But you're back now. You'll see them soon.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes went red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... Misaki and the others...

**Suzuki Asuka**: They're never coming back...

**Narrator**: Her voice grew smaller and smaller.

**Suzuki Asuka**: How am I supposed to tell Misaki's parents...

**Suzuki Asuka**: How am I supposed to tell Kenta's family...

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie was silent for a moment. Then he gently took Asuka's hand.

**Li Jie**: Asuka... you did everything you could.

**Li Jie**: Their deaths aren't on you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But...

**Li Jie**: You made it back alive. That's the best ending there could be.

**Li Jie**: At least... you can tell their families their story.

**Li Jie**: Let them know their kids were brave right to the end.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked at Li Jie, tears sliding down her cheeks.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... thank you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: ...I'd never have made it this far without you.

**Li Jie**: I want to thank you too.

**Li Jie**: In that world, you gave me a reason to keep going.

**Narrator**: A faint flush rose to Asuka's cheeks.

**Narrator**: She turned to look at Li Jie, and their eyes met.

**Narrator**: The room was small and cold, and yet the space between them had never felt so close.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... I...

**Narrator**: She hesitated, as if there were something she wanted to say, but it reached her lips and she swallowed it back.

**Li Jie**: What is it?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I just wanted to say...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm really glad... I met you.

**Narrator**: Looking into Asuka's earnest eyes, Li Jie felt a warmth spread through his chest.

**Li Jie**: Me too. Meeting you was the luckiest thing that happened on that whole journey.

**Narrator**: Asuka gently rested her head on Li Jie's shoulder.

**Narrator**: Li Jie didn't push her away. He simply stayed with her, quietly.

**Narrator**: In that moment of uncertainty, each other's warmth was the only comfort they had.

---

**Narrator**: There was no knowing how much time had passed.

**Narrator**: Maybe half an hour. Maybe an hour.

**Narrator**: In a room without a clock, time had gone hazy.

**Narrator**: Suddenly, there were footsteps outside the door.

**Narrator**: Heavy, regular footsteps, like military boots striking the floor.

**Li Jie**: Someone's coming.

**Narrator**: The two of them sprang to their feet, eyeing the metal door warily.

**Narrator**: The footsteps stopped outside.

**Narrator**: Then came the sound of a key turning.

**Narrator**: With a click, the lock gave.

**Narrator**: The heavy metal door slowly swung outward.

**Narrator**: Standing in the doorway was a soldier in an olive-green uniform.

**Narrator**: He looked about thirty, burly, with a stern expression.

**Narrator**: He wore a sidearm at his hip and rank insignia on his shoulders.

**Soldier**: You two. Don't move.

**Narrator**: The soldier's voice was low and commanding.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka froze where they stood, not daring to make a move.

---

**Narrator**: The soldier took a phone from his pocket.

**Narrator**: He tapped the screen, apparently checking some kind of file.

**Narrator**: Then his eyes moved back and forth between Li Jie and Asuka, as if he were matching them against something.

**Narrator**: A few seconds later, the soldier raised the phone to his ear.

**Soldier**: This is Room 12.

**Soldier**: Two missing persons located. One male, one female.

**Narrator**: He paused and looked at Li Jie and Asuka again.

**Soldier**: Identifying features match.

**Soldier**: Male, approximately twenty-eight, East Asian.

**Soldier**: Female, approximately twenty, East Asian.

**Soldier**: Preliminary identification: missing persons Li Jie and Suzuki Asuka.

**Narrator**: At the sound of his own name, Li Jie's heart gave a violent lurch.

**Li Jie**: How... how do you know our names?

**Narrator**: The soldier didn't answer. He went on speaking into the phone.

**Soldier**: Yes, both are conscious, and their physical condition appears normal.

**Soldier**: Copy that. I'm taking them to the quarantine room now.

**Narrator**: The soldier ended the call and put the phone back in his pocket.

**Soldier**: Mr. Li Jie, Miss Suzuki Asuka.

**Soldier**: Please come with me. We're leaving this room.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie was taken aback.

**Narrator**: The soldier really did know their names.

**Narrator**: Did that mean... they really were back on Earth?

**Li Jie**: Wait, how did you...

**Soldier**: You'll be briefed on the details later.

**Soldier**: Right now, I need you to come with me. Immediately.

**Narrator**: The soldier's tone left no room for argument.

**Narrator**: Li Jie glanced at Asuka. Her eyes were full of confusion and unease.

**Li Jie**: Come with me, Asuka.

**Narrator**: He took her hand, to give her a little reassurance.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay...

**Narrator**: The two of them followed the soldier out of the room.

---

**Narrator**: Outside was a long corridor.

**Narrator**: Both sides were lined with metal doors just like theirs, each one marked with a number.

**Narrator**: The walls were clean white tile, the floor smooth cement.

**Narrator**: At the far end of the corridor was a glass door, and through it they could see outside.

**Narrator**: The soldier walked in front, Li Jie and Asuka close behind.

**Narrator**: The corridor was silent except for the echo of their footsteps.

**Narrator**: The nearer they drew to the glass door, the clearer the view beyond it became.

**Narrator**: Suddenly Asuka stopped in her tracks.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... look...

**Narrator**: Her voice trembled with emotion.

**Narrator**: Li Jie followed her gaze.

**Narrator**: Through the glass door lay a wide-open stretch of ground.

**Narrator**: And above it...

**Narrator**: ...a clear blue sky.

**Narrator**: And warm, bright sunlight.

---

**Narrator**: It was a sight they hadn't seen in months.

**Narrator**: Not violet moonlight. Not endless night.

**Narrator**: But real sunlight — the sunlight of Earth.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Sunlight...

**Narrator**: All at once, Asuka's eyes brimmed red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's sunlight...

**Narrator**: Her voice broke, and the tears spilled over before she could stop them.

**Narrator**: They weren't tears of grief, but of joy and release.

**Narrator**: Li Jie, too, felt a powerful wave of emotion surge up inside him.

**Narrator**: In that world, they had known nothing but the endless violet night and the twin moons.

**Narrator**: They had forgotten the warmth of sunlight, forgotten how beautiful a blue sky could be.

**Narrator**: And now, all of it had come back.

**Li Jie**: We're... really back.

**Narrator**: His voice was trembling too.

**Narrator**: Asuka clung to Li Jie's hand, the tears streaming down and refusing to stop.

**Suzuki Asuka**: We're back... We're really back...

**Narrator**: The soldier stopped and looked back at them.

**Narrator**: His expression was as stern as ever, but there was a flicker of understanding in his eyes.

**Soldier**: Yes. You're back.

**Soldier**: Welcome back to Earth.

**Narrator**: Through the glass door, Li Jie and Asuka gazed out at the sunlight they had gone so long without.

**Narrator**: Its warmth fell across their faces, a feeling as familiar as it was comforting.

**Narrator**: It was the light of hope.

**Narrator**: It was proof that they were home.

**Narrator**: After so many dangers, after losing so many companions...

**Narrator**: They had finally made it back to their own world.

**Narrator**: Back to a place with sunlight, with family, with a future.
