# Act 28: The Weight of the Truth

**Narrator**: The soldier led Li Jie and Suzuki Asuka out of the corridor and through several security doors.

**Narrator**: They followed a flagstone path outdoors, lined on both sides with neatly trimmed shrubs.

**Narrator**: Warm sunlight poured over them, and Asuka couldn't help closing her eyes to soak in a warmth she hadn't felt in so long.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so warm...

**Narrator**: Her voice shook a little.

**Narrator**: Ahead stood a building in the style of a European villa, its cream-white walls trimmed with delicate carved ornament.

**Narrator**: Gardens surrounded it, planted with every kind of flower.

**Narrator**: After the cold isolation facility they had just left, the place felt warm and welcoming.

**Soldier**: This is it. Mr. Shimizu is waiting for you inside.

**Narrator**: The soldier pushed open the villa's front door and motioned them in.

---

**Narrator**: Inside, the villa was elegantly furnished, its living room spacious and bright.

**Narrator**: Sunlight streamed in through the floor-to-ceiling windows, laying warm patches of light across the wooden floor.

**Narrator**: A man of about fifty was sitting on the sofa. When he saw them come in, he rose to his feet.

**Narrator**: He was of medium build and wore a dark blue suit, his hair neatly combed and already white at the temples.

**Narrator**: He wore a mild smile, with faint crow's feet at the corners of his eyes.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: So you've finally arrived. Please, have a seat. There's no need to stand on ceremony.

**Narrator**: His voice was gentle and rich, with a quality that put people at ease.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka exchanged a glance, then sat down on the sofa, a little hesitantly.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: (to the soldier) You may go. I'll speak with them alone.

**Soldier**: Yes, Director Shimizu.

**Narrator**: The soldier saluted, turned, and left the living room, closing the door behind him.

---

**Narrator**: Only the three of them remained in the room.

**Narrator**: The man sat back down on the sofa and picked up a teapot from the coffee table.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Have some tea. It's Japanese green tea. It should be to your taste.

**Narrator**: He deftly poured three cups and slid two of them across to Li Jie and Asuka.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Th... thank you.

**Narrator**: Asuka lifted the cup carefully. The scent of the tea felt familiar, and it settled her a little.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Shimizu Kosuke. I'm a senior official with ISPA.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: ISPA is the International Supernatural Phenomena Agency.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: We specialize in investigating and handling supernatural incidents around the world.

**Li Jie**: An international organization...

**Narrator**: Suddenly it all made sense to Li Jie.

**Li Jie**: So that's how you knew our names, and that we'd gone missing.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Exactly. Sixteen people vanished at the same moment, and that drew our attention.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The train itself never disappeared. But the sixteen passengers aboard it simply vanished into thin air.

**Narrator**: Li Jie frowned at that.

**Li Jie**: Wait... the train didn't disappear?

**Li Jie**: But from where we sat, the whole train went into that world...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Yes. That's the strangest part of this whole case.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: As far as the outside world could tell, the train ran as normal. It was only the sixteen passengers aboard who suddenly vanished.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: But for you, the entire train was transported to another world.

**Narrator**: Kosuke paused, and a deep shadow came into his eyes.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The reason I've followed this case so closely... is that my sister was one of those sixteen.

---

**Narrator**: Kosuke took a photograph from the inside pocket of his suit jacket and laid it on the coffee table.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. Li, there's something I'd like to ask you.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: In that world... did you ever see this girl?

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the photograph.

**Narrator**: It was an ID photo of a young woman.

**Narrator**: She looked about twenty, with a delicate, fine-featured face and short black hair that fell to her shoulders.

**Narrator**: Her eyes were gentle and calm, and the corners of her mouth turned up in a faint smile.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's pupils contracted.

**Li Jie**: This is...

**Narrator**: He knew that face.

**Narrator**: It was the quiet girl — the first one to choose to get off the train at Jakkai Station.

**Narrator**: The girl who had been so timid and fearful, and yet in the end had found the courage to step out through the doors.

---

**Li Jie**: I... I've seen her.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Where? Is she alive?

**Narrator**: Kosuke's voice turned urgent, and he leaned forward a little.

**Narrator**: Li Jie was silent for a moment, his expression growing heavy.

**Li Jie**: When we reached the first station — Jakkai — she chose to get off.

**Li Jie**: A few people decided to leave the train then. She was one of them.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: And then? What happened after that?

**Li Jie**: We... we went our separate ways. I chose to stay on the train.

**Li Jie**: But later... a few months later, in a forest up north, we...

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath.

**Li Jie**: ...found her body.

---

**Narrator**: Silence fell over the living room.

**Narrator**: Kosuke's face froze, and his eyes went empty.

**Narrator**: He sank slowly back into the sofa, closed his eyes, and let out a long, deep sigh.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I see...

**Narrator**: His voice had gone hoarse.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The truth is... I prepared myself for this a long time ago.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: After so many years, the odds of anyone coming back alive...

**Narrator**: He didn't finish. He only shook his head.

**Narrator**: It was a long while before Kosuke opened his eyes again.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Did she... did she suffer?

**Li Jie**: ...I'm sorry. I don't know the details.

**Li Jie**: By the time we found her, a lot of time had already gone by.

**Narrator**: Li Jie kept the crueler details to himself.

**Narrator**: He didn't want this man to know just how gruesome the state of those bodies had been.

---

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I understand. Thank you for telling me.

**Narrator**: Kosuke took a handkerchief from his pocket and dabbed at the corners of his eyes.

**Narrator**: Asuka spoke up in a small voice.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Shimizu... your sister's name was...?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Shimizu Emi. She went missing thirty-eight years ago.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's head jerked up, his eyes full of confusion.

**Li Jie**: Wait... thirty-eight years ago?

**Li Jie**: But... but she only looked about twenty.

**Li Jie**: If she's your sister...

**Narrator**: Li Jie's mind raced through the numbers.

**Narrator**: The man in front of him was about fifty. If his sister had gone missing thirty-eight years ago...

**Narrator**: Back then, she would have been only about twenty herself.

**Narrator**: But how could that be?

---

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Ah...

**Narrator**: Something seemed to dawn on Kosuke, and understanding crossed his face.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Forgive me. I forgot to tell you something very important.

**Narrator**: He set down his teacup and looked at Li Jie and Asuka gravely.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: You asked just now how long my sister has been missing, and I said thirty-eight years.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: But you may not know yet... what year it is.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's heart began to race.

**Narrator**: A sense of foreboding welled up inside him.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: It's 2043.

**Narrator**: The words struck like a thunderbolt, leaving Li Jie and Asuka utterly stunned.

**Suzuki Asuka**: 2043?!

**Li Jie**: Then... then we...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Yes. You disappeared in 2025.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Eighteen years have passed since that day.

---

**Narrator**: The color drained from Asuka's face.

**Narrator**: The teacup slipped from her hand and hit the floor with a crack, splashing tea everywhere.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Eighteen years... I've been missing for eighteen years...

**Narrator**: Her voice was shaking badly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then... then my mom and dad... are they...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. and Mrs. Suzuki are both well. They've been waiting for you to come home all this time.

**Narrator**: At that, Asuka let out a breath of relief.

**Narrator**: But Kosuke's gaze turned to Li Jie, and there was an apology in his eyes.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. Li... your mother is well, but your father...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: He passed away in an accident eight years ago. I'm very sorry.

**Narrator**: A slight shudder ran through Li Jie.

**Li Jie**: ...I see.

**Narrator**: His voice was calm, but Asuka could feel his palm turn faintly cold.

**Narrator**: She gently took his hand, offering him silent support.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned to look at her, gratitude in his eyes.

---

**Narrator**: Asuka took a deep breath, tears sliding down her cheeks.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Eighteen years... My little brother's thirty-three now...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I missed him growing up... I missed so much...

**Narrator**: Li Jie held her hand tight, but he felt a wave of dizziness himself.

**Narrator**: Eighteen years... He should be forty-six by now.

**Narrator**: His father was gone. And his mother — how old must she be now?

**Narrator**: His friends, his coworkers — everyone's lives had kept moving forward.

**Narrator**: Only the two of them had been left stranded outside of time.

---

**Narrator**: Kosuke rose and went over to a cabinet at the side of the room.

**Narrator**: He took out a box of tissues and handed it to Asuka.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I know this is hard to accept. But at least... you came back.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That's far more fortunate than my sister was.

**Narrator**: There was a trace of bitterness in his voice.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: When she disappeared, I was only fifteen.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Thirty-eight years of waiting, and I went from a boy to a middle-aged man.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: And she... will be twenty forever.

**Narrator**: The only sounds left in the living room were Asuka's weeping and the birdsong outside the window.

**Narrator**: The sunlight still streamed warmly in.

**Narrator**: But hidden in that warm light was a rift in time that could never be mended.

**Narrator**: Eighteen blank years, stretched out between them and this world.
