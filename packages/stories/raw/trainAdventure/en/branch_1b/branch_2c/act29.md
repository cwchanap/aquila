# Act 29: Rest in the Hot-Spring Town

**Narrator**: After a brief silence in the living room, Kosuke sat up straight again.

**Narrator**: His eyes had regained their professional calm, though the rims were still a little red and swollen.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. Li, there's one more thing I'd like to confirm.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Besides the two of you... did any of the other passengers make it back alive?

**Narrator**: Li Jie faltered for a moment. He still wasn't used to the leap in time.

**Li Jie**: No. For us... only a few months have gone by.

**Li Jie**: But apart from the two of us, everyone else... none of them made it back.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: And the others? Are they...

**Narrator**: There was a trace of hope in Kosuke's voice, but far more unease.

**Li Jie**: Most of them were... were killed.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's voice grew heavy.

**Li Jie**: There are a few we never found out what happened to. But...

**Li Jie**: Considering how dangerous that world is — especially with the black-sword man out there...

**Li Jie**: I don't think they're still alive.

---

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The black-sword man?

**Narrator**: Kosuke's brow creased slightly.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Who is that?

**Li Jie**: Someone incredibly powerful... I'm not even sure he counts as human.

**Li Jie**: He carries a long black sword, and he's far stronger than any ordinary person.

**Li Jie**: We suspect most of the passengers died by his hand.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Something that dangerous... Then how did you get away from him?

**Li Jie**: We met the elves. Their leader, Ailaru, protected us.

**Li Jie**: In the end... Ailaru gave his own life to bring the black-sword man down.

**Narrator**: A flicker of sorrow passed through Li Jie's eyes.

**Narrator**: Ailaru's death still weighed heavily on his heart.

---

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The elves...

**Narrator**: Kosuke nodded thoughtfully.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That world is even more complicated than we imagined.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That you made it back alive is nothing short of a miracle.

**Narrator**: He stood and bowed deeply to Li Jie and Asuka.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Thank you for being willing to tell me all this.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: News of my sister, and of the other missing persons... it means a great deal to us.

**Li Jie**: It's the least we could do.

**Narrator**: Asuka gave a small nod as well.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If it helps at all... we're glad to.

---

**Narrator**: Kosuke sat back down and picked up a document from the coffee table.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Now, let me explain the arrangements we've made for you.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: You'll need to stay at the inn here for one week.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: During that time, we'll give you both thorough medical examinations.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: There will also be some psychological evaluations and readjustment training.

**Narrator**: A little nervously, Asuka spoke up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And after a week... we can go home?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Yes. Your families have already been notified.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: They know you've come back safely, and you'll be able to see them in a week.

**Narrator**: At that, Asuka's eyes lit up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?!

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Yes. Rest assured.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked out the window. Sunlight was falling across the lush green garden.

**Narrator**: In the distance rose a rolling line of mountains, and there seemed to be a faint smell of sulfur in the air.

**Li Jie**: Mr. Shimizu, may I ask... where are we?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: You're in the Hokkaido countryside.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: In a quiet hot-spring town, far from the noise of the city.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: We chose it in the hope that you could readjust gradually, somewhere peaceful.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The inn has hot-spring baths, so you'll be able to truly relax.

**Narrator**: Asuka's face lit up with delighted surprise.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Hot springs?!

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Yes. The springs here are famous, and very good for the body.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: After everything you've been through, you've earned a proper rest.

---

**Narrator**: Kosuke pressed the intercom on the table.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Kobayashi, come in for a moment.

**Narrator**: A moment later, the soldier from before pushed open the door and came in.

**Soldier**: Yes, Director Shimizu.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Take them to the inn. Give them the best room there is.

**Soldier**: Sir.

**Narrator**: Kosuke turned to Li Jie and Asuka with a warm smile.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Rest well this week.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: If you need anything, just tell the staff.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: We'll do everything we can to see to your needs.

**Li Jie**: Thank you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you, Mr. Shimizu.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka followed the soldier out of the villa.

**Narrator**: They walked along the flagstone path through a meticulously tended Japanese garden.

**Narrator**: After they crossed a small bridge, a traditional Japanese inn came into view ahead.

**Narrator**: The inn was built of wood, with dark roof tiles and white walls.

**Narrator**: A large noren hung over the entrance, its three characters spelling out the name: Yu no Yado.

**Soldier**: Here we are.

**Narrator**: The soldier pushed open the inn's front door.

**Narrator**: The entryway was laid with soft tatami, and the air carried a faint scent of wood.

**Narrator**: A middle-aged woman in a kimono came out to greet them.

**Innkeeper**: Welcome. This way, please, both of you.

---

**Narrator**: The innkeeper led them up to the second floor.

**Narrator**: Paper sliding doors lined both sides of the hallway, and the wooden floorboards creaked softly underfoot.

**Narrator**: The innkeeper stopped at one of the doors and slid it open.

**Innkeeper**: This is your room. If there's anything you need, please call for us at any time.

**Narrator**: The room was spacious and elegant.

**Narrator**: Tatami covered the floor, and a low table sat by the window.

**Narrator**: Through the window they could see the mountains, and white steam rising in the distance.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so pretty...

**Narrator**: Asuka went to the window, her eyes shining as she took in the view.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, look! The hot springs are over there!

**Li Jie**: Yeah, I see them.

---

**Narrator**: The soldier stood in the doorway and spoke respectfully.

**Soldier**: Please rest well, both of you. If you need any assistance, press the call button in your room.

**Soldier**: Dinner is at six. Someone will come to let you know.

**Li Jie**: Got it. Thanks.

**Narrator**: The soldier and the innkeeper bowed and left, and the paper door slid softly shut.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka were alone in the room.

**Narrator**: Asuka wandered around the room, too excited to keep still.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I can't believe it... We really made it back.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And we get to stay at a hot-spring inn!

**Suzuki Asuka**: In a week... in a week I get to see Mom and Dad and my little brother!

**Narrator**: Her face was glowing with happiness.

---

**Narrator**: Li Jie stood by the window, quietly gazing out at the mountains.

**Narrator**: The warm sunlight, the gentle breeze, the steam from the distant hot springs...

**Narrator**: Everything was so peaceful, so lovely.

**Narrator**: After so many ordeals, they could finally rest somewhere this tranquil.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li?

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice broke into Li Jie's thoughts.

**Narrator**: She came to his side and looked at him with concern.

**Suzuki Asuka**: What are you thinking about? You look like something's weighing on you...

**Li Jie**: It's nothing. It's just...

**Narrator**: Li Jie trailed off.

**Narrator**: He thought of the news that his father was gone, and a tangle of emotions rose inside him.

**Li Jie**: I'm just not used to being back yet. And... my father.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm sorry...

**Narrator**: Asuka gently took Li Jie's hand.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But at least... we made it back safe.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Your mom must be dying to see you.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked into Asuka's sincere eyes and gave a small nod.

**Li Jie**: ...Yeah. You're right.

**Narrator**: Asuka leaned her head on Li Jie's shoulder, and the two of them quietly watched the view outside the window.

---

**Narrator**: Outside, the sun was slowly sinking in the west.

**Narrator**: Warm orange light spilled over the mountains, dyeing the whole sky gold.

**Narrator**: The steam from the distant hot springs shimmered with a faint gold in the setting sun.

**Narrator**: Everything was so beautiful, so still.

**Narrator**: In this hot-spring town, far from the bustle of the world, they had at last found a moment of peace.

**Narrator**: In a week, they would be reunited with their families.

**Narrator**: And go back to the lives that truly belonged to them.

**Narrator**: Their long, hard journey had at last come to its end.
