# Act 30: Night Talk by the Hot Spring

**Narrator**: It was their third night at the hot-spring inn.

**Narrator**: After a few days of rest, Li Jie and Asuka both looked much healthier.

**Narrator**: Their medical checkups and psychological evaluations had gone smoothly, too.

**Narrator**: After dinner, the two of them sat in their room drinking tea.

**Narrator**: Night had fallen outside the window, and in the distance white steam rose from the baths.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li, we still haven't been to the hot springs together.

**Narrator**: Asuka set down her teacup and looked at Li Jie, her eyes shining.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Want to go tonight? I heard the open-air bath is especially beautiful at night.

**Li Jie**: Sure. I could use a chance to really unwind, too.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yay! Then let's meet at nine, in the garden by the open-air bath?

**Li Jie**: All right. It's a deal.

**Narrator**: Asuka smiled happily, her face bright with anticipation.

**Narrator**: At nine o'clock, Li Jie changed into the yukata the inn had laid out and made his way to the men's bath.

**Narrator**: Steam curled off the water, and the air was heavy with the smell of sulfur.

**Narrator**: He lowered himself slowly into the spring, and the hot water closed around him.

**Narrator**: The warmth sank deep into his body, and his knotted muscles slowly began to loosen.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) This feels amazing...

**Narrator**: Li Jie leaned back against the edge of the pool and closed his eyes.

**Narrator**: The examinations and interviews of the past few days had all gone smoothly.

**Narrator**: The ISPA people had treated them kindly, with no coercion and no threats of any kind.

**Narrator**: In four more days, they would be going home.

**Narrator**: Back to real, everyday life.

**Narrator**: And yet, deep in Li Jie's heart, there was a tangle of feelings he couldn't quite put into words.

**Narrator**: After soaking for about twenty minutes, Li Jie climbed out of the bath.

**Narrator**: He changed back into his yukata, pulled a light haori over it, and set off for the garden beside the open-air bath.

**Narrator**: The garden glowed under soft lamps and was planted with flowers and greenery of every kind.

**Narrator**: In the middle lay a small pond, with a few wooden benches set along its edge.

**Narrator**: Asuka was already there, waiting.

**Narrator**: She wore a pale blue yukata, and her long hair fell damp over her shoulders.

**Narrator**: Her cheeks were faintly flushed from the hot water.

**Li Jie**: Asuka. Sorry to keep you waiting.

**Narrator**: Asuka turned, and when she saw Li Jie her face broke into a dazzling smile.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li! I only just got here myself.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Come sit, quick! The view from here at night is gorgeous!

**Narrator**: Li Jie sat down beside her.

**Narrator**: From here they could see the distant mountains, and a sky full of stars.

**Narrator**: With no city lights to wash them out, the stars shone especially bright.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's been so long since I've seen this many stars...

**Narrator**: Asuka tipped her head back to look at the sky, starlight caught in her eyes.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Back in that world, there were only the violet moons.

**Suzuki Asuka**: They were pretty too, but... I still like the stars in our world better.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. This is the night sky we know.

**Narrator**: They sat side by side, quietly taking in the stars.

**Narrator**: The only sounds in the garden were the chirping of insects and the distant murmur of the hot springs. It was wonderfully peaceful.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li...

**Narrator**: Asuka spoke softly, breaking the silence.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Have you thought about... what happens after we go back?

**Li Jie**: I have. Seeing my mother, sorting things out with work...

**Li Jie**: There's a lot to get used to. Eighteen years have gone by out there, after all.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yeah... eighteen years.

**Narrator**: Asuka lowered her head and looked down at her hands.

**Suzuki Asuka**: My little brother's thirty-three now. He might even be married with kids.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I've become... I've become an auntie.

**Narrator**: She said it with a rueful smile.

**Li Jie**: You're still twenty. It's only the world that's changed.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... all my classmates are thirty-eight now.

**Suzuki Asuka**: They're all grown-ups... with their own families, their own careers...

**Suzuki Asuka**: And me... I'm still stuck at twenty.

**Narrator**: Asuka's voice caught in her throat.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and gently took her hand.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, don't overthink it.

**Li Jie**: No matter how much the world has changed, your family is still waiting for you.

**Li Jie**: They'll be overjoyed to see you.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked up, her eyes a little red.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you... Mr. Li.

**Suzuki Asuka**: All this time, I was so lucky to have you by my side.

**Narrator**: She gave his hand a soft squeeze.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Honestly... I'd never have made it this far without you.

**Li Jie**: Same goes for me.

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked into Asuka's eyes.

**Li Jie**: In that world, you gave me a reason to keep going.

**Li Jie**: Your smile, your strength... they're what carried me all the way to the end.

**Narrator**: Asuka's cheeks turned redder still, though whether from the lingering heat of the bath or from shyness, it was hard to say.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I'm not strong at all...

**Suzuki Asuka**: You were the one protecting me the whole way. All I ever did was cry...

**Li Jie**: That was your strength, too.

**Li Jie**: Somewhere that terrifying, you never gave up.

**Li Jie**: You held on right to the end, and you made it back with me.

**Narrator**: Asuka bowed her head, and tears slid down her cheeks.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li...

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached out and gently wiped her tears away.

**Li Jie**: Don't cry. This is a happy moment, isn't it?

**Li Jie**: We made it back alive. We'll be seeing our families soon.

**Narrator**: Asuka gave a firm nod and smiled through her tears.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... You're right.

**Narrator**: The two of them sat on side by side, gazing up at the starry sky.

**Narrator**: A night breeze drifted past, carrying the scent of flowers and grass.

**Narrator**: Their hands were still clasped tight. Neither of them let go.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... after we go back, can we still see each other?

**Narrator**: Asuka asked in a small voice.

**Li Jie**: Of course. We're both in Tokyo. It'll be easy to meet up.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really...? I'm so glad.

**Li Jie**: Besides... I don't want us to just go our separate ways.

**Li Jie**: After everything we've been through, you've... become someone really important to me.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes went wide, and her heart began to race.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li...

**Narrator**: Her voice trembled.

**Li Jie**: Asuka, I know it might be too soon to be saying this.

**Li Jie**: You're only twenty, and I'm already... I may look twenty-eight, but really I ought to count as forty-six by now.

**Li Jie**: That's a big age gap, and you still have to get used to this world all over again...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I don't care!

**Narrator**: Asuka cut him off.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I don't care about age!

**Suzuki Asuka**: To me, you're Mr. Li, and you're twenty-eight.

**Suzuki Asuka**: You're the one who protected me and stayed by my side the whole time in that world.

**Narrator**: Asuka took his hand in both of hers.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I don't want to be apart from you either.

**Suzuki Asuka**: All this time, I've gotten used to having you beside me.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If we suddenly went our separate ways... I'd be so lonely.

**Narrator**: Looking into Asuka's earnest eyes, Li Jie felt the last of his doubts slip away.

**Li Jie**: Then... after we go back, let's stay in touch.

**Li Jie**: Once you're back with your family, and you've settled into your new life...

**Li Jie**: I'd like us to start seeing each other. Properly. Would that be all right?

**Narrator**: Asuka's face went bright red in an instant.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Y... yes... it would...

**Narrator**: Her voice came out as faint as a mosquito's hum.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'd... I'd really like that.

**Narrator**: Li Jie smiled and gently drew Asuka into his arms.

**Narrator**: Asuka didn't resist. She simply rested her head on his shoulder.

**Narrator**: They held each other beneath the stars, feeling each other's warmth.

**Narrator**: After facing death together so many times, what they felt for each other had long since gone beyond ordinary companionship.

**Narrator**: In that dangerous world, they had leaned on each other and survived together.

**Narrator**: And now, at last, in a world at peace, they could be honest about their feelings.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li...

**Li Jie**: Hm?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you... for liking me.

**Li Jie**: I should be the one thanking you.

**Narrator**: They held each other in silence, savoring the calm and lovely night.

**Narrator**: After a while, Asuka slowly lifted her head.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mr. Li... can I ask you something?

**Li Jie**: What is it?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Could I... could I call you by your name?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Not "Mr. Li," but... Jie.

**Narrator**: Asuka's cheeks flushed, and her voice grew very small.

**Li Jie**: Of course you can.

**Narrator**: Li Jie smiled and gently patted her head.

**Li Jie**: And I'd like to call you Asuka. Just Asuka, not Miss Suzuki.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay... then... Jie.

**Narrator**: Asuka tried the name out, and her face went even redder.

**Li Jie**: Mm. I'm right here, Asuka.

**Narrator**: They looked at each other and smiled, and felt the distance between them close a little more.

**Narrator**: Time flowed quietly by at the hot-spring inn.
