# Act 31: Unhurried Days at the Inn

**Narrator**: Time flowed quietly by at the hot-spring inn.

**Narrator**: On the morning of the fourth day, the two of them took a walk around the inn together.

**Narrator**: A flagstone path wound through the Japanese garden, lined on both sides with hydrangeas and maples.

**Narrator**: The maples hadn't turned yet, but the lush green was every bit as soothing to the heart.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie, look over there!

**Narrator**: Asuka pointed at the koi gliding lazily through the pond.

**Suzuki Asuka**: They're huge! And their colors are so pretty!

**Li Jie**: Yeah, they really are.

**Narrator**: Watching Asuka's happy smile, Li Jie felt a rush of warmth in his chest.

**Narrator**: An ordinary, lovely day like this would have been unimaginable in that other world.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie, let's feed the fish, okay?

**Li Jie**: Sure.

**Narrator**: They fetched some fish food from the inn and crouched at the edge of the pond to feed them.

**Narrator**: The koi came jostling over, sending ripple after ripple across the surface.

**Narrator**: Asuka laughed with delight, and her smile was so infectious that Li Jie caught it too.

**Narrator**: Around noon, they strolled through the little town near the inn.

**Narrator**: It was a quiet place, with only a handful of traditional shops and restaurants.

**Narrator**: They wandered into a tiny souvenir shop.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Wow, there's so much cute stuff!

**Narrator**: Asuka picked up a little carved wooden owl.

**Suzuki Asuka**: This is so cute... Should we get one as a keepsake?

**Li Jie**: Sure. Let's each get one, to remember this trip by.

**Narrator**: They each picked out a small ornament.

**Narrator**: Asuka chose the owl, and Li Jie chose a carved wooden fox.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Whenever I see this, I'll remember our time here.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. I'll treasure it.

**Narrator**: On the evening of the fifth day, the inn served them an exquisite kaiseki dinner.

**Narrator**: Course after course was carried in, each one a work of art.

**Narrator**: Fresh sashimi, grilled dishes, simmered dishes, fried dishes...

**Narrator**: Every one of them captured the very essence of Japanese cuisine.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's been so long since I've eaten anything this delicious...

**Narrator**: Asuka closed her eyes in contentment.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Back in that world, just getting enough to eat was good enough.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I never imagined that once we got back, we'd get to enjoy food like this.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. We really are lucky.

**Narrator**: Li Jie raised his sake cup.

**Li Jie**: To making it back safe. Cheers.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Cheers!

**Narrator**: They touched cups lightly, and the clear clink rang through the room.

**Narrator**: After dinner, the two of them sat out on the room's balcony.

**Narrator**: The distant mountains were hazy against the night, and the steam from the hot springs shimmered in the moonlight.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie... have you ever wondered what would have happened if we'd made different choices back then?

**Li Jie**: What do you mean?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Like... if I'd chosen to get off the train.

**Suzuki Asuka**: If you'd gotten off too... would we have met Miss Shimizu?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Would we... have met the same fate?

**Narrator**: Li Jie was silent for a moment.

**Li Jie**: Maybe. But that's all just "what if" now.

**Li Jie**: What matters is that we made the right choice, and we made it back alive.

**Li Jie**: And... we found each other.

**Narrator**: Asuka leaned gently against his shoulder.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yeah... I'm really glad I met you.

**Narrator**: On the afternoon of the sixth day, the two of them sat reading on a bench in the garden.

**Narrator**: The inn's library had plenty of books, and each of them had borrowed a few.

**Narrator**: Sunlight filtered through the leaves in dappled patches, and a light breeze stirred the air.

**Narrator**: An afternoon this leisurely left them more relaxed than they had ever been.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie, what are you reading?

**Li Jie**: A mystery novel. You?

**Suzuki Asuka**: A travel magazine.

**Narrator**: Asuka held the magazine up in front of him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Look, it has all these places to visit in Japan.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Once we've both settled in, let's go on a trip together, okay?

**Li Jie**: Sure. Where do you want to go?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Kyoto! I want to see Kinkaku-ji, and Kiyomizu-dera.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And I want to feed the deer in Nara, and see the sunrise on Mount Fuji...

**Narrator**: Asuka chattered on excitedly, her eyes shining.

**Li Jie**: Sounds good. Then it's a promise.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm! It's a promise!

**Narrator**: As the sun went down, the two of them came out to the garden again.

**Narrator**: An orange-red sunset set the whole sky aflame.

**Narrator**: The distant mountains looked especially majestic in the evening light.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so beautiful...

**Narrator**: Asuka took out her phone, wanting to capture the view.

**Narrator**: Then she remembered: her phone had died long ago.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Oh... I forgot my phone doesn't work.

**Li Jie**: That's okay. Some views are better kept in your heart.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently wrapped his arms around Asuka from behind.

**Li Jie**: Besides... as long as we're together, we'll have plenty more chances to see beautiful things.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm...

**Narrator**: Asuka turned around to face him.

**Narrator**: The glow of the setting sun fell over them, stretching their shadows long across the ground.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie... thank you.

**Li Jie**: For what?

**Suzuki Asuka**: For... making me believe there are still so many wonderful things waiting in the future.

**Suzuki Asuka**: For making me not afraid anymore.

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently stroked her hair.

**Li Jie**: Silly. I should be the one thanking you.

**Li Jie**: You're the one who brought color back into my life.

**Narrator**: The seventh day was their last full day at the inn.

**Narrator**: In the morning they soaked in the hot springs together; at noon they sat over tea.

**Narrator**: In the afternoon they stayed in their room, talking over their plans for when they got back.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I want to spend some time with my family first.

**Suzuki Asuka**: After all... I made them worry for so long.

**Li Jie**: Of course. Family comes first.

**Suzuki Asuka**: What about you?

**Li Jie**: I have to go see my mother too. And there's a lot I'll need to take care of.

**Li Jie**: Work, a place to live... It could take a while.

**Suzuki Asuka**: So... how long before we can see each other again?

**Narrator**: Asuka asked, a little uneasily.

**Li Jie**: It shouldn't be too long. A month? Two?

**Li Jie**: Once we've both settled in, I'll come find you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Okay... then I'll wait for you.

**Narrator**: Asuka held out her little finger.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Pinky promise. No take-backs.

**Narrator**: Li Jie laughed and hooked his little finger around hers.

**Li Jie**: Okay. Pinky promise.

**Narrator**: That night, they went to the hot springs together for the last time.

**Narrator**: They sat side by side on the garden bench, looking up at the stars.

**Narrator**: They didn't say much. They simply kept each other quiet company.

**Narrator**: Hand in hand, feeling each other's warmth.

**Narrator**: This week had been more precious to them than words could say.

**Narrator**: In this hot-spring town far from the noise of the world, they had found peace within themselves.

**Narrator**: And they had become sure of each other's hearts.

**Narrator**: Tomorrow they would leave this place and return to their own lives.

**Narrator**: But they knew this wasn't an ending.

**Narrator**: It was a new beginning.

**Narrator**: And so, the week flew by.
