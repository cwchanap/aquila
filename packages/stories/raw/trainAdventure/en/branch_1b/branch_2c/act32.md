# Act 32: Farewells and Words of Caution

**Narrator**: The morning they left the inn was bright and sunny.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka finished packing and came down to the inn's entrance hall.

**Narrator**: Shimizu Kosuke was already there, waiting for them.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Did the two of you rest well this week?

**Li Jie**: Very well. Thank you for taking such good care of us.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really, thank you so much. This place was amazing.

**Narrator**: Kosuke smiled and nodded.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I'm glad to hear it. Before you go, there are a few things I need to tell you.

**Narrator**: Kosuke took two documents out of his briefcase.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: This explains the living allowance the agency has arranged for you.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Four hundred thousand yen a month, for one year.

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes went wide with surprise.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Four hundred thousand?!

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That's right. You were missing for eighteen years, and readjusting to this world will take time.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: With this money, you won't need to worry about living expenses for a while.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: You can concentrate on adjusting to your new surroundings and taking care of family and personal matters.

**Narrator**: Kosuke pointed out the details in the documents.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: We've already opened bank accounts for you.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The allowance will be deposited automatically on the first of every month.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Here are your bank cards and the paperwork that goes with them.

**Li Jie**: Thank you. This really is a huge help.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took the documents and the card, and felt a surge of gratitude.

**Narrator**: Next, Kosuke took two elegant boxes out of his briefcase.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: And these. Your new phones.

**Narrator**: Asuka opened her box, and her eyes lit up at once.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Whoa! It's a GPhone 35 Pro Max!

**Suzuki Asuka**: This is the newest model, isn't it?! I saw it in a magazine!

**Narrator**: She picked up the phone excitedly, turning it over and over in her hands.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so pretty... and so light!

**Narrator**: Li Jie opened his box too, and looked at the brand-new phone inside.

**Narrator**: Then he took his own broken phone out of his pocket.

**Narrator**: A GPhone 16, released in 2024.

**Li Jie**: (sighs) Just eighteen years... and they've gone from the 16 to the 35.

**Narrator**: He shook his head with a wry smile.

**Li Jie**: This world sure changes fast.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: It does. Sometimes even we can't keep up with how fast technology moves.

**Narrator**: Kosuke's expression grew serious.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Please make sure you remember what I'm about to tell you.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka both looked at Kosuke intently.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: My advice is... if at all possible, stay away from Tokyo and Osaka.

**Narrator**: Li Jie frowned.

**Li Jie**: Why?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: There's nothing wrong with the cities themselves.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: But... there may be other organizations looking for you.

**Narrator**: A wave of unease came over Li Jie.

**Li Jie**: Other organizations... meaning?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I'm sorry. That's as much as I can say.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: But we can't guarantee your safety in those big cities.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: In places that crowded, it's hard for us to keep a full watch.

**Narrator**: Kosuke's tone was firm. He clearly wasn't going to say any more.

**Narrator**: Asuka spoke up, sounding a little worried.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Then... what about my family? Are they still living in Tokyo?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Don't worry. Your family has moved to Fukuoka.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Ever since they heard you'd come back safely, they've been looking forward to seeing you again.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Fukuoka...

**Narrator**: Asuka let out a breath of relief, though she still looked a little puzzled.

**Suzuki Asuka**: When did they move there?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Five years ago. For work.

**Narrator**: Kosuke turned to Li Jie.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. Li, your mother is living on her own in Zhuhai now.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: She's in fairly good health, and we've already let her know that you've come back safely.

**Li Jie**: Zhuhai...

**Narrator**: Li Jie fell silent for a moment.

**Narrator**: His mother had left Tokyo and was living alone in China.

**Narrator**: And his father was already gone.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Given your circumstances, the agency has arranged housing for you in Fukuoka.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Mr. Li, we've secured an apartment for you for one year.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: It isn't far from Miss Suzuki's home.

**Narrator**: Li Jie glanced at Asuka. Her cheeks had turned faintly pink.

**Li Jie**: Thank you for arranging all this.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That way, you can look out for each other.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Readjusting to this world won't be easy. Having each other close will help a great deal.

**Narrator**: Li Jie thought for a moment, then asked again.

**Li Jie**: Mr. Shimizu, can you at least tell me...

**Li Jie**: Which organizations are looking for us? And why?

**Narrator**: Kosuke shook his head.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I'm sorry. That's classified.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: All I can say is that the very fact that you came back from that world...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: ...is enough to draw the interest of certain parties.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: For your own safety, please try to keep a low profile.

**Narrator**: Unease welled up in Li Jie's heart.

**Narrator**: Coming back from another world had somehow made them the target of certain organizations.

**Narrator**: But from the look on Kosuke's face, he clearly wasn't going to say any more.

**Li Jie**: ...I understand. We'll be careful.

**Narrator**: Kosuke took two business cards from his pocket and handed one each to Li Jie and Asuka.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: This is how to reach me.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: If you run into any problems after you get back, or if you remember anything important...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: feel free to contact me anytime.

**Li Jie**: All right. Thank you.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took the card and put it carefully away.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Also... there may come a time when we need your help.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: More information about that world, for instance, or some other related investigation...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I may get in touch with you. It won't be mandatory, of course.

**Li Jie**: I understand. If we can help, we'll be glad to cooperate.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Yes. If there's anything we can do...

**Narrator**: Kosuke's expression grew graver still.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: There's one more very important thing.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Please don't speak of that world to anyone.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka both looked at Kosuke intently.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: That includes your families and your friends. Don't tell anyone at all.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: If anyone asks where you've been all this time...

**Shimizu Kosuke**: just say you were in hospital being treated, with no memory of it.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: We'll prepare the necessary medical certificates for you.

**Li Jie**: Understood. We'll be careful.

**Suzuki Asuka**: But... we can't even tell our families?

**Shimizu Kosuke**: No. For your safety, and for your families' safety as well.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: The more they know, the more likely they are to become targets.

**Narrator**: Asuka bit her lip, then finally nodded.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I... I understand.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Thank you for your cooperation. This is for everyone's good.

**Narrator**: Kosuke bowed deeply.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Thank you for understanding.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: Well then... I wish you a safe journey.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: I hope your new lives get off to a good start.

**Li Jie**: Thank you. We'll make the most of this chance.

**Shimizu Kosuke**: ...Take care.

**Li Jie**: Goodbye.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Goodbye, Mr. Shimizu.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka stepped out of the inn. A black sedan was waiting outside.

**Narrator**: The soldier opened the car door for them respectfully.

**Soldier**: I'll be taking you both to New Chitose Airport.

**Soldier**: Miss Suzuki's family is already waiting at the airport.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?!

**Narrator**: Asuka's face broke into a radiant smile.

**Narrator**: The two of them got in, and the sedan slowly pulled away from the inn.

**Narrator**: Through the car window, Li Jie took one last look at the hot-spring inn.

**Narrator**: The week they had spent there was the first truly peaceful time they'd had since returning to Earth.

**Narrator**: It was also the precious time in which they had become sure of each other's hearts.

**Narrator**: The car drove along a Hokkaido highway.

**Narrator**: Outside the window stretched broad fields and distant mountains.

**Narrator**: Autumn sunlight lay over the land, and the scenery was breathtaking.

**Narrator**: Asuka leaned against Li Jie's shoulder, watching the view go by.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie... I'm a little nervous.

**Li Jie**: Hm?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm about to see Mom and Dad...

**Suzuki Asuka**: I don't know what to say to them...

**Narrator**: Li Jie gently took Asuka's hand.

**Li Jie**: Don't overthink it. They'll be overjoyed to see you.

**Li Jie**: You coming home safe is the best gift they could ever ask for.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm...

**Narrator**: Asuka held on tight to his hand, her eyes a little red.

**Narrator**: The car drove on.

**Narrator**: About an hour later, the airport buildings came into view in the distance.

**Narrator**: New Chitose Airport, the largest airport in Hokkaido.

**Narrator**: At the sight of it, Asuka's breathing quickened.

**Suzuki Asuka**: We're here...

**Li Jie**: Yeah. Ready?

**Narrator**: Asuka took a deep breath and nodded firmly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm! I'm ready!

**Narrator**: The car pulled into the airport and stopped in front of the departures hall.

**Narrator**: The soldier got out and opened the door for them.

**Soldier**: Here we are. Have a safe trip, both of you.

**Li Jie**: Thank you for looking after us all this time.

**Narrator**: Carrying their luggage, Li Jie and Asuka walked toward the terminal.

**Narrator**: Asuka's heart was racing, and her palms were damp with sweat.

**Narrator**: She was about to see the family she had been parted from for eighteen years.

**Narrator**: The family she had longed for, day and night.

**Narrator**: She took a deep breath and stepped through the airport doors.
