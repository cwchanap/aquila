# Act 9: The Road to Silver Moon Lake

**Gentle Mother**: (looking at Li Jie) Will you... come with us?

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the mother and her two children in silence. The cuts on her knees were still oozing blood, and the two children clung tightly to her hands, their eyes full of fear and unease.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Follow them to the Canyon of the Dead? An injured mother and two small kids... In a godforsaken place like this, they'd only drag me down.

**Narrator**: He thought of the map he had found in the station. Three routes, clearly marked — the Canyon of the Dead to the north, the Misty Forest to the south, and Silver Moon Lake to the west.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) We met only a few hours ago. When it comes down to it, we're strangers. When I could die at any moment, bringing them along would only put me in danger too.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took a deep breath and made his decision. He pulled the yellowed map out of his pocket and unfolded it to show the mother.

**Li Jie**: (calmly) This is a map I found in the station. Following the tracks will get you to the Canyon of the Dead, but that route is completely exposed. There's no cover at all.

**Gentle Mother**: (looking up, a flicker of hope in her eyes) Then... is there another way?

**Li Jie**: (pointing at the map) There's a main path through the forest that also leads north to the Canyon of the Dead. It should be a little safer than following the tracks — at least the trees will give you some cover.

**Gentle Mother**: (nodding gratefully) Thank you... Thank you so much.

**Li Jie**: (putting the map away) But I won't be coming with you. I have plans of my own.

**Narrator**: The mother was taken aback for a moment, but quickly understood what he meant. She showed no disappointment or reproach; she simply gave a small nod.

**Gentle Mother**: (softly) I understand. Thank you for telling us all this... You've already helped us so much.

**Sensible Big Sister**: (holding her mother's hand, whispering) Mister's not coming with us?

**Gentle Mother**: (gently stroking her daughter's hair) Mister has things of his own to do. We're going to go find Daddy by ourselves, right?

**Sensible Big Sister**: (nodding, looking at Li Jie) Then... be careful, Mister, okay?

**Innocent Little Brother**: (waving too) Bye-bye, Mister.

**Li Jie**: (with a slight nod) You too. Be careful.

**Narrator**: The mother took her children by the hand and set off north into the forest. Her figure looked a little frail in the violet moonlight, but she still held herself straight. The two small figures stayed close at her side.

**Narrator**: Before long, they had vanished into the darkness of the forest. Li Jie was alone on the platform.

**Li Jie**: (murmuring to himself) Good luck.

**Narrator**: He turned and looked west, to where the map marked Silver Moon Lake. The name, at least, sounded less menacing. Better, anyway, than "the Canyon of the Dead" or "the Misty Forest."

**Li Jie**: (thinking) On my own, I can move more freely. No dead weight, no one else's safety to answer for. In a place like this, that's the most rational choice.

**Narrator**: Gripping the rusty sword, he walked down the platform to the western exit. There a narrow path overgrown with weeds began, with faint signs that someone had once walked it.

**Narrator**: Li Jie set off down the path, and the derelict station of the City of the Netherworld slowly fell away behind him. The two violet moons still hung in the sky, stretching his shadow long across the ground.

---

**Narrator**: The air in the forest was cold and damp, smelling of rotting leaves and wet earth. The dense canopy overhead blocked out nearly all the light; only scattered rays of violet moonlight slipped through the gaps, dappling the ground with light and shadow.

**Narrator**: The path twisted and turned, here blocked by fallen trunks, there buried under creeping vines. Li Jie carefully pushed aside the branches in his way and pressed on, one step at a time.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) No one's walked this path in a long time... but at least I can still tell which way it goes.

**Narrator**: The forest was frighteningly silent. No insects, no birdsong; even the wind in the leaves sounded hollow and far away. There was only the rustle of his own footsteps in the fallen leaves.

**Narrator**: After walking for about an hour, Li Jie stopped and leaned against a massive tree trunk to rest. Sweat had beaded on his forehead, and his breathing had grown a little ragged.

**Li Jie**: (panting, under his breath) This forest... goes deeper than I thought.

**Narrator**: He took the map from his pocket and studied it by the faint moonlight. According to the map, Silver Moon Lake wasn't all that far from the station, but actually walking it was much harder than he had expected.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) On the map, it looks like just a short stretch. But with these rough hill trails... it could take hours.

**Narrator**: He put the map away and kept going. Time ticked by, but beneath this endless violet night sky there was no way to tell how long had passed.

**Narrator**: Two hours. Three. Li Jie's legs began to ache, and his throat was tight with thirst. He had lost count of how many slopes he had climbed, how many giant trees he had skirted.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Don't tell me I took a wrong turn... No, the path's still here. As long as I follow it, I should be fine...

**Narrator**: Just as he was beginning to doubt that the path really led to Silver Moon Lake, the trees ahead suddenly thinned out. Through the gaps between the trunks, he saw a more open clearing.

**Narrator**: And in the middle of that clearing stood a wooden house.

**Li Jie**: (stopping short, surprised) Is that... a house?

**Narrator**: It was a single-story cabin, simply built but intact, and it looked remarkably well kept. The plank walls were a little weathered, but showed no sign of rot. The roof was covered in dark tiles, the windows were shut tight, and a small set of wooden steps led up to the door.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) A house in this good a condition, deep in a forest this desolate? That's way too strange...

**Narrator**: Gripping the rusty sword, he approached the cabin warily. He placed every step with care, afraid of waking something that was better left asleep.

**Narrator**: All was quiet around the cabin. No lights, no voices; it looked long abandoned. And yet it was in such good repair that he wondered whether someone might still live there.

**Li Jie**: (thinking) Let's see what's inside first... Maybe I'll find some supplies, or some clue about this place.

**Narrator**: He climbed the wooden steps and took hold of the doorknob. The door wasn't locked. He drew a deep breath and gently pushed it open.

**Narrator**: "Creeeak—"

**Narrator**: The hinges let out a grating screech. The room was pitch dark, except for the faint moonlight spilling through the doorway onto a small patch of floor.

**Narrator**: Li Jie had barely crossed the threshold —

**Bubbly Student**: (screaming) No! Don't kill me! Please! Please don't kill me!

**Narrator**: A young woman's voice burst from a dark corner, full of fear and despair. It was shaking, thick with tears.

**Li Jie**: (freezing, raising the sword) Who's there?!

**Bubbly Student**: (wailing) I didn't do anything! I really didn't do anything! Please let me go! I don't want to die...!

**Narrator**: Li Jie's eyes gradually adjusted to the dark. In the corner of the room, he saw a girl curled into a ball, her arms over her head, her whole body shaking violently.

**Narrator**: Her clothes were caked with mud and leaves, her hair a tangled mess, her face streaked with tears. She looked frightened half out of her wits.

**Li Jie**: (lowering the sword, frowning) Wait, calm down. I'm not some killer, and I'm not going to kill you.

**Bubbly Student**: (her sobs subsiding, raising her head, trembling) Y-you... you're not going to kill me?

**Li Jie**: (sighing) No. I'm just a traveler passing through.

**Narrator**: The girl slowly lifted her head, and by the moonlight from the doorway she finally got a clear look at Li Jie's face. Her eyes went wide.

**Bubbly Student**: (surprised) You... you're... the guy from the train?

**Li Jie**: (recognizing her too) And you're... one of those university students?

**Narrator**: It was true. The terrified girl before him was one of the four young people who had been the first to get off at Jakkai — the bubbly student who had been laughing so happily on the platform.

**Li Jie**: (lowering the sword, his voice softening) Calm down. My name is Li Jie. You don't need to be afraid. I won't hurt you.

**Bubbly Student**: (wiping her tears, her voice still shaking) I... I'm Suzuki Asuka...

**Li Jie**: (nodding) Asuka, what are you doing here? Where are the others?

**Narrator**: At the question, Asuka's face went deathly pale. Her body began to shake violently again, and fresh tears spilled from her eyes.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (choking up) I... I don't know... I don't know where they are...

**Narrator**: She hugged her knees and curled up even tighter. Fear had her in its grip once more.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (trembling) We... after the four of us got off, we saw there were two paths next to the station. One was a wide main road, and the other was this hidden little trail, tucked away behind some bushes...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (going on) Kenta said the main road was boring, and the hidden trail looked way more exciting — we might even find something special...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (smiling bitterly, tears running down her face) Back then we thought... it was like playing an RPG and finding a hidden route... So we followed Kenta onto the trail...

**Li Jie**: (frowning) Just the four of you?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (shaking her head) No... The steady older man saw us leaving the main road, and I think he tried to call us back...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (going on) He shouted after us that the trail looked dangerous, and that we'd be safer on the main road...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (looking down) But Kenta said we were young and had a spirit of adventure, and didn't want some boring road where you can see all the way to the end... So we ignored him and kept going down the hidden trail...

**Li Jie**: And the older man?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (remembering) I think he sighed... and then I heard him say something to the other two like, "These kids are way too reckless"...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (uncertainly) I guess... the three of them must have followed us. Because after we'd gone a ways, every now and then we'd hear sounds coming from behind us...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her voice trembling) But they were pretty far back, so I'm not sure it was them...

**Li Jie**: (nodding) And then?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (crying) It was okay at first. The trail was narrow, but Kenta kept saying this was what a real adventure was like... With the four of us together, we weren't that scared...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her voice trembling) But the farther we went, the thicker the fog got... thicker and thicker, until we could hardly see each other...

**Narrator**: Her voice grew smaller and smaller, as though she were living through that terror all over again.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (tearfully) We'd been walking forever, and everyone was exhausted... Takumi said he could see a more open space up ahead where the trail ended, and we could rest there for a bit...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (suddenly louder, panicked) But we'd only just come out of the trail when Misaki suddenly screamed!

**Li Jie**: (frowning) What happened?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her voice shaking with fear) I... I don't know! All I heard was her scream, and then Kenta yelled "Run!"

**Suzuki Asuka**: (sobbing) Everyone was terrified. I didn't even have time to see what was happening — I just ran for my life with everyone else...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (breaking down) All I remember is Kenta yelling "Run, run!" over and over, and Takumi yelling too, and then I just ran forward as fast as I could...

**Narrator**: She clutched her head and curled up in the corner like a frightened little animal.

**Suzuki Asuka**: (sobbing) I don't even know how long I ran... When I finally stopped, there was nobody around me anymore...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (her voice trembling) I wanted to go back and look for Misaki, but the fog was too thick... I couldn't find the way at all... Everywhere I went looked exactly the same...

**Suzuki Asuka**: (wiping her tears) I wandered around in the fog for hours, totally lost... and then I finally saw this house and hid in here...

**Narrator**: When she had finished, she sagged to the floor, nothing left but the sound of her sobbing. After that horrifying attack and hours lost in the fog, she was on the very edge of breaking.

**Li Jie**: (after a moment's silence) ...So you don't know where your friends are now?

**Suzuki Asuka**: (nodding, choking up) I don't know... I really don't know... Misaki... is she even still alive...

**Narrator**: Li Jie looked at the shaken girl in front of him. Curled up in the corner, she was like a fallen leaf the wind might carry off at any moment.

**Narrator**: And so, in this unfamiliar cabin, two strangers had crossed paths once again by a twist of fate. Outside, violet moonlight slipped in through the cracks, lighting up a room full of unease.
