# Epilogue: A New Life

**Narrator**: Five years later.

**Narrator**: Autumn, 2048.

**Narrator**: People came and went along the streets of Fukuoka.

**Narrator**: Over those five years, the city had become Li Jie and Asuka's true home.

**Narrator**: Seven in the morning. The alarm went off.

**Narrator**: Li Jie reached over to switch it off and opened his eyes.

**Narrator**: Beside him, Asuka was still fast asleep, her face serene.

**Narrator**: Li Jie eased himself out of bed, careful not to wake her.

**Narrator**: He went into the kitchen and started on breakfast.

**Narrator**: It was a habit they had fallen into after they married.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took care of breakfast; Asuka took care of dinner.

**Narrator**: The aroma of coffee drifted through the apartment.

**Narrator**: Woken by the smell, Asuka came out of the bedroom rubbing her eyes.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Morning... Jie.

**Li Jie**: Morning. The coffee's almost ready.

**Narrator**: Asuka came up behind Li Jie and wrapped her arms around him.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Do you have to work late today?

**Li Jie**: I shouldn't. The new project went live yesterday.

**Li Jie**: What about you? Don't you have a big meeting today?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm... at two this afternoon. I'm a little nervous.

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned around and gently stroked her hair.

**Li Jie**: You'll do great. Just like always.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mm!

**Narrator**: They sat down at the table to a simple breakfast.

**Narrator**: Toast, fried eggs, a salad, and piping-hot coffee.

**Narrator**: Sunlight streamed in through the window, filling the room.

**Narrator**: It was the most ordinary of days.

**Narrator**: And the everyday life they cherished most.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Oh, right. We're going back to Zhuhai next month, aren't we?

**Li Jie**: Yeah. I've already booked the tickets.

**Li Jie**: Come with me this time. Mom's been wanting to see you.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'd love to! I've been wanting to see Auntie too.

**Narrator**: Asuka smiled, her eyes full of anticipation.

**Narrator**: At half past eight, they each headed off to work.

**Narrator**: Li Jie worked at a software company in central Fukuoka.

**Narrator**: It was the second job he had found since returning to Earth.

**Narrator**: His first had been at a small startup, where he'd stayed for two years.

**Narrator**: His current company was bigger, and the pay was better.

**Narrator**: It wasn't quite on par with the firm he had worked for in Tokyo...

**Narrator**: But that firm had gone under from mismanagement five years after he disappeared.

**Narrator**: Li Jie had long since stopped feeling any regret over it.

**Narrator**: His job now was stable, and his coworkers were friendly.

**Narrator**: That was enough.

**Narrator**: Asuka, meanwhile, worked at a trading company nearby.

**Narrator**: After returning to Earth, she had chosen to re-enroll at a university in Fukuoka.

**Narrator**: She started again from her second year, making up the credits she had lost.

**Narrator**: Three years later she graduated without a hitch and joined the company where she worked now.

**Narrator**: She was only an ordinary office worker, but she loved what she did.

**Narrator**: Her coworkers all adored this sunny, hardworking young woman.

**Narrator**: No one knew what she had once been through.

**Narrator**: To them, she was just an ordinary twenty-five-year-old office worker.

**Narrator**: Working hard and making her way through life, like everyone else.

**Narrator**: At six in the evening, Li Jie left work right on time.

**Narrator**: He picked up some groceries at the supermarket and went home to the apartment.

**Narrator**: Asuka got home half an hour after him.

**Narrator**: The moment she walked in, she collapsed onto the sofa.

**Suzuki Asuka**: I'm so tired...

**Li Jie**: How did the meeting go?

**Suzuki Asuka**: Great! My boss praised me!

**Narrator**: Asuka's eyes lit up, her exhaustion swept away in an instant.

**Suzuki Asuka**: He said my proposal was really creative!

**Li Jie**: That's wonderful. Let's have your favorite tonight: curry.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Really?! Yay!

**Narrator**: Over dinner, they swapped the funny stories of their day.

**Narrator**: Li Jie told her a joke a coworker had made, and Asuka doubled over laughing.

**Narrator**: Asuka shared the latest office gossip, and Li Jie listened attentively.

**Narrator**: Ordinary conversations like these made up their everyday life.

**Narrator**: No danger. No fear.

**Narrator**: Only a calm, warm happiness.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie, do you remember five years ago?

**Narrator**: Asuka asked out of the blue.

**Li Jie**: Of course I do. Why do you ask, all of a sudden?

**Suzuki Asuka**: I was just thinking... we really made it through, didn't we?

**Suzuki Asuka**: From that terrifying world, all the way back here.

**Suzuki Asuka**: And then... we built a new life.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took Asuka's hand.

**Li Jie**: Yeah. We did it.

**Narrator**: After dinner, they did the dishes together.

**Narrator**: Then they sat on the sofa and watched TV.

**Narrator**: The news covered world affairs and the economy.

**Narrator**: Now and then there was a story about Tokyo or Osaka.

**Narrator**: But to them, those cities felt very far away now.

**Narrator**: They had never once thought about going back to visit.

**Narrator**: Fukuoka was their home.

**Narrator**: Here they had their work, their friends, their life.

**Narrator**: And each other.

**Narrator**: On the weekend, Asuka's parents came to visit.

**Narrator**: Taro and Keiko brought dishes they had cooked themselves.

**Narrator**: Daiki's family of four came too.

**Narrator**: Yui was thirteen now, a lively young girl.

**Narrator**: Yuma was ten, a mischievous little boy.

**Suzuki Yui**: Aunt Asuka, look! My report card!

**Narrator**: Yui pulled out her report card excitedly.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Wow! Straight A's! That's amazing!

**Suzuki Yuma**: Me too! I got first place in P.E.!

**Li Jie**: Great job, Yuma.

**Narrator**: Li Jie ruffled Yuma's hair.

**Narrator**: The whole family gathered around the dinner table.

**Narrator**: Taro raised his glass.

**Suzuki Taro**: To our family.

**Narrator**: Everyone raised their glasses, smiles on every face.

**Narrator**: It was the most ordinary of family dinners.

**Narrator**: But for Asuka, it was a happiness she had once believed she would never have again.

**Narrator**: And for Li Jie, it was a sense of belonging he had found once more.

**Suzuki Keiko**: Asuka, so when are you two going to have children?

**Narrator**: Keiko asked out of nowhere.

**Narrator**: Asuka's face turned red in an instant.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Mom...

**Li Jie**: We're still thinking about it. But... maybe next year.

**Narrator**: Li Jie smiled and took Asuka's hand.

**Narrator**: Asuka looked at Li Jie, her eyes shining with happiness.

**Narrator**: In November, Li Jie and Asuka flew to Zhuhai.

**Narrator**: It was their second visit to his mother that year.

**Narrator**: The plane touched down at Zhuhai Airport.

**Narrator**: Warm southern air washed over them.

**Narrator**: Li Jie's mother, Lin Wanru, was waiting for them at the airport.

**Narrator**: She was sixty-eight now, but still hale and hearty.

**Narrator**: Standing beside her was a man of about her age.

**Narrator**: It was Uncle Zhang, a good friend of Li Jie's late father.

**Narrator**: In all the years his mother had lived in Zhuhai, Uncle Zhang had looked after her.

**Lin Wanru**: Jie! Asuka!

**Narrator**: His mother waved, her face all smiles.

**Li Jie**: Mom!

**Narrator**: Li Jie hurried over and hugged his mother.

**Narrator**: Asuka stepped forward too, and bowed respectfully.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Hello, Auntie.

**Lin Wanru**: Oh, Asuka, there's no need to be so formal. Come here, let me have a look at you.

**Narrator**: Wanru took Asuka's hands and looked her over fondly.

**Lin Wanru**: Prettier every time I see you. Jie is a lucky man.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Thank you, Auntie.

**Narrator**: Uncle Zhang stepped forward too.

**Uncle Zhang**: Jie, it's been a long time.

**Li Jie**: Uncle Zhang, are you keeping well?

**Uncle Zhang**: Fit as a fiddle. Come on, let's go home. Your mom's cooked up a feast for you two.

**Narrator**: For their three days in Zhuhai, Li Jie and Asuka stayed at his mother's home.

**Narrator**: It was a small apartment by the sea.

**Narrator**: From the balcony, you could see the deep blue water.

**Narrator**: His mother's life was simple and peaceful.

**Narrator**: Every morning she went to the market for groceries, and in the afternoons she chatted with the neighbors.

**Narrator**: In the evenings, she watched TV with Uncle Zhang.

**Narrator**: Uncle Zhang's wife had passed away ten years earlier.

**Narrator**: Two lonely old people, keeping each other company through their twilight years.

**Narrator**: Li Jie was deeply grateful to Uncle Zhang for looking after her.

**Narrator**: He knew that without Uncle Zhang, his mother would have been very lonely on her own.

**Narrator**: On the last night, the four of them sat out on the balcony.

**Narrator**: A sea breeze blew softly, and the stars glittered overhead.

**Lin Wanru**: Jie, Mom's so glad to see you doing well.

**Lin Wanru**: If your father were still here, he'd be so happy too.

**Narrator**: Li Jie took his mother's hand.

**Li Jie**: Mom, please take care of yourself.

**Li Jie**: If you need anything, just tell me. Anytime.

**Lin Wanru**: I know. And you take good care of Asuka.

**Lin Wanru**: She's a good girl.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Auntie, I'll take good care of Jie.

**Narrator**: Asuka said it earnestly.

**Narrator**: Wanru smiled, her eyes misting slightly.

**Lin Wanru**: The two of you being together... it makes me so happy.

**Narrator**: Back in Fukuoka, life settled once more into its peaceful rhythm.

**Narrator**: Going to work, coming home, shopping on the weekends, the occasional get-together with friends.

**Narrator**: And so the days went by, one after another.

**Narrator**: The memories of that other world slowly blurred.

**Narrator**: Sometimes, Li Jie would go back there in his dreams.

**Narrator**: He would see the violet moonlight on Silver Moon Lake, the white buildings of the elf village.

**Narrator**: But when he woke, he would simply lie there quietly.

**Narrator**: Feeling Asuka's warmth beside him, listening to her steady breathing.

**Narrator**: And then, he would know.

**Narrator**: All of that was behind them now.

**Narrator**: Shimizu Kosuke got in touch with them now and then.

**Narrator**: To ask about some detail or other of the other world.

**Narrator**: But less and less often.

**Narrator**: By the fourth year, the contact had stopped altogether.

**Narrator**: The mysterious organizations seemed to have given up looking for them, too.

**Narrator**: Or perhaps their ordinary life in Fukuoka had simply made those organizations lose interest.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka never talked about what they had been through.

**Narrator**: To the outside world, their story had always been the same: they had been in hospital being treated, with no memory of it.

**Narrator**: Not even Asuka's parents and brother knew the truth.

**Narrator**: Only the two of them shared that secret.

**Narrator**: But even with each other, they rarely brought it up.

**Narrator**: Because they both understood.

**Narrator**: The past was past.

**Narrator**: Those unsolved mysteries...

**Narrator**: Why did that other world exist?

**Narrator**: Where did the door lead?

**Narrator**: Who was the sleeper on the altar?

**Narrator**: Where had the black-sword man come from?

**Narrator**: Why did time flow so differently?

**Narrator**: Those questions had once kept Li Jie awake at night.

**Narrator**: But now...

**Narrator**: He no longer cared about the answers.

**Narrator**: Neither did Asuka.

**Narrator**: All they wanted was to live their lives well.

**Narrator**: To cherish everything they had now.

**Narrator**: That was enough.

**Narrator**: In December, the first snow of the year fell on Fukuoka.

**Narrator**: Li Jie and Asuka stood on the balcony of their apartment.

**Narrator**: Watching the snowflakes drift slowly down.

**Suzuki Asuka**: It's so beautiful...

**Li Jie**: Yeah.

**Suzuki Asuka**: Jie, do you think... we'll always be this happy?

**Narrator**: Li Jie turned and looked at Asuka tenderly.

**Li Jie**: We will. I promise.

**Narrator**: He drew Asuka into his arms.

**Narrator**: The snowflakes landed on them and quickly melted away.

**Narrator**: But their warmth would never fade.

**Narrator**: Five years ago, they had come back from another world.

**Narrator**: Carrying pain. Carrying loss.

**Narrator**: And carrying uncertainty about the future.

**Narrator**: But now, five years on...

**Narrator**: They had rebuilt their lives.

**Narrator**: Found new jobs, and new friends.

**Narrator**: Made a home together, and found an ordinary kind of happiness.

**Narrator**: That other world, those terrifying memories...

**Narrator**: They had faded into the distant past.

**Narrator**: Like a long, absurd dream.

**Narrator**: But they knew it hadn't been a dream.

**Narrator**: It had really happened.

**Narrator**: And that was exactly why they cherished the present all the more.

**Narrator**: But Li Jie and Asuka's lives go on.

**Narrator**: They will go on working, go on living.

**Narrator**: Perhaps next year, there will be a child.

**Narrator**: Perhaps they'll move to a bigger home.

**Narrator**: Perhaps they'll travel to many more places.

**Narrator**: But whatever comes...

**Narrator**: In this peaceful world.

**Narrator**: Beneath the sun.

**Narrator**: Living, loving, happy.

**Narrator**: Does it still exist?

**Narrator**: Does anyone know the answers to those unsolved mysteries?

**Narrator**: None of that matters anymore.

**Narrator**: To Li Jie and Asuka...

**Narrator**: It is only a part of their past.

**Narrator**: A past they have chosen to let go.

**Narrator**: Because they know...

**Narrator**: Living in the present is what matters most.

**Narrator**: The warm winter sun of Fukuoka shines down on the couple.

**Narrator**: Calm smiles on their faces.

**Narrator**: The story of what came after they returned from another world.
